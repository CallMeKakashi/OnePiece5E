# Session 010 — Sea of Nightmares

## TL;DR

- The two [[Rings of Aegir]] in [[Veyl Corven|Veyl]]'s box pull everyone aboard into **shared nightmares**. The crew's nightmare (a Marine ambush with [[Daniel]] betraying them) is broken by [[B.O.B|Bob]] and [[Baptiste]].
- Awake at midnight, Bob traces the rings' energy. Baptiste **puts both rings on** (they latch and won't come off) so the crew can enter Veyl and [[Serica Corven|Serica]]'s nightmare: the Virellis throne room.
- There the rings' **patron** demands Baptiste find its vessel and revive it. Baptiste refuses; the patron vows to haunt him. Veyl and Serica wake. The [[Spider Nest Pirates|Spider's Nest]] crew are still trapped.

## Actual play outcomes

> Most of this session is a **nightmare**. Only the section headed *Real* happened in the waking world (DM, 2026-10-01).

### Nightmare 1 — Malphas's memory

- A childhood memory: Malphas brings lunch to his father at a lookout tower while his sister cheers him on. His father spars with him and teaches him the Lunarian knack of resisting blunt hits ("remember that feeling, son"). Outposts have been falling. The scene turns to fire and cannons; Malphas runs carrying his sister, and a Marine's spear breaks against him.

### Nightmare 2 — the crew's shared ambush

- An explosion rocks the ship. Over a den den mushi a Marine ship demands surrender; [[Daniel]] says he called them in and traps Baptiste, Malphas and Bob in bars.
- [[Calder Voss|Voss]] arrives with the G-45 armours "in the name of [[Commodore Briggs]]", wanting the two Lunarians alive for Mugen research, and unleashes **Project Wonderland**: a winged armour that, when Roma tears its helmet off, wears Malphas's sister's face with the eyes and mouth stitched shut. Veyl and Serica are missing.
- Two bodies hang from the Marine ship's mast: [[🎶 Red — “The Songbird of the Black Comet”|Red]] and **Bob's mother**. Voss taunts Bob that her last word was "Basilisk". Bob reveals he changed his name and identity so that nothing he did would reach his family.
- The fight: Roma kills Daniel by dropping on him from Baptiste's grip; Ben drowns; Baptiste and Roma kill or throw off the Marines; Bob warps Voss into the sea, then drags him under.
- **The tells**: no smoke from the cannons, no birds or animals, no wind; Bob's mother has no wrinkles, though she should have aged. Bob and Baptiste snap out of it first and pull the others out.
- **Reward**: everyone gains **1 Inspiration**, which can now trigger one type of haki spontaneously (as a reaction for now). Inspiration accumulates.

### Real — midnight aboard ship

- Everyone wakes exhausted (1 level; Bob 2). The Spider's Nest crew and Veyl and Serica are still asleep, sweating and clawing at their necks; nothing wakes them.
- Bob's Detect Magic traces purple energy threads from each sleeper to **Veyl's blue box**, where the two rings are joined end to end like an infinity symbol and glowing purple. They are **Kirro's black ring** and **the ring Malphas and Bob brought from the North Blue** (DM, 2026-10-01).
- After a skill challenge, **Baptiste puts both rings on** (ring finger and pinky). They latch onto him, can't be pulled off, and separate onto the two fingers. A Charisma check lets him sense flashes of the others' nightmares.
- Bob casts Identify (paid for with about 100,000 berries' worth of stolen valuables): the familiar darkness and voices whisper "**connect the strings**". Bob's theory: the rings feed on emotions, objects or memories, here by feeding nightmares; a dreamer wakes only by willingly dying in the dream or by someone breaking the cycle; touching the ring connects you to a shared nightmare.

### Nightmare 3 — Veyl and Serica's throne room

- The crew enter by touching the ring: a bloodied hallway full of dead soldiers, leading to the **Virellis throne room**. Serica lies with one arm torn off. A floating figure covered in purple crystals, hollow-headed with golden strands leaking from its skull and wearing **all five rings**, holds Veyl by the neck. Serica, despairing: "He killed **[[Vireth]]**."
- Bob talks Serica back into fighting; Malphas shocks Veyl out of his despair ("I won't let yours be taken the same as mine"). The figure fights as a Hexblade warlock; Circle of Death kills Bob, Veyl and Serica, and drops Baptiste and Malphas.
- The figure realises it is caught in one of its own conjured nightmares. It pulls Baptiste close: "**I am your patron.** Child, I became your patron as soon as you put those rings on." It wants Baptiste to **find its vessel and revive it** so it can conquer the world, and to find the rest of the rings. Baptiste's deception fails and he refuses; the patron vows to haunt him "every waking moment" until he gives in. "We will have our meeting next time."
- *DM note*: the patron is **Aegir**; the party learns the name later. In the past it possessed **Cecil Cindross**, the usurping youngest son of [[Virellis Kingdom|Virellis]], through a single ring; the nightmare version wore all five.

### Waking

- Veyl and Serica wake, shaken. Serica: "We killed him… the king just had that ring and then he started acting differently. Well, it wasn't the king. It was the king's youngest son that took over." The Spider's Nest crew are still trapped (next session).
- Everyone takes another level of exhaustion (2 total); Bob takes two more (**4 total** — one more death and Bob dies). Bob doesn't remember the patron's conversation.
