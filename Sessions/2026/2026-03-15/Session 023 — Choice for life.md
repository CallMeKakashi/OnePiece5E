# Session 023 — Choice for life

## TL;DR

- [[Commodore Briggs]] calls Voss's den den mushi from Decibella and demands him back. Baptiste refuses, and Lex Imperia's warships chase the crew into a naval battle.
- The ship drops to about 300/600 HP with irreparable damage. A sea king drawn by Linus's bait wrecks the chasing warship and ends the chase.
- Linus's serum work turns Roma human, and Baptiste brands and mutilates Voss. The crew anchors by the nobles' channel to ambush a ship, probably the new Baron Lazarus's.

## Cast

- PCs: [[Baptiste]], [[Roma]], [[Malphas]], [[B.O.B]] (Bob is away for the next few weeks; with Lou, the dog)
- Ship: [[Cade Tigor Cooper|Tigor]], [[Dravos]] (still recovering)
- Allies: [[Linus Marrow]], [[Zim]]
- Marines: [[Commodore Briggs]] (on the den den mushi), [[Calder Voss]] (prisoner), Lex Imperia warship crews

## Where/When

At sea off [[Noosehold]] and [[Lex Imperia]], straight after [[Session 022 — The Missing Piece|Session 22]]: the chase, then the next day anchored by the channel nobles use between islands. Zim's hearing was due that afternoon. A long rest and a short rest pass in the session.

## Actual play outcomes

> What happened comes from the transcript ([[Episode 23 - Choice for life]]) and the DM's answers at the grill on 2026-10-02. The prep below is not canon.

1. **Briggs's call.** A den den mushi rings on the top deck. [[Commodore Briggs]] speaks in an altered voice, says taking the vehicle was a mistake, and demands the crew hand over Voss. His aide reports they have arrived at Decibella, where the new king is waiting. Baptiste refuses, and Briggs vows to collect Voss's corpse. Linus finds a device on the car's wheel and throws it overboard. Linus doubts the choice, and Baptiste says it was never theirs to decide for Decibella. The call tells Briggs that the crew holds Voss and has ties to Riff, so Riff's fate and his "choice" are left for next session.
2. **The naval battle.** Warships from Lex Imperia close in. The ship is at 560/600 HP with one swivel gun. A first volley costs it 32 HP, and a huge cannon hit takes 213, leaving it at about 300 with irreparable damage. The horses panic and a sabre-toothed tiger breaks its cage. Roma's air elemental whirlwinds Marines off the enemy bow. Baptiste jumps aboard the enemy ship and dodges their volleys. Malphas's call lightning explodes the big cannon; Baptiste is caught in the blast, falls into the sea and drowns, and Roma pulls him out. Bob drowns too (his armor is heavy) and is hauled up with Roma and Linus. A cannonball knocks Bob out and he is revived. Tigor handles the helm and Malphas uses gust of wind to gain distance.
3. **The sea king.** Linus's bait draws a sea king that wraps the chasing warship, bites off two of its top-deck cannons and keeps tearing at it. The chase ends, and the second warship is left behind. The sabre-tooth kills the two horses below deck, including Malphas's mount. Tigor calms the tiger with food and animal handling, and it is tied up.
4. **Voss.** Baptiste carves the Blackhand mark into Voss's chest with fire, and Voss says "you are not me" before passing out. Later Voss wakes, and Roma's sheet prank rips at him. Baptiste uses force blows until Voss is mutilated and unconscious, then hangs him from a steel beam in his room. Linus warns him not to keep Voss aboard.
5. **The serums.** Linus works all night. Roma drinks the white serum (033) and turns fully human, losing his Yeti form (not confirmed permanent). Linus says the white serum should revert "mutated monsters" and that the serums were made for mink blood. Baptiste licks a dab of the green serum (11), gets goosebumps and a sense of a bad omen, and keeps the vial. Linus is out of supplies and needs organs from a smaller sea king.
6. **The plan.** Baptiste weighs two ideas: retake the Valehart mansion as a base, or ambush the new Baron Lazarus's ship before he reaches Lex Imperia. [[Zim]] points to the channel nobles use between islands. Malphas flies her up above the clouds to look, and finds it unguarded. Dravos is awake but his powers are out for about a week and he can only teleport within 120 ft, and warships are seastone-guarded. The crew anchors near the channel at a safe distance and waits for a noble ship.
7. **After.** The crew decides not to tell [[Mira the Unbreakable|Mira]] about the captive for now. The next newspaper is due in four or five days. A local repair costs millions the crew does not have.
