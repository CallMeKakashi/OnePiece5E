# Session 022 — The Missing Piece

## TL;DR

- [[Roma]] and [[Linus Marrow]] find [[Zim]] tortured in a seastone cell on [[Calder Voss]]'s warship and break her out.
- [[Baptiste]], [[Malphas]] and [[B.O.B]] beat Voss and his white G-45 armor outside the Valehart mansion. Baptiste spares Voss, who is taken prisoner with his car, serums and papers.
- They drive the car through the mansion and out through the cave dock, and sail away under fire. Linus and Zim join them at sea. The interrogation of Voss is left for next session.

## Cast

- PCs: [[Baptiste]], [[Roma]], [[Malphas]], [[B.O.B]] (with Lou, the dog)
- Ship: [[Cade Tigor Cooper|Tigor]], [[Dravos]] (still recovering)
- Allies: [[Linus Marrow]], [[Zim]]
- Marines: [[Calder Voss]] (prisoner), the white G-45 armor and its faceless pilot, Valehart guards
- Valehart household: [[Cassian Valehart]], the judge's son, fighting with a flute-baton (about 91 HP)

## Where/When

[[Noosehold]], straight after [[Session 021 — House of Justice|Session 21]]: the Valehart mansion, the cells and docks of [[Lex Imperia]]'s warships, and the sea off the island. Zim's hearing was set for the next day.

## Actual play outcomes

> What happened comes from the transcript ([[Episode 22 - The Missing Piece]]), which is mostly dice and Foundry glitches, so this is grilled with the DM on 2026-10-02. The prep below is not canon.

1. **Lou returns.** Lou comes back to the ship scratched, without Baptiste. Tigor reads that the captain is in trouble. Tigor stays to watch the helm, and Bob goes ashore to find out what happened. Bob is arrested in the Valehart mansion after posing as a doctor and is driven off in a Marine wagon, cuffed.
2. **Roma and Linus.** Roma falls into a cell and meets Linus, who has just walked through a wall. Linus tells him that he found [[Dr Nikolai Tesla]]'s lab and research. Saplea, one of Nikolai's subjects, is being kept alive and was sent to Zou in the care of **Pasha**, a former [[Sixfold]] friend. Roma and Linus wait five minutes, then sneak to the warship at the dock.
3. **The Voss fight.** Baptiste and Malphas fight Voss and a new white G-45 armor outside the mansion. The armor fires radiant beams, drops shockwaves and recharges its powers; Voss uses finger-pistol Rokushiki. Baptiste is brought low. Bob is freed from his cuffs and joins. Bob drops to 0 and is revived, and Malphas heals Baptiste with Lay on Hands. Malphas smashes the armor into the ground and Baptiste's punches crack it open, revealing a faceless, non-human pilot. The armor is wrecked.
4. **Voss is spared.** Voss's mask cracks and he looks like an ordinary man. He smiles and says "this isn't the end, I will be back". Baptiste chooses to knock him out instead of killing him.
5. **Zim.** On the warship, Linus finds Zim in a seastone cell. She is tied to a chair with a broken nose, cut wrists and a black eye. Roma punches the seastone door off its frame, exhausting himself. Linus finds the key at the last moment. They steal a small boat, and Linus says he came north because of the device the Marines are building.
6. **The loot.** The crew strips Voss and the armor. They take his den den mushis, a black den den mushi and two radios, and a small box of four serums: white 033, green 11, red 44 and black 99 (eight slots empty). The papers include the escort route for [[Droven Calligos|Droven]] (Commander [[Commander Leon|Leon]] of G-9 and Captain [[Drez Crown, Captain of G-45|Drez Crown]] of G-45) and Mugen sites: Io (south), Europa (west), Ganymede (New World) and Callisto, being rebuilt. Roma keeps the serums.
7. **The escape.** Baptiste drives Voss's car back through the Valehart gates, down the stairs and past [[Cassian Valehart]] in a baby-blue coat, who spikes a wheel with his flute-baton. Bob shoves him off; the car crashes into a wall, Bob casts Aid on himself and Baptiste, and Baptiste patches the car. Tigor lowers the ramp and the car comes aboard. Marine guards fire from the stairs, Malphas cuts some down, and a cannonball takes the ship from 600 to 560 HP before Tigor sails out of the cave.
8. **Meeting at sea.** The ship stops away from the island. Linus and Zim join them in a boat. Linus tells them Droven's ship docks on Noosehold in three days with two other captains.
9. **Downtime.** The crew has a short rest. Roma cooks, and anyone who eats gains a hit die. Baptiste ties Voss up in his room and pours candle wax on him; Voss says Baptiste "belongs to him" and Baptiste says it is training. Roma walks in and Baptiste knocks Voss back out. Linus takes Voss with a surgeon's kit and starts an interrogation that continues next session.
10. **After.** Malphas, shaken after the fight, tells Baptiste that he may stay in the North. Baptiste says he'll back whatever Malphas chooses. Zim, bandaged and with a broken hand, says she's a spy, that she's useless and that the only people she cared for are gone. Baptiste tells her the crew came for her and that she can make use of the life they saved.
