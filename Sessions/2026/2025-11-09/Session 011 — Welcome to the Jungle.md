# Session 011 — Welcome to the Jungle

## TL;DR

- The crew enters the [[Spider Nest Pirates|Spider's Nest]] nightmare (a burning Virellis town square), beats a nightmare-only **Pasha**, and wakes. Roma unlocks his **Sulong form**; the patron ([[Rings of Aegir|Aegir]]) bluffs Baptiste.
- [[Veyl Corven|Veyl]] tells [[Daniel]] that Liz and Sister Elise were **fused with Vireth's power**. Daniel's crew leaves to report to [[Simon The One Armed Tyrant|Simon]].
- Bob's fuel core crashes and Roma revives him. The party reaches **W.G.A.R.** and meets the **Circle of Clowns**, a traveling-circus front revealed as slave traders. A fight starts; Malphas chases the clown captain by speedboat and Veyl rams it before it reaches the Calm Belt.

## Actual play outcomes

### Nightmare — Spider's Nest

- Everyone touches the rings, and Veyl and Serica join. They land in a burning Virellis town square. Daniel, Ben and Chloe are already there; none of it happened that way, because the Spider's Nest were unconscious during the real events.
- A red-eyed, blood-spattered **lion-mink** looking like Sixfold's Pasha attacks. Its roar forces Wisdom saves (frightened). It bites off Daniel's arm and his prosthetic legs fall apart, and Ben dies. Roma, in a **Sulong form** triggered by the moon, kills it. It reminds Roma of someone he can't place (see Open threads).
- The crew wins with no deaths and no extra exhaustion; Daniel realises it is a nightmare, and Roma wakes everyone by hitting him.
- **Nightmare-only Pasha**: the real Pasha is alive and has left Sixfold. Only one Sixfold member died at Virellis.
- **Aegir's bluff**: Baptiste alone stays behind. The patron says he did not use the rings' power and that he will "lose everything" and obey. This was a one-off lie or bluff to control Baptiste; it does not change his nightly saves.

### Real — aftermath

- Roma's **Sulong form** carries over to the waking world. He cannot control it yet, and it only triggers on a full moon. It was unlocked by being near Pasha's nightmare form, and the moon triggered it as it does for minks.
- Veyl and Daniel talk in the crow's nest. Veyl tells him that during the fight at Virellis they had to **fuse Liz and Sister Elise** with Vireth's power to hold an explosion. Liz now has only the barrier fruit and no memory of Elise. Veyl offers a lead to undo the fusion by finding Vireth. Daniel is shocked and refuses to join them: he has to report to **Simon** and explain why they helped destroy the Doctor's island. He will drop the crew at the next island and head back.
- **Bob's crash**: Bob is found cold, with no pulse and a dim blue orb in his chest. Malphas's shock does nothing, so Roma pours four chemical jars from Bob's journal (ammonia, baking soda, acetic acid, methane, ethanol and salt) into his core, and he reboots. He has no memory of the crash. **Rule**: Bob has a fuel core that runs on **salt**; he can crash again without it.

### W.G.A.R.

- The dome island is empty except for a sign. The crew pays **600 berries** (six people, after Roma haggles from 6,000) to **Bore**, a ticket-taker in a bear costume. Daniel and Ben go to the shops; Chloe stays aboard.
- The staff wear animal costumes and say they are "gifted by our God to entertain". They claim their Lord is inside everyone and that "the Lord perished all the devils". Their story: visitors from the World Government once came to make a habitat, but only sent one animal, so the staff took over the animals' roles.
- Baptiste frees one staffer ("Do you want to know more about the world?") who runs off. Roma threatens to cut Bore to find the Lord, and Bob rips off Bore's tail costume.

### Circle of Clowns

- A tent sits in a hole cut in the dome. **Giggles D. Cloud**, a man in a half porcelain mask, introduces himself as captain of the Circle of Clowns, a traveling circus "stranded for about a year". Their front is a circus; they are slave traders (revealed this session). He claims there is a ship on the south side and wants help at night.
- Veyl cuts the drapes and the **Spider's Nest ship sails away** as promised; the clowns are unrelated. Roma starts the fight after a clown throws a bowling pin at Baptiste. The clowns use laughing gas, grease and fire; one has an inflating body that halves damage.
- **Cliffhanger**: the captain flees by speedboat toward the Calm Belt. Malphas chases, and Veyl uses his thunderclap to run on the water at high speed and collides with the boat to stop it. Whether the captain is captured is open.
