# Session 005.5 — Callisto

> **Unrecorded.** There is no transcript. Everything below comes from the DM's after-the-fact account (grill, 2026-09-26) plus the recap at the start of [[Episode 06 - Hallow's End]]. This may have been more than one table session.

## TL;DR

- After the [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Interlude — Red Departs and Reverse Mountain|Reverse Mountain interlude]], both parties and [[Veyl Corven]] and [[Serica Corven]] meet up on [[Callisto]] in the Grand Line.
- They see workers (children among them) labouring day and night in terrible conditions.
- [[Hallow]] sends them to a large building, claiming it holds a clue about **Vireth**, the man [[Sixfold]] is hunting. It is a trap: the [[Spider Nest Pirates]] are on the island and hold a grudge against Veyl and Serica.
- The group is split up. The party fights [[Daniel]] and [[Chloe]], then follows Hallow through the facility to the Core, where he sets his chimeras on them. Veyl and Serica crash in from above with a beaten [[Ben]].

## Where/When

- **Location**: [[Callisto]] (Grand Line). The Mugen Industries facility: factories, the garbage chute and trash compactors, the corridors, and the Core (the "Divine Furnace").
- **In-world date**: TBD. This comes before the Callisto collapse reported in [[1478-12moon [Newspaper] Grand Line Tribune — Morning]].

## Outcomes

- **Reunion**: the two parties come together on Callisto. [[Baptiste]] and [[Roma]] arrive via Reverse Mountain with Veyl and Serica; [[Malphas]] and [[B.O.B]] stow away on a Germa merchant ship to Harpoon Cay.
- **Hallow's lure**: Hallow, a lineman, points them to a large building and claims there is a clue about Vireth there.
- **Spider ambush**: the Spiders attack. Veyl and Serica are cut off and fight Ben.
- **Garbage chute**: the rest of the party falls down a garbage chute and fights Daniel and Chloe. Meanwhile Hallow, in the control room, tries to crush them with the trash compactors.
- **Prisoners**: Chloe and Daniel are beaten and tied up.
- **Pursuit**: the party chases Hallow through the facility, fighting empty Mugen-made armours and mechanical factory arms.
- **Core**: Hallow unleashes chimeras in the Core. Once those are dealt with, Veyl and Serica crash down from above with a defeated Ben. The Episode 6 recap picks up here (Daniel turns on the party to escape with Chloe; Ben lands on the platform).
