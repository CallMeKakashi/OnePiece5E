# Session 013 — The Reaper

## TL;DR

- Sailing toward [[W.G.A.R.]] after the clown fight, the crew is attacked by the [[Shadow Guild]]'s two-person **Scorpion Unit**, [[Kyle Bloodfin]] and [[Esper Grimrose]], who came on the crew's own ship. Kyle is killed and Esper is captured; [[Veyl Corven|Veyl]] stops the party from killing her.
- Next morning Veyl and [[Serica Corven|Serica]] read the news of [[Vireth]]'s attack on a South Blue Marine base, take the Scorpion Unit's ship and Esper, and leave through the Calm Belt.
- At W.G.A.R. the crew stands off with retired Admiral [[Graff Bolt]] and his disciple [[Melina Celeste]], who want the clown and the crab; Graff knocks the crew out with Conqueror's Haki.
- They wake in the worker settlement. Graff puts them to work on repairs, tells [[Baptiste]] the Marine version of the Unity War, then splits off with Melina to follow the slave-trader trail. [[Cade Tigor Cooper|Tigor]] joins as helmsman.

## Actual play outcomes

> The recording has **no DM audio** and the later transcript is looped garbage, so the usable detail ends early. This recap comes from the DM's account at the grill (2026-10-02), with the Marine standoff partly visible in the transcript.

1. **Scorpion Unit attack**: on the way to W.G.A.R. the crew sees the [[Shadow Guild]]'s Scorpion Unit arrive on their ship and start attacking. Open season has been declared on all [[Blackhand]] crew since [[Blackhand Cane]]'s disappearance, and Kyle and Esper are hunting the whole crew. Kyle is a bull shark fishman and grabs [[Roma]], holding his jaw over Roma's neck; someone tackles him and they go into the water. Kyle is killed in the fight (the crew can't say who landed the blow). Esper sees it.
2. **Esper**: captured. She is hell-bent on revenge against Baptiste and Malphas, whose past crew-killing wiped out the **Reaper** unit that her brother belonged to. Veyl takes responsibility for her and stops the party from killing her.
3. **Sixfold leave**: the crew ties Esper up and sleeps. Next morning, while they travel back toward the island, Veyl and Serica read the news of [[Vireth]] ("Vireth Strikes G-5-5", [[Timeline/1478/1478-12moon [Newspaper] Grand Line Tribune — Morning|Tribune 1478-12moon]]) hitting a South Blue Marine base and taking down a group of Vice Admirals. Veyl reads it as a beacon: *I'm here, come to me.* They take the Scorpion Unit's ship, take Esper, and leave through the Calm Belt for the South Blue.
4. **W.G.A.R. standoff**: the crew arrives at the island, which is wrecked. They meet [[Graff Bolt]] and [[Melina Celeste]], who ask them to stand down and hand over the clown and the crab while tracking slave-trader rumours. The party refuses, calling it slavery and demanding verification of any promise; things escalate and Graff knocks out the crew with Conqueror's Haki.
5. **Waking**: the crew wakes in the W.G.A.R. worker settlement and finds Veyl and Serica gone with Esper. Graff is waiting and puts them to work repairing the damage.
6. **What had happened**: Kyle and Esper had hit W.G.A.R. earlier, destroying the island in search of the Lunarfolds, and went away after the [[Spider Nest Pirates|Spider's Nest]] fought them off. The Spiders left before Graff arrived.
7. **Unity War**: Graff tells Baptiste the Marine-side story of the war: three Marines ([[Blackhand Cane|Eulisis]], [[Horus]] and Stella Celeste) and a tragedy that ends with Stella allegedly killed by Blackhand and Horus punished for failing to stop it (see [[Timeline/Undated/[Backstory] Linus — The Motley Crew|Linus's account]]). Melina already knew it and doesn't react.
8. **The old ship**: someone on the island finds the crew's old ship; the crew stays back to deal with it. This is the ship they sail to the next island, where they later lose it (not played yet).
9. **Graff and Melina split** from the party to follow the slave-trader trail to the South Blue.
10. **Tigor**: [[Bugor]] asks the crew to take his younger brother Tigor, who always wanted to sail and is wasting his potential on the island, and who needs animals to restart the reserve. Tigor is a skilled helmsman, which the crew needed, and reluctantly agrees.
11. **The Lord is a bit**: the townsfolk and staff were playing up their characters about a "Lord" to keep visitors entertained; there is no Lord.
