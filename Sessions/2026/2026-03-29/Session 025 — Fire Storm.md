# Session 025 — Fire Storm

## TL;DR

- Roma and Bob recover at the Valehart mansion while a den den mushi repeats a Marine distress call: the Droven transport is under attack by a flying figure. Aegir speaks to Baptiste through the rings. Cassian prepares a ship and Goru, masked, takes Roma and Bob to the crew's ship.
- The crew boards the escort fleet in a storm. Commander Leon, a Flame Logia, nearly wipes out the party, and Alice (Malphas's sister, who burned the Marine ship) ends the fight with a Conqueror's Haki burst that knocks out the whole ship. Linus and Dravos free Droven in the meantime.
- Alice and Malphas reunite, but a drone and a mist controlled by Nikolai (calling himself Anton) take Alice, Malphas and the captive Voss off the ship.

## Cast

- PCs: [[Baptiste]], [[Roma]], [[Malphas]], [[B.O.B]]
- Crew and allies: [[Cade Tigor Cooper|Tigor]] (helm, stays out of the fight), [[Linus Marrow]], [[Dravos]], [[Zim]], [[Calder Voss]] (prisoner), [[🗡️ Goru Yamashita (formerly Goru Valencruz)|Goru]] (masked), [[Matthew -The Jack- Burgess|Jack]], Mikey ([[Morrow]]), Luca the wolf cub
- Hosts: [[Cassian Valehart]], [[Jefferson Talley]]
- Marines: [[Commander Leon]] (G-9), his Petty Officer Marines
- New or returning: [[Alice]], [[Droven Calligos]], [[Dr Nikolai Tesla]] (heard as "Anton"), an unknown wolf-beast
- Voices: the Commodore ([[Commodore Briggs]]) on the den den mushi, [[Aegir (Control)|Aegir]] in the rings

## Where/When

Starts in the [[Cassian Valehart|Valehart]] mansion on [[Lex Imperia]] (about 30 to 40 minutes after the S24 fight), then the crew's ship and the merchant ship that is really a Valehart vessel. The battle is at sea in a storm, on the Marine escort fleet carrying Droven (three warships: one burning and capsizing, one with energy cannons, and the middle one the crew boards). The crew then sails away from the burned fleet. It is the morning after [[Session 024 — The Friendly Baron|Session 24]].

## Actual play outcomes

> What happened comes from the transcript ([[Episode 25 - Fire Storm]]) and the DM's answers at the grill. The prep is not canon.

1. **Waking in the mansion.** Roma wakes with Bob resuscitating him. Jefferson tells them to respect the mahogany floor and says Lord Valehart wants any able-bodied person. Cassian and Goru show Malphas the den den mushi on loop. Roma's clothes are lost in town, so he wears a chef's outfit, and Jefferson tells him to return it intact.
2. **Aegir.** Baptiste hears the voice say "Finally, I have recovered enough". It says he will lose everything he loves and then come back, and wakes on deck with Linus saying the rings are worsening and "we do not want another incident". Aegir is trying to take control. No mechanical cost.
3. **The distress call.** The Marines on the escort ships report a flying figure scorching one warship (now capsizing) and request backup. Linus warns that one Marine captain knows him personally and that this will make it harder to help.
4. **Vials.** Linus has studied them. Green closes wounds, white (used by Roma) reverts the mix transformation, blue is a speed boost, red is frenzy, black is unknown. Baptiste keeps the green, blue, red and black vials.
5. **The Commodore call.** Baptiste dials the Commodore. A distorted voice says retaliation for anything done to Voss will come tenfold. Baptiste says the Marines struck first, he will return Voss, but they will "always be my bitch". The line stays open on static and no harm comes to Voss.
6. **Mobilising.** Linus can extend Dravos's teleport range by a few tens of feet. Cassian orders Jefferson to prepare a ship for Bob and Roma. Malphas radios the crew's ship and Tigor answers. Malphas flies ahead and lands on the crew's ship about 30 minutes earlier than Roma and Bob, and Tigor turns the ship toward the fight.
7. **The masked Goru.** Goru boards the Valehart ship in a tengu mask to cover himself, with Jack, Mikey and a small group, and meets Roma and Bob. He asks the crew not to kill Commander Leon and says the other Marine is better "got rid of" for the sorrow he caused Goru's people. No evidence, no name. Roma and Bob cook a rice ball for the voyage.
8. **The pipe.** The crew sails into a heavy storm toward a smoking horizon. Dravos opens a pipe to the middle warship (the limit of his range). Linus goes first to find Droven. Baptiste, Bob (with the saber-tooth), Roma, Jack and Goru follow, and Malphas arrives by air.
9. **The fight.** The deck holds seven or eight Marines who use Haki-style slashes and rifle fire in formation. Malphas is shot at in the air (first shot misses). Malphas calls lightning, then catches a bullet and falls 15 feet. Baptiste throws Marines overboard and shoots two. Bob coats his halberd in dark blood, makes the area darkness (blinding the Marines) and cuts one down. Roma goes hybrid and rages. Goru beheads one Marine and shoots another. Roma smashes the head of a Marine who tells his captain to flee. Malphas tells some Marines to get off the sinking ship and they swim away. Jack fires from range all fight under a table rule that natural 1s hit allies; one such critical goes through Roma's leg (33 damage), and his marked shots later hit Alice.
10. **Commander Leon.** Leon stands on the mast and offers a peaceful departure under G-9 orders. He then fights with air-slash Tempest Kicks and Haki, and shrugs off Baptiste's void form. When pressed, he ignites: his burn scars and right eye burn, the rain stops, and he floats up as a Flame Logia. His flame slashes kill the saber-tooth and drop Roma and Bob. Roma goes to 1 HP before Jack's critical bullet goes through his leg. Baptiste drinks the black vial mid-fight (see Loot) and Bob is healed with the green vial.
11. **Alice.** The figure that burned the Marine ship is Alice, Malphas's sister, who came to help Droven. She and Malphas meet on the sinking ship. Jack's shots hit her twice (6, then 56 damage through the shoulder). She uses her Conqueror's Haki on the whole ship and everyone but Malphas and Tigor falls unconscious, including Leon. Marines drag Leon away and the warship turns.
12. **Droven's rescue (off-session, DM-supplied).** See the block below. Linus and Dravos free Droven in the lower decks and bring him through a pipe to the crew's ship while the others are down.
13. **Aftermath.** About 20 minutes later Linus heals the crew. Droven punches Baptiste to the floor, angry that his brother was put at risk, and tells him to leave him alone. Dravos calms him. Jack refuses treatment and guards Goru, who is resting after the blood under his mask. Alice tells Malphas she never thought to meet one of their kind again and that she has a lead on "that code guy", and warns that "the doctor will experiment on you". She suggests they leave at dawn.
14. **The mist.** A small drone with a rotating eye locks on to Alice and Malphas and a friendly voice calls them "my dear subjects". It teleports around the ship as mist rises. When the mist clears, Alice, Malphas and Voss are gone, and a section of the hull next to Voss's cell is missing. Linus recognises the voice: Nikolai, his old teacher, calling himself Anton, with some control over mist. Droven says it is the same mist that took the old man and his captain. Linus knows where Nikolai's house is, north of East Blue.

### Off-session scene: the rescue of Droven (DM-supplied)

Played outside the recorded episode and posted by the DM.

- Dravos and Linus step out of the white pipe into a burning Marine vessel with rising water. Dravos drills a hole to the lower level. Linus stays and meets a bipedal wolf (black and white fur, steel chains, red-glowing eyes, "RED… ON…") that slams him across the room and knocks him out.
- Dravos finds a bound prisoner and asks if he is Droven. Droven, half out of it, thinks it must be a dream. Dravos crushes the cell door with pipes and they surface in the room where Linus lies with water rising and the beast approaching.
- Dravos charges the beast with pipe volleys and says he is "Ser Dravos the Mole, the great knight of Driftroot". Droven has taken keys from a fallen guard and is free. He stretches a green fist and hurls the wolf into the flood. Its arm reverts to human before it sinks.
- The ship shakes and the deck floods. Dravos makes another pipe and they leave.
- The wolf-beast and its origin are unknown.

# 🎲 Session Outline: _Flames Over Valehart Waters_

## 🎬 ACT 1 — The Aftermath (Roma / Malphas POV)

### **Opening Scene: “Water, Mud, and Mahogany”**

**Goal:** Ground players, reorient after chaos, introduce urgency + tone contrast

- **Roma wakes up** choking as **Bob spits water on him**
- Describe:
    - Soaked floors
    - Mud + sand dragged inside
    - Heavy breathing, exhaustion
- **Malphas** is in the corner recovering

👉 **Knock at the door**

### **Enter Jefferson (Butler of House Valehart)**

- Calm, controlled, slightly annoyed
- Key line tone:
    
    > “That flooring is _mahogany_. Do try to respect it.”
    
- He:
    - Visibly judges the mess
    - Ignores chaos emotionally (contrast humor + tension)
    - Requests:
        
        > “Any able-bodied individuals, follow me. Lord Valehart requires you.”
        

---

### **Transition → Lord Valehart’s Chamber**

**Purpose:** Deliver plot hook + looming threat

- Before full exposition…

---

## 🎭 ACT 2 — Perspective Shift (Ring Bearer POV)

### **Scene: Inner Mind / Aegir Dialogue**

**Goal:** Build dread + foreshadow corruption

- Player hears **Aegir (entity in the rings)**
- Tone:
    - Calm, predatory, inevitable
- Key beats:
    - “I have recovered…”
    - “The pain is only just beginning.”
    - “You will have everything you desire.”

👉 Let player respond freely — this is a _roleplay goldmine_

---

### **Wake-Up Scene (Linus)**

- Linus is **concerned, maybe frustrated**
- Observes rings worsening

**Key lines:**

- “We don’t want another incident.”
- “Why are you back on the ship?”

👉 This implies:

- Past loss of control
- Stakes tied to the rings

---

## 🔁 ACT 3 — Return to Valehart POV

### **Intel Drop (Malphas + Marines Call)**

**Goal:** Introduce main threat: Lunarian woman

- Communication from marine forces:
    - Escort warship destroyed
    - “A flying figure”
    - Fire-based destruction
- Description:
    - Ships scorched
    - Survivors panicking
    - Urgent request for reinforcements

👉 Let players ask questions here

## 🚢 ACT 5 — The Battlefield

### **Arrival Scene (Cinematic)**

**Describe this slowly and vividly:**

- One warship:
    - **Half-sunken**
    - Blackened, smoking
- Another:
    - Still fighting
    - Firing volleys into the sky
- In the distance:
    - A **figure flying rapidly**

### **Important Constraint (Player Control)**

If players try to skip ahead:

- The **front warship fires on them**
- Cannons force engagement

👉 Make it clear:

- They must:
    - Disable cannons OR
    - Board and fight

    - Cannons firing
