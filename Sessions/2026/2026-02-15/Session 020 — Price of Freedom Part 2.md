# Session 020 — Price of Freedom Part 2

## TL;DR

- [[Joe -Mitchblade- Mitch|Joe Mitch]]'s [[Sharkfin Pirates]] arrive at [[Freefield]], kill Mayor [[Rashid al-Saffar]] and loot his house.
- The crew fights the Sharkfins alongside [[Khael Dhamar]]: Mitch and the teleporting fighter die, the pistol user is knocked out, and [[Eric]] is let go.
- The crew recovers the loot bag and uses it to pay for the repairs. [[Khael Dhamar|Khael]]'s Sand Rats take the captives away. No level-up.

[[Freefield]], the plantation and the forest around it, directly after [[Session 020 — Price of Freedom Part 1|Part 1]].

## Actual play outcomes

> What happened comes from the transcript ([[Episode 20 - Price of Freedom Part 2]]), which is mostly dice and rules chatter, so this is grilled with the DM on 2026-10-02. The prep below is not canon.

1. **The Sharkfins arrive.** Once the crew and [[Khael Dhamar]]'s raiders stop fighting, Captain [[Joe -Mitchblade- Mitch|Joe Mitch]] and the Sharkfin Pirates show up, kill [[Rashid al-Saffar]] and take loot from his house.
2. **The fight.** The crew and Khael fight the Sharkfins.
   - [[Baptiste]] uses void arms and follows the teleporting fighter through the void, then finishes him with a punch through the sword.
   - [[Malphas]] lands Divine Smite and paladin bombs.
   - [[B.O.B]] knocks one out with smoke and cuts off the teleporter's escape.
   - Captain Mitch and the teleporter die; the pistol user is knocked out.
3. **The bag.** The loot bag is dropped mid-fight; the crew picks it up. The Sharkfins' crewman [[Eric]], left without a captain, is sent away.
4. **Aftermath.** The crew argues about taking the pirates in for the bounty, but as pirates they cannot claim Mitch's. [[Khael Dhamar|Khael]]'s Sand Rats take the captives to safety; no favour is owed. The bag pays for the repairs. The crew does not level up.
