# Session 020 — Price of Freedom Part 1

## TL;DR

- The crew sails from Decibella under its own flag for the first time and leaves a spare with [[Riff Sin]].
- They put in at [[Freefield]] for ship repairs and supplies. [[Bramble]] quotes the repair price and Mayor [[Rashid al-Saffar]] offers to cover it if the crew protects his chili plantation from pirates and bandits.
- The crew meets [[Khael Dhamar]]'s raiders at the plantation and fights them. Talking to Rashid's captives shows the mayor is a slave trader (he offered them to the crew "to pass the time").

- PCs: [[Baptiste]], [[Roma]], [[B.O.B]], [[Malphas]]
- Freefield: [[Rashid al-Saffar]], [[Bramble]] (the shipwright who quoted the price), a captive woman (unnamed)
- Sand Rats: [[Khael Dhamar]]
- Decibella: [[Riff Sin]]

## Where/When

Leaving [[Decibella Kingdom]], then [[Freefield]] island, directly after [[Session 019 — The Walking Dead|S19]].

## Actual play outcomes

> What happened comes from the transcript ([[Episode 20 - Price of Freedom Part 1]]), which is mostly garbled, so this is grilled with the DM on 2026-10-02. The prep below is not canon.

1. **Departure.** The crew raises its own flag at last and leaves a spare flag with [[Riff Sin]].
2. **Freefield.** They stop for ship repairs and supplies, including materials to reinforce the cage for their pets (the sabre-toothed tiger and the other animals from Decibella).
3. **The noble cover.** The crew passes itself off as nobles of a forgotten Lunar house that helped the Decibella rebellion and is rebuilding.
4. **The offer.** [[Bramble]] quotes the repair price. Mayor [[Rashid al-Saffar]] offers to give the crew the money in exchange for protecting his chili plantation from pirates and bandits.
5. **The truth.** The crew first moves to stop [[Khael Dhamar]]'s raiders. They talk privately with a captive woman and learn Rashid is a slave trader; he had offered captives to the crew "to pass the time". Part 1 ends as the fighting starts.
