# Session 019 — The Walking Dead

## TL;DR

- The Davy Back Fight for [[Dravos]] ends 3-0 for the crew: [[B.O.B]] beats [[Shako]], [[Roma]] beats [[Malak Samum]], the Battle of Beasts goes to the crew's animal, and [[Baptiste]] knocks out [[⚔️ Fenris Wolfblood — “The Blood of Sun and Shadow”|Fenris]] in round 1.
- [[Malphas]] and Baptiste find Dravos in the forest. Baptiste learns his brother is [[Droven Calligos|Droven]], held by the Marines, and Dravos agrees to work with the crew for now.
- Roma tries to take [[Riff Sin]] along in a barrel; Baptiste makes it conditional on finding the surviving twin. Roma and Bob chase her trail to the sea and lose it. The crew leaves at sunrise.

## Actual play outcomes

> What happened comes from the transcript ([[Episode 19 - The Walking Dead]]), grilled with the DM on 2026-10-02. The prep below is not canon.

1. **The Davy Back Fight finishes (crew 3-0).** All four contests below were part of it; no devil fruit powers.
   - **Bob vs Shako.** Bob uses Shield repeatedly, Divine Smite, and the Cursed Bloodstone (a bong, for advantage on initiative). He vomits toxic blood in Shako's face. Shako goes down and Bob wins, then passes out.
   - **Roma vs Malak Samum** (called Malik). Roma rolls a natural 20 on initiative and rages. Malak heals 40 but Roma wins.
   - **Battle of Beasts.** The crew's animal wins the race; Fenris's monkey passes out. Roma accuses the other side of cheating with a banana.
   - **Baptiste vs Fenris.** Baptiste uses Haste, a clone, his astral arm, Stunning Strike and his elemental domain. The first punch that lands knocks Fenris out in round 1.
2. **Aftermath.**
   - Baptiste tells Fenris to get stronger before going after [[Cline The Plague|Cline]]; anything to do with the Sixfold is going to be difficult.
   - Bob talks to [[Facade]], who cannot remember the name of the person who mended him after the blowup. It means nothing to Bob; they part as "brothers from different mothers" and the handshake leaves bad-smelling blood on Bob's hand.
   - Baptiste gives the people of Decibella a farewell speech.
3. **Dravos.** Malphas and Baptiste find him in the forest. Baptiste calls Mira, who gives him free rein, and learns Dravos's brother is [[Droven Calligos|Droven]], captured by the Marines. Dravos is told Droven is alive and the crew means to free him. He agrees to work with the crew for now. The crew plans to leave at sunrise.
4. **Riff.**
   - Baptiste advises [[Riff Sin]] to help the people but let them decide, rather than turn to the World Government, which would be a different oppression.
   - Roma tries to smuggle Riff aboard in an empty wine barrel (a note saying he is "on tour"). Baptiste refuses to approve it unless Roma finds the surviving twin ([[Sephra Anthem]], who got away).
5. **The twin hunt.** Roma and Bob (Malphas does not join) follow her blood trail with Detect Magic. Bob jumps off a cliff and Roma follows. The trail runs out to sea, with days of ocean that way, so they turn back. The rest of the night is spent getting high with the island's panthers.
6. **The helm.** Malphas touches the ship's helm and tells the spirit of the ship they will let it go eventually, but for now it should stay.
