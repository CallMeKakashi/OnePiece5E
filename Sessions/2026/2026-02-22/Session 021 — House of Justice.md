# Session 021 — House of Justice

## TL;DR

- [[Dravos]] opens a Thousand-Mile Tunnel and the crew crashes out of it onto **Noosehold**, the island of judges and gallows, instead of arriving quietly near **Lex Imperia**. The ship is hidden in a cave dock.
- [[Roma]] talks his way into Judge Valehart's household as a chef, learns [[Zim]]'s hearing is tomorrow and [[Droven Calligos|Droven]]'s execution is within weeks, then gets fired by the judge's son. A radio call from [[Calder Voss]] makes clear the Marines expect them.
- In the afternoon [[Baptiste]] burns two Marines in the cathedral, Roma opens every cell in the basement and sets off a prison riot, and the Marines shoot into the crowd.
- Armored Marines fall on the island. The session ends mid-fight: Baptiste against a new white G-45 armor, [[Malphas]] against Voss, and Roma pulled through the floor by [[Linus Marrow]].

## Cast

- PCs: [[Baptiste]], [[Roma]], [[Malphas]] (with Lou, the dog)
- Ship: [[Dravos]] (unconscious after the tunnel), [[Cade Tigor Cooper|Tigor]], [[B.O.B]] (stayed with the ship)
- Noosehold: [[Judge Valehart]], [[Cassian Valehart]], Jean (a kitchen hand), the Valehart head butler and household
- Marines: [[Calder Voss]], a new white G-45 armor, ordinary Marines
- Phasing ally: [[Linus Marrow]]

## Where/When

Morning after leaving [[Freefield]]. [[Noosehold]], the execution island, and the cathedral, the judges' mansion quarter and [[Lex Imperia]] across the bridge. The day before Zim's hearing.

## Actual play outcomes

> What happened comes from the transcript ([[Episode 21 - House of Justice]]) and the DM's answers at the grill (2026-10-02). The prep below is not canon.

1. **The tunnel.** Baptiste wakes from a dream of his body being cut up to feed a sick person and of blood drawn onto himself, ending in hatred and endless suffering. Dravos is pacing nervously; after a failed attempt in the past he tries again and opens a Thousand-Mile Tunnel to carry the ship north. A teleportation mishap drops the ship out of the tunnel in free fall: everyone takes 13 damage, the ship takes 30 (Bramble's reinforcements save it) and Dravos passes out. Tigor puts him to bed.
2. **Twin islands.** The crew sees two islands joined by a bridge. Marine ships crowd the right-hand island and civilian boats the left. They take a long loop around Noosehold, and Tigor finds a cave dock. Tigor, Bob and the animals stay with the ship; Roma cooks breakfast, and the crew's short rest includes his treats (extra d8 HP).
3. **The mansion.** The stairs lead into a judge's mansion. Baptiste is caught sneaking up, taken for a beggar and thrown out. Malphas follows, and Roma walks in as a new cook. Roma takes over the kitchen and wins the head butler over.
4. **Noosehold.** Baptiste and Malphas find a cathedral with Marines building a gallows platform and signposts to Lex Imperia and Noosehold's docks. They work out it is an execution island ("people get hanged here"), and that the judging happens on Lex Imperia.
5. **Wanted posters.** Malphas finds new bounties and tears down the posters: Obsidian 600M, Baptiste 130M, Roma 65M, Malphas 75M, Bob 25,000, plus a new poster for Red in human form at 25M.
6. **What Roma learns.** The household belongs to Judge Valehart, one of the Lex Imperia judges. He refuses a feast until the executions are done. He is the only judge on Zim's case; her hearing is tomorrow, and charges (prison or execution) are undecided. Droven gets no trial at all; the Marines will execute him within a couple of weeks.
7. **The note.** Roma slips a forged note into lunch for the judge's son Cassian, a lawyer at the Lex Imperia high court who holds a public line of petitioners. Cassian sees it is not his father's hand, kicks Roma out of the window, and fires him. Jean, the kitchen hand, says he is scared of Cassian.
8. **Two radio calls with Voss.** Roma's information reaches Baptiste. Baptiste uses the radio, and Voss answers: Zim is alive only because she did not snitch, and Droven and Zim will be executed next week whatever it takes. The crew was expected, Voss has authority only for low-level Marines and is bringing someone else, and he will not pick up again.
9. **The cathedral.** Baptiste's frustration breaks and he punches a man in the cathedral. The Marines surround him. He kneels, then burns two of them to death with fire, punches three more unconscious, and white-steps out to Malphas. The crowd flees and the cathedral is locked down.
10. **The jailbreak.** Roma poses as a guard, finds the basement cells and feeds a guard a treat, knocks him out with a pan to the head and takes his keys, then opens every cell. About 1,900 cells hold roughly 600 real criminals. The prisoners riot, take hostages and kill people in the courtroom. The Marines from Voss's warship fire into the crowd, killing prisoners and civilians alike.
11. **The hunt.** Marines put up Baptiste's poster and everyone else's with a reward. The Marines blockade the islands and G-45 armors patrol. Voss arrives by carriage and a flying armor is sent up. Baptiste calls Voss again, goading him, and Voss drives off toward the judges' quarter, and Baptiste and Malphas give chase across rooftops.
12. **Cliffhanger.** The armor shoots Baptiste and Malphas down with a laser (18 damage), and Malphas falls through a roof. A new white armor (8–9 ft, blue eyes, thicker leg plates) lands in front of Baptiste. Malphas lands on Voss's carriage and is punched off it; Voss steps out and calls him "the navigator". Roma, shot at on the docks, is pulled down through the floor into a cell by Linus Marrow.
