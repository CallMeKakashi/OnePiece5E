# Session 005 — Midnight Chainbreak

## TL;DR

- The crew uses Halward’s stolen keys to slip into the [[World Government Bank]] at [[Spirit Cliff]] while [[Roma]] runs a fountain distraction.
- [[🦾 Saplea D. Isla — “Dreadnaught”|Saplea]] and [[Red]] get inside and [[Red]] secures **Bramble’s chest** from the director’s cabin without opening it.
- The op spirals into a street/rooftop clash: marines converge (casino fallout), [[Serica Corven]] goes hostile, [[Veyl Corven]] is present but conflicted, and [[Linus Marrow]] steps into the mess.
- A shadowy pressure campaign is revealed: debt-collection leverage tied to **deeds**; Linus burns the stash in the bank, detonating everyone’s motives.
- Cliffhanger at ~midnight / full moon: [[Baptiste]] reveals wings and takes off; the chest hits the ground; [[Calder Voss]] and the marines close in.

## Outcomes

- **Bank infiltration succeeded**: Saplea + Red got in using **Halward’s keys** (from [[Session 004 — Masquerade of the Stolen Keys]]).
- **Objective secured (unopened)**: Red recovered **Bramble’s chest** from the director’s cabin; contents were **not** opened on-table.
- **Heat exploded**: an exterior scuffle and comms mishaps snowballed into a multi-party confrontation (marines + Sixfold + crew).
- **Motives surfaced**: the conflict was not “just marines” — a debt/deed leverage scheme was in play, and Sixfold were being directed at the crew.
- **New ally posture**: Linus forcibly intervenes by burning the deed cache, changing what everyone is fighting for.
- **Cliffhanger**: Baptiste reveals wings and flies; chest dropped; marines closing; fight unresolved.
