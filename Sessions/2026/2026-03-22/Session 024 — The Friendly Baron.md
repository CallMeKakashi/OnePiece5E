# Session 024 — The Friendly Baron

## TL;DR

- Baptiste dreams of Aegir's defeat. The crew boards the noble ship at night and meets Goru Yamashita, Baron of Driftroot, who knows Fenris. Goru agrees to land them on Lex Imperia and hide them at his cousin Cassian Valehart's mansion.
- Mira orders Baptiste to kill or release Voss and keep clear of the Sixfold. Roma, now human from the white serum, kills two robbers and two Marines on a night walk, then goes Sulong under the full moon in a Noosehold brothel and slaughters 10 to 20 Marines.
- Malphas lures Roma into the sea to drop the form, and Roma nearly drowns before Malphas and Bob drag him out. Baptiste takes Goru's ship back to the crew's ship.

## Cast

- PCs: [[Baptiste]], [[Roma]], [[Malphas]], [[B.O.B]]
- Ship: [[Cade Tigor Cooper|Tigor]], [[Dravos]], [[Linus Marrow]], [[Zim]], [[Calder Voss]] (prisoner aboard)
- Allies and hosts: [[🗡️ Goru Yamashita (formerly Goru Valencruz)|Goru Yamashita]], [[Cassian Valehart]], [[Jefferson Talley]] (butler), Jack ([[Matthew -The Jack- Burgess|Jack]], Goru's hired escort), Mikey ([[Morrow]], Goru's spiked dog)
- Voice: [[Mira the Unbreakable|Mira]] (den den mushi call)
- Aegir (in Baptiste's dream)

## Where/When

Off the nobles' channel, then aboard the noble ship and the crew's ship at night. After a two-hour sail the crew lands on [[Lex Imperia]] through the back channel and stays at the [[Cassian Valehart|Valehart]] mansion. Roma's walk and the full-moon rampage happen in [[Noosehold]]'s town, black market and red-light district that night. This is the night straight after [[Session 023 — Choice for life|Session 23]].

## Actual play outcomes

> What happened comes from the transcript ([[Episode 24 - The Friendly Baron]]) and the DM's answers at the grill on 2026-10-02. The prep is not canon.

1. **The dream.** Waiting for the noble ship, Baptiste falls asleep and sees Aegir's memory of his defeat: crumbled cities and split mountains, then five figures through the smoke. A man in golden-sapphire armour holds a sword set with a stone of swirling golden dust. Beside him stand a woman with a closed third eye, a white-haired, red-eyed man with black wings, a lion-maned mink, and a fishwoman. The man says "This is the end for you, Aegir." A wave hits the ship and Baptiste wakes. No mechanical cost.
2. **Boarding the noble ship.** The ship has two sails and a black rose with thorns on the top sail. Roma stays on the crew's ship, and Malphas flies Baptiste across at night. They knock out two guards and spot a cylinder with a remote receiver. A rifleman on the mast (Jack) fires warning shots. Baptiste persuades the Baron's people they only want the ship to return to the island.
3. **Goru.** [[🗡️ Goru Yamashita (formerly Goru Valencruz)|Goru Yamashita]], the Baron of Driftroot, knows Fenris and says Blackhand helped him back home. He shows the cylinder (a mist samurai his grandfather built). He is going to oversee a trial, and he has seen Baptiste's poster. He agrees to take the crew to the island, deny it all afterwards, and send word to his cousin Cassian. He comes to the crew's ship by boat with Jack and his spiked dog Mikey.
4. **Jack and Linus.** Jack is a hired gunman of the [[Royal Flush Gang]] (West Blue). Linus once stole money from a raid the gang planned on a World Government bank. Jack recognises him ("you're that kid... it was only a job"), fires, and the bullet passes through Linus and hits Bob's armour. He holsters it because Goru is now allied to the crew. Roma and Bob had been waiting with pizza and a tiger.
5. **Goru and the crew.** Goru finds Dravos aboard and says he will help save him. He learns [[Zim]] is the criminal whose trial he is overseeing, and that the crew holds a Marine. He calls Cassian and tells him everything. Cassian refuses the case, so the legal route is out, but he will harbour the crew. Zim goes back to the crew's ship. Jack comes only if Linus does not.
6. **Arrival.** Two hours later the ship comes through the back channel. [[Cassian Valehart]] meets them alone. He recognises Roma (the man who bought his lunch) and notices nothing wrong with his human form. He says the damage to his mansion was hard to explain to his father, sends his guards away, and offers the crew a week and says he will not tell the Marines. [[Jefferson Talley]], the butler, is the only staff left. The crew sleeps in the upstairs servants' hall. Malphas scouts the channel, which runs under the bridge to the lower docks of Lex Imperia.
7. **Roma's walk.** Roma goes into town alone for supplies and a drink. Two robbers with wooden knives jump him, and Roma kills one with his pan and grapples the other. Two Marines arrive. Roma knocks one out. The other shoots him through the neck for 33 damage (a critical hit). Roma kills that Marine, then the second robber with a javelin. He arranges the bodies so the Marines and robbers appear to have killed each other, and bandages his neck. All four die (the robbers by Roma, and both Marines).
8. **Mira's call.** Cassian leaves the room, and Mira rings Baptiste's den den mushi, furious that someone from the other family told her the crew holds a Marine. She orders him to kill or release Voss, keep clear of the Sixfold, and tells him to talk to Thompson. Baptiste lies that Voss is not with him and lays out the rescue plan (Dravos's teleport at the last checkpoint, with the others as a distraction). She hangs up. Bob heard all of it.
9. **Planning with Cassian.** Cassian sits in on the planning. Prisoners go from the Lex Imperia docks to the lower basement (cleared of prisoners), out of the city, across the bridge checkpoint and to the cathedral, with three warships escorting, two of them flanking the bridge. The party weighs striking at the first checkpoint, intercepting the transport, or using Dravos's teleport. They settle on nothing.
10. **Roma's full moon.** In the Noosehold black market Luca leads Roma to the Lunes brothel, where Roma pays 200,000 Beli for the top courtesan, Angelica. When she opens the curtain the full moon is out, and Roma goes Sulong despite the white serum, breaks through the roof and rampages through the red-light district. The howl carries across the island. He kills civilians in the district (count not given), then reaches the market, where 10 to 20 Marines gather. He kills all of them.
11. **Malphas and the sea.** Malphas zaps Roma to get his attention, and Roma chases him to the beach in a fog Malphas raises. Roma jumps into the water and drops the form, and begins to sink. Malphas ties a rope on him, but is pulled back toward the water, so he flies to the crew for Bob. Bob (out of his armour for the night) and Malphas drag Roma out, unconscious and almost dead. Baptiste takes Goru's ship back to the crew's ship, and the session ends there.
12. **Session 24.5 (not in the transcript).** The scenes in [[Session 024.5 — Interstitial RP]] are canon: Ace (a woman in the Royal Flush Gang) calls Jack, tells him to ally with the Lunarfolds, and shares Baptiste's Gambino/Haruki file and his aliases (Masquerade, Oroboros, Raven). Thompson calls Baptiste for help, and Mira is heard sparring. Linus checks Roma, Bob and Cade talk below deck, Zim asks Roma about Zou, Goru sails away with Cassian following to make sure he is safe, and Linus tells Jack what the stolen time cost him. Linus apologises to Baptiste for hitting him, says he and Dravos will leave the crew at the next island to find his captain, and Dravos says it is a big brother's job to protect his younger brother.
