# Session 012 — Clown Tricks

## TL;DR

- The crew subdues the [[Circle of Clowns]] aboard their ship and fights **Bessie Jr**, the Giant Crab Sea King that carries it. [[B.O.B]] turns the handler, **Bubbles D. Clown**, against the clowns.
- Captain **Giggles D. Cloud** is questioned and killed; every other clown dies in combat.
- A den den mushi reaches a slave-trade contact. [[Baptiste]] impersonates Bubbles and asks for a reward for delivering the ship intact.
- The crew takes a long rest on Bessie Jr's back at sea and heads back to W.G.A.R., where they learn the Spider's Nest has already left.

## Actual play outcomes

> The transcript is badly degraded (looped lines, no DM audio for long stretches); this recap combines it with the DM's account at the grill.

- **Opening**: [[Malphas]] is already aboard the clowns' ship. [[Veyl Corven|Veyl]], who rammed the speedboat, catches up fast with his speed-boosting fruit and is back at Bessie. The crew knocks clowns out as they wake.
- **Bessie Jr**: the ship is carried by a crab Sea King. She reaches up at the crew and grapples one of them; Veyl distracts her while [[Baptiste]] levitates [[Roma]] above the water to hit her. They try to stop the ship without killing the crab.
- **The handler**: Bob feeds treats to the crab, then uses Animal Handling on the handler Bubbles instead ("he doesn't respect you or Bessie"). With promises of freedom, food and good treatment for both, Bubbles turns on the clowns and agrees to take them back toward W.G.A.R. The crew bonds with him and nicknames him **Bessie Senior**.
- **Giggles**: questioned, he keeps yelling at Bubbles, and the crew kills him afterwards. All the other clowns die in combat. No slaves were aboard.
- **The call**: the crew searches the bodies and tries a den den mushi. A slave-trade contact picks up and says to bring the captured slaves to South Blue. Baptiste impersonates Bubbles and asks for a reward for returning the ship intact with the "captured crew". The clowns were already dead.
- **Rest**: the crew takes a long rest on Bessie Jr's back mid-ocean, then rides her back to W.G.A.R. Arriving there they learn Daniel, Ben and Chloe (the Spider's Nest) have already left.
