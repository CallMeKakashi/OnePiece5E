# Session 026 — Daliance of Hearts

## TL;DR

- Linus and Zim split off in the spare boat to gather information so that [[Mira the Unbreakable|Mira]] does not see them with the crew. The rest sail through a waterfall into a cave, where Mira's sea beast Crunch attacks, [[Tray]] and [[Julie]] appear, and Mira lands from the sky and hugs [[Baptiste]].
- [[Thompson Caneheart]] explains the Giuseppe–Capone feud and that he called the crew in. Baptiste meets the Giuseppe family in Yorktown.
- At the training grounds Mira beats the party in a series of fights, then floors all but Baptiste with a Haki test and orders them to train without Devil Fruit powers.

## Cast

- PCs: [[Baptiste]], [[Roma]], [[B.O.B]], [[Matthew -The Jack- Burgess|Jack]]; [[Cade Tigor Cooper|Cade]] (helm) and Lou (Baptiste's dog) also on the island
- Braveheart Pirates: [[Mira the Unbreakable|Mira]], [[Tray]], [[Thompson Caneheart]], [[Julie]] (mascot), Crunch (sea beast)
- Royal Flush Gang: [[King]], [[Queen]], [[Arno Capone]]
- Giuseppe family: [[Rum Guiseppi]] (head), [[Rhum Guiseppi]], [[Gin Guiseppi]], [[Tequila Guiseppi]], [[Vodka Guiseppi]], [[Whisky Guiseppi]], [[Brandy Guiseppi]]
- Elsewhere: [[Linus Marrow]], [[Zim]]

## Where/When

A waterfall cave off the Juniper Islands, then Mira's ship, then Yorktown (the Risky Choice tavern and the training grounds on the outskirts). The Juniper Islands are Westtown (where the crew left from), Yorktown (where they land) and Brooktown to the north. Straight after [[Session 025 — Fire Storm|Session 25]] (the crew has since travelled to the Juniper Islands).

## Actual play outcomes

> What happened comes from the transcript ([[Episode 26 - Daliance of Hearts]]) and the DM's answers at the grill. The prep is not canon.

1. **The split.** Linus and Zim tell Baptiste they should separate. They take the spare boat to the island to gather information, because Mira does not know they are affiliated with the crew (Linus says he is scared of her). Zim and Baptiste say goodbye. Droven and Dravos are not mentioned in the scene.
2. **The waterfall cave.** [[Cade Tigor Cooper|Cade]] steers the ship through a waterfall, with the mast closed. In the cave an armadillo-porcupine sea beast circles the ship and lunges at Baptiste. Roma hears a high-pitched whistle. The beast opens its mouth and a green slime blob crawls to Baptiste: it is Tray, his old friend. Julie, a girl about 4'3" tall who calls herself the mascot of the Braveheart Pirates, jumps out of the beast's tongue, asks for a present and then food for the beast, Crunch. Roma promises to cook.
3. **Mira arrives.** A wind pulls the crew toward the cave's end. Mira flies in fist first and Baptiste catches her blow in void form. She then lifts him up and hugs him, glad to see "one of us" again after months without word. Trey leads the way to her ship, and Julie rides Crunch to guide them.
4. **Jack and the Royal Flush Gang.** On Mira's ship [[King]] and [[Queen]] greet Jack. Queen says she asked Mira to take Jack in, and King warns it will be rough. In a side huddle Jack tells Queen that [[Linus Marrow|Linus]] drank all the money away and is somewhere on the island. Queen says they have no time to chase him and do not want the Giuseppes' eyes on them. [[Arno Capone|Arno]] asks Jack "You called for backup? When?" and dislikes the new arrivals. King and Queen leave by sub.
5. **Roma and Julie.** Roma finds a toy kaiju among Julie's toys, and she lets him have it after patting his head. The pat forces a Wisdom save, which Roma fails: he blacks out for a second and feels compelled to obey her. Tray pulls her hand away, and Julie kicks him across the room. Thompson warns Roma and Bob not to let their guard down around her.
6. **The briefing.** In the captain's quarters, Mira guesses Thompson called the crew and he nods. Thompson explains: the Giuseppes accused the Capones of peddling drugs in their territory, and Blackhand sent Mira to negotiate. Three or four days ago someone made an attempt on Lorenzo Capone, who is in a temporary coma, and the Capones blame the Giuseppes. King and Queen belong to the Royal Flush Gang, a smaller Capone gang. Mira owes King a favor, so she agrees to train Jack. A map shows the Juniper Islands: Westtown, Yorktown and Brooktown.
7. **Thompson and Jack.** Thompson tells Jack he does not trust the Royal Flush Gang and that if they harm the crew no one will save him. Jack answers that he has favors to cash in too and will keep his deals.
8. **Money and Bramble.** Mira asks Baptiste how much cash the crew has and Baptiste says under a million berries. Mira has been out about three months feeding Crunch, and her own funds are low. Baptiste asks Mira about Bramble, the shipwright: she says he scammed her out of millions of berries and she doesn't know where he is.
9. **Yorktown and the Giuseppes.** Mira takes Baptiste and Jack to the Risky Choice tavern. The old woman greets them, then they meet the family upstairs: [[Rum Guiseppi|Rum]] (head), twin [[Rhum Guiseppi|Rhum]] (leaves ink marks), [[Brandy Guiseppi|Brandy]], [[Tequila Guiseppi|Tequila]] (raincoat), [[Gin Guiseppi|Gin]] (youngest), [[Vodka Guiseppi|Vodka]] and [[Whisky Guiseppi|Whiskey]]. Baptiste had bumped into Gin outside and Mira told Gin to leave it alone. Baptiste asks Rum about Bramble: Rum has been dodging collection calls from him too and tells Tequila to look for him. Vodka will assist the training. Whiskey blurts that Gin is busy with his own little crew of pirates, and Gin angrily says he told her that in confidence.
10. **Training grounds.** Mira explains the next days, weeks or months are drills until each shows a form of Haki. Tray, who ate a fruit that instantly heals any injury, will heal anyone she beats. Mira beats Baptiste (unconscious by the second round), Bob (held out longest, 1 HP after a throw), Roma (stunned and floored) and Jack and Cade together (Jack landed the first hits on her, Cade scratched her with vampire claws).
11. **Haki test.** Mira asks if they know Haki, then makes everyone roll Wisdom. Everyone, including Lou, Vodka and Tray, passes out; only Baptiste stays standing. Mira says he may have potential in Conqueror's and tells everyone to train with raw strength only, no Devil Fruit powers. She asks who has felt unnatural strength; she thinks observation suits Jack, and sees Roma needing both observation and armament.
12. **Mira's lost contacts.** Her spy unit has disappeared and other pirates are not responding. She sent Akira to North Blue and has not heard back. She hopes to hear from Droven; if he finds Goru, that helps them, otherwise they will have to go to him.
