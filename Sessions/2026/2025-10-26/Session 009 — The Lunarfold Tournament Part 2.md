- The ranking tournament ends: [[Roma]] beats [[B.O.B|Bob]], [[Malphas]] beats [[Veyl Corven|Veyl]], and captain [[Baptiste]] beats Malphas in two rounds. Final order: **Baptiste · Malphas · Veyl · Serica · Roma · Bob**.

## Actual play outcomes

### Tournament, day 2

- **Morning**: Baptiste checks in on Serica ("because I went easy on you… I don't care about these stupid rankings anyway") and asks Veyl for tips. Bob hides in a cupboard; Veyl finds him and tells him he can't avoid fighting forever. Veyl has heard that "the old man", **[[Blackhand Cane]]**, is already missing.
- **Bout 4 — Bob vs Roma (last place) → Roma.** Before the bell Bob offers a deal: if he puts up a fair fight, Roma lets him run tests on his devil fruit. Bob reveals he was an **interrogator in Blackhand's crew**: not the good cop or the bad cop, but the one behind the suspect reading their thoughts. Roma ties himself to the mast against levitation; [[Daniel]] shoots the rope apart. Bob's Mirror Image, Portent and two levitation attempts don't stop Roma, who knocks him out with a bent skillet.
- **Bout 5 — Malphas vs Veyl (1st/2nd) → Malphas.** Bets: Roma puts 5 berries on Veyl; Daniel puts 20,000 on "the birdman". Veyl uses Quick Blade, Action Surge, thumb darts and a sky-walk to 60 ft, and drops Malphas to 4 HP. Malphas wins by wing-slamming him through the deck. Veyl: "you improved really well, fast."
- **Puzzle box**: Bob's progress reaches about 9%.

### Zim

- Baptiste's look-out roll (a natural 20) spots a woman in black clothes on a log; Bob's owl revives her with Cure Wounds. She is **[[Zim]]**, an informant who "mostly deals with Marines" but has no allegiance to them, trying to get home to **Loguetown** in the East Blue. She reacts sharply to Vireth's name and says she has been following him for about two weeks on a Marine's request. Veyl recognises her and asks if she knows **Astor**; she does.
- She is locked in the brig with Daniel's key. Her bag has a **sea-prism latch**, so the devil fruit users can't steal from it, and her prized notebook turns out to be **blank**.
- **The trade**: Baptiste gives her [[Hallow]]'s journal, the island blueprints and the shipment ledgers (some leading to the South Blue). In return:
  - Vireth was last seen by a Marine outpost in the North Blue, flying towards the **South Blue**. The Marines wanted to know whether another Sixfold was with him; he was with **[[Liz]]**.
  - She confirms Baptiste works for Blackhand himself. She also knows of "the Baptiste who was there about eight years ago" and asks what he really is.
  - **Lunarian sightings**: two in all. One is the one Baptiste would know of; the other was a Lunarian escaping from a North Blue Marine post a few months ago.
- Baptiste angrily pulls her into the cell wall with a black hole. That night [[Serica Corven|Serica]] bangs on the bars, accusing Zim over something to do with "him"; Zim ignores her. Roma smashes down through the floor and knocks Zim out when she tries to sea-prism cuff him, then dangles her over the sea on a rope. Veyl sky-walks down, cuts her free, and tells the crew "we don't torture people". He falls out with Baptiste for not keeping Roma in check: "maybe I was wrong about you."
- Bob's Detect Magic and Investigation confirm the notebook really is blank. Her bag holds a name and an address in Loguetown and a second set of sea-prism cuffs.

### The reveal (next morning)

- Bob casts Detect Thoughts while Baptiste questions her. Veyl loses patience and pins her by the neck; Serica calms him ("we've tried everything… it never works").
- What Zim gives up:
  - She believes **Astor** is effectively dead, but there is a way to save him: a **device, held at a Marine base, that permanently removes a devil fruit's power**. Her Marine correspondent in Loguetown may tell her where it is.
  - **Vireth is not one person.** [[Ronan]] found a fusion fruit in the North Blue about four years ago and used it to fuse with Astor. After an explosion they couldn't separate, and when [[Aralia]] died "it broke something in them". Vireth has mixed memories and doesn't know he is two people; the Sixfold play along. [[Linus Marrow|Linus]] has worked on this for years.
- Veyl explains [[Liz]]'s Paper-Paper powers: the Sixfold ID cards let Liz track and briefly talk to each member, and anything folded from her paper works like the real thing. The Sixfold always work in pairs ("Lin" isn't really a member). Liz is with Vireth. Vireth told Veyl and Serica to stay at [[Spirit Cliff]]; they left anyway to find him, gather the crew again, and clean up what has resurfaced (the Spiders, Callisto). There are "two more locations". Veyl's spare Liz pages are on the lost ship.
- Zim's last word on Vireth: the Marines only saw two Sixfold on his ship, so he hasn't found **[[Cline The Plague|Cline]]** yet. She guesses he is heading south after Cline.
- Before she leaves, Malphas asks about the Lunarian. When Droven Calligos attacked a North Blue Marine post, someone saw **another flying devil fruit user with white hair and red eyes**. Droven has since been captured. Malphas: "someone of importance to me."
- **Departure**: Veyl gives Zim his radio (the crew keep the other end). Bob quietly keeps one of her two sea-prism cuffs. Daniel lets her take one of his lifeboats; in return Baptiste agrees to owe Daniel a job, as long as it isn't against the Sixfold or Blackhand. Zim folds pages from her notebook (the one Astor gave her) into origami jet engines and boosts away towards the East Blue.

### Captain's bout (that night)

- **Malphas vs Baptiste → Baptiste**, in two rounds: three blows (one crit) knock Malphas out of bird form, then Arms of the Astral Self finishes him. "You guys still have much to learn."
