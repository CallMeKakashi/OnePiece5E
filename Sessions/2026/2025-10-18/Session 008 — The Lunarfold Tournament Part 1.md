# Session 8 — The Lunarfold Tournament Part 1

## TL;DR

- Three days of downtime at sea after [[Session 005.5 — Callisto|Callisto]] and [[Session 00006 — Hallow's End|Hallow's End]]: the crew recovers, trains, and talks about what they want next.
- Acting captain [[Baptiste]] declares the [[Lunarfolds]] officially a crew, with [[Veyl Corven]] and [[Serica Corven]] counted in, and calls a **ranking tournament** for everyone below the captain. The only rule is no killing.
- [[Veyl Corven|Veyl]] beats [[B.O.B|Bob]], [[Serica Corven|Serica]] beats [[Roma]] on the captain's ruling, and [[Malphas]] beats Serica. The finals carry into [[Session 00009 — The Lunarfold Tournament Part 2|Part 2]].

## Actual play outcomes

### Downtime (three days)

- **Baptiste** trains his speed and dexterity with Veyl, and goes round the crew asking about their long-term goals. His own goal: a place where his people, who have long been hunted, can settle, and to restore their reputation. "A long and perilous road."
- **Malphas** wants to find his sister ([[Alice]]), then free the others who were captured with them. Day 1 he meditates to build his energy; day 2 he trains with Veyl; day 3 he flies above the ship, practising discharging lightning into his legs for speed.
- **Roma** cooks nonstop: curing, smoking and dehydrating meat, "in three places at once". He brings bowls to the Spider's Nest prisoners, then collapses at breakfast. Bob wakes him with a bucket of ice water.
- **Serica** spends all three days scrubbing the deck.
- **Bob** shuts himself in a storeroom, studying the crew's fighting styles to improve his survivability. His mission is to find out what happened to the missing **Second Fleet commander**, Goro ([[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Interlude — Queen Anne's Revenge and Frosthaven]]). He realises almost everyone aboard has a devil fruit and questions Roma and Baptiste. Roma admits he ate his while taste-testing it, and that something happens when he gets angry. Baptiste says "not that I remember".

### Ranking tournament

- **Rules**: no killing; otherwise anything goes, including pushing a fighter back into the ring. Bob asks to referee; Baptiste refuses (the referee must be able to stop a fight) and referees himself.
- **Bout 1 — Bob vs Veyl → Veyl.** Bob levitates Veyl 20 ft with a drone, then runs. Veyl reaches him behind the mast with a **Rokushiki** technique and knocks him out. Bob recognises the term (History 22) and makes Veyl promise to tell him everything about it.
- **Bout 2 — Roma vs Serica → Serica.** Roma rages and goes "yeti time"; Serica crits him to 6 HP. Roma is still standing, but Serica has her cannon ready, and Baptiste awards her the bout.
- **Bye roll-off** among Malphas, Veyl and Serica; after a short rest, Malphas fights Serica.
- **Bout 3 — Malphas vs Serica → Malphas.** Malphas catches her bullet with Deflect Missiles, absorbs the explosion's fire, and kites her from 35 ft up, where she can't reach him.
- **Standings at the end of Part 1**: Serica is guaranteed 3rd. Malphas vs Veyl decides 1st and 2nd; Bob vs Roma decides last.
- During the short rest Bob takes **[[🦾 Saplea D. Isla — “Dreadnaught”|Saplea]]'s puzzle box**. Solving it will take at least 100 days.
