# Session 006 — Hallow's End

## TL;DR

- This picks up in the Core on [[Callisto]], straight after the unrecorded [[Session 005.5 — Callisto]]. [[Roma]] keeps [[Ben]] down while [[Daniel]] agrees to help destroy the facility.
- Veyl's ring box flares. The party escapes to [[Hallow]]'s old workshop, reads his journal (which names "the Doctor", [[Dr Nikolai Tesla]]), loots the place and takes a long rest.
- In the morning they face Hallow in the graveyard. His amalgam [[RyeNera]] kills him, and Ben lands the killing blow on the amalgam. Veyl and Serica evacuate the workers, most of them children.
- Their ship has sailed off by itself. They get passage north on the Spiders' ship, name the crew the **[[Lunarfolds]]**, reach level 4, and sink Callisto with Hallow's red button.

## Actual play outcomes

- **Deal with Daniel (00:03–00:10)**: Roma skillets Ben unconscious again. Daniel agrees to help destroy the facility in exchange for freedom for himself, Ben and Chloe.
- **Interrogation (00:10–00:15)**: Serica and Veyl question Daniel. The original spiders are "six feet under", and **Silas**, the old Tyrant, was killed by "our boss" ([[Simon The One Armed Tyrant|Simon]]).
- **Ring flare (00:15–00:18)**: Veyl opens the small blue ring box to prove Sixfold didn't cause the earlier island's destruction. It flares white, the pooled blood evaporates, a cog opens, and the platform starts heating up. Nobody is teleported.
- **Escape (00:18–00:23)**: the Spiders are untied. Veyl's Speed Fruit carries Roma and Malphas to the workshop door, and B.O.B. fails his CON save and throws up. Serica loses the race and must mop the deck for a week.
- **Hallow's workshop (00:23–00:57)**:
  - Baptiste reads Hallow's journal. Hallow came to the island hoping to change things for the better with "the Doctor". The Doctor built the core; Hallow tried to stop it but stayed a cog in the system. The same thing may be happening in other places. Hallow cared for the Spider's Nest kids.
  - The Doctor gave Daniel his legs. Daniel must now report this to [REDACTED], and vows to destroy the core.
  - Malphas asks about his sister and gets pointed to **G-45**.
  - The bank journal is probably on their ship.
  - Long rest.
- **Graveyard (00:58–02:24)**:
  - A siren goes off at dawn. Hallow waits in the graveyard with a remote and says he always meant to destroy the place. He drops RyeNera out of a tree to attack them.
  - Veyl leaves with Serica and Chloe to evacuate the civilians.
  - Baptiste's black hole pulls Hallow prone. With his last words Hallow tells them to destroy the machine, then **RyeNera crushes his head** (01:25).
  - RyeNera heals and raises zombies from the graves. Roma grabs the remote (02:02) and cuts off a healing tendril, and **Ben kills the amalgam** (02:23).
- **Aftermath (02:26–02:52)**:
  - Veyl got the workers, many of them children, off the island on merchant ships.
  - The Lunarfolds' ship has left by itself: the anchor rose and nobody was aboard (02:29). The DM confirms it has a **Klabautermann** that went looking for its old crew.
  - They search Hallow's body and his office. Daniel and Ben burn the bodies.
  - The other buildings are sealed and need ID cards, and breaking the doors fails.
- **Passage (02:52–03:02)**: at the other dock, Roma persuades Daniel to take them north to the next island. The crew is named the **Lunarfolds** (part of [[Blackhand]]), and Veyl says that "for the time being" Sixfold are part of the crew.
- **Level 4 (03:02–03:35)**: Roma takes Berserker. B.O.B. takes Fey Touched (Misty Step, Silvery Barbs).
- **Island destroyed (03:36)**: once they're safely at sea, Roma presses the red button. The core collapses inward and Callisto sinks completely.
- **Post-session OOC**: they plan the crew ranking bracket, which is played in [[Session 008 — The Lunarfold Tournament Part 1]]. From about 03:38 to 03:55 the audio is unusable.
