# Session Flow

## 1. Hallway Finale

- Party finishes fighting the remaining Mafia thugs in the narrow hallway.
- During the fight, one player notices a medkit fall from the hands of a thug at the back.
- After combat, the large door at the end of the hallway opens automatically.

---

## 2. The Main Hall

- Players enter a brightly lit central hall.
- Large staircase leading upward.
- Suspended walkways surrounding the chamber.
- Zara is waiting in the center.
- The room is filled with dead Mafia bodies.
- Blood is visibly being absorbed into the floor.
- If players investigate/History succeeds:
    - They recognize this is the same phenomenon they saw in the previous room.

### Simon Introduction

- Simon greets the crew through Den Den Mushi speakers.
- Welcomes them to the facility.
- Introduces Zara.
- Mentions he has a story he wants them to hear.
- Tells Zara to give them a tour.
- Zara immediately leaps away.

---

## 3. The Trap

- Doors open.
- Players begin following Zara.
- Doors suddenly slam shut.
- Simon laughs.
- "Ah... did I forget? Guests have to earn the tour. Good luck."

### Zombie Encounter

- All dead Mafia bodies begin rising.
- Horde battle begins.

After combat:

- Players can freely investigate the room.
- Search crates for:
    - Healing
    - Food
    - Water
    - Replacement armor
    - Miscellaneous supplies

Only once they're ready does the exit open.

---

## 4. Elevator

- Long elevator descent.
- Simon begins telling Part 1 of his story.
- Story deliberately cuts off as the elevator reaches the bottom.

---

## 5. Lunar Test Chamber

- Massive chamber.
- Downward bridge leading to Zara waiting below.

Simon explains:

- This room tested Doctor's Lunarian Serum experiments.
- "Why don't you test them for us?"

Containment cells begin breaking open.

### Encounter

- Failed Lunarian experiments attack.
- Secret:
    - Every creature dies in one successful Haki hit.
    - Players must discover this themselves.

After combat:

- Zara silently walks toward the correct exit.
- If players attempt another route:
    - Simon mocks them over the speakers.
    - Every incorrect path loops or dead ends until they follow.

---

## 6. The Forge

- Huge furnace.
- Molten metal.
- Machinery constantly producing parts.
- Assembly lines feeding into the forge.
- Blueprints scattered around.

If investigated:

- Players discover mechanical design notes.

As players approach:

- Something begins emerging from the molten vat.

Simon continues his story.

Reveals:

- These are copies created from the crew members analyzed in Mistframe.

### Encounter

- Two Roma copies.

---

## 7. Twin Chambers

- Two containment pods.
- One exit.
- Door requires both encounters completed.

Players must split:

- Two teams of two.

Each side fights:

- Bob's copy.

Simon continues telling his story throughout both fights.

When both encounters finish:

- Exit unlocks.

---

## 8. Environmental Reveal

Throughout previous rooms:

If players investigated well:

- They notice thick pipes.
- Filled with dark viscous liquid.
- Running deeper into the facility.

No explanation yet.

---

## 9. Blood Collection Chamber

Players reach the final room.

Immediate reveal:

- The pipes lead here.
- Giant machinery collecting blood.

Above them:

- Windows/openings reveal West Town.

They witness:

- Giuseppe's forces fighting.
- Mira fighting.
- Capone Family battling.
- Blood soaking the streets.

All blood drains downward into the machinery.

---

## 10. Zara's Transformation

- Zara stands beside a large machine.
- Presses a button.
- Climbs inside.
- Horrific screaming.
- Silence.
- Pod opens.
- Zara emerges with newly attached mechanical arms.

Boss battle begins.

During battle:

- Simon finishes telling his story.
- Final command:
    - "Make sure you bring them to me alive."

---

## 11. NPC Behavior Rules

### Simon

- Never acknowledges player questions.
- Completely ignores anything they say.
- Speaks only when he wishes.
- Entire conversation is one-sided.

### Zara

- Cannot speak.
- Communicates only through:
    - Smiles
    - Frowns
    - Glares
    - Shrugs
    - Grunts
    - Body language

---

## 12. Jack Presence Hints (Throughout Facility)

Scatter these naturally during exploration:

- Jack occasionally feels someone watching him.
- A shadow disappears just before he looks.
- Brief sensation of killing intent.
- Faint footsteps behind the party.
- Feeling passes whenever he turns.
- No one else notices.

Never explain it.

---

## 13. Finale

Regardless of whether the party defeats Zara or loses:

Joker arrives.

Without effort:

- Instantly slices apart Zara's mechanical arms.
- Demonstrates overwhelming power.

He greets Jack directly.

Reveals:

- He's been watching Jack throughout the entire facility.

End session.
