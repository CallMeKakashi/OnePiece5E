

This is a solid setup because it naturally gives the party a chance to:

1. Recover and reconnect.
2. Receive major world-state updates.
3. Finish personal character arcs/training.
4. Get exposition from Linus.
5. End on a large-scale offensive operation.

The only thing I'd suggest is tightening the pacing so the session escalates steadily instead of feeling like several disconnected scenes.

# Session Structure

## Act 1 — Three Weeks Later

Open with:

> The smell of medicine.
> 
> The sound of gulls outside.
> 
> The creaking of wooden floorboards.
> 
> Three weeks have passed.

Give a short montage of everyone waking up and realizing how much time has passed.

---

## Act 2 — Individual Catch-Up Scenes

### Roma

Cover:

- What happened during the 3 weeks.
- His final training phase.
- What Thompson told him.
- His thoughts on the Capone invasion.

Goal:  
Give Roma his spotlight before the group reconvenes.

---

### Jack

Cover:

- Conversations with Ace.
- What Ace has learned.
- What Ace's orders are.

This is where you can reinforce:

> "You can help them.
> 
> But no Capone blood.
> 
> Not yet."

That gives Jack freedom while maintaining Ace's larger plans.

---

### Remaining Crew

Quick scenes showing:

- Recovery.
- Final training.
- Realization of how much the island changed while they were unconscious.

---

# Act 3 — State of the Island

Once everyone is together:

Thompson finishes explaining the situation.

## Current Situation

### Capones

- Landed at West Town.
- Conquered it almost immediately.
- Had already prepared for war.

### Royal Flush Gang

- Forced into conflict.
- Cannot back down now.
- King and Queen returned early.
- Ace arrived later.

### Guisseppi Family

- Nearly surrounded.
- Most ports lost.
- Only York Town remains.

### Capone Demand

> Deliver every remaining Guisseppi blood relative.
> 
> We leave the island alone.

This should feel terrifying because it's reasonable enough that ordinary civilians might actually consider accepting it.

---

# Act 4 — Sato Returns

I really like this section.

Reveal:

- Sato successfully infiltrated occupied territory.
- Pretended to be a former Guisseppi slave seeking refuge.
- Dropped the act after hearing about Baptiste.

Rum's reaction is important.

Not forgiveness.

Not trust.

Just necessity.

Something like:

> "I should kill you."
> 
> ...
> 
> "But we're running out of people willing to stand with us."

# Act 5 — Linus' Briefing

This is where you stop before going too deep into the Spider Nest plot.

The purpose here is:

### Answer

- What is the drug?
- How does Nikolai fit in?
- What are the rings?
- Why are Marines involved?
- Who are Spider Nest?

### Establish

Everything happening is connected.

The Ravine.  
The Drug.  
The Rings.  
Nikolai.  
Spider Nest.

One network.

One conspiracy.

Then stop before revealing too much.

Let the players discuss.

# Act 7 — Mission Assignment

This becomes the session's playable content.

## Jack

Permission from Ace.

Objective:

- Support the operation.
- Do not kill Capone family leadership.

This creates tension if Jack gets an opportunity later.

---

## Main Party

Assigned to Vodka Guisseppi.

Mission:

### Infiltrate

Board a Capone vessel stationed between:

- West Town
- York Town

### Intelligence

Discover:

- What supplies are moving.
- What troops are stationed there.
- What role the ship serves.

### Rescue

Whisky's warning:

> "They keep hostages aboard."
> 
> "Insurance."
> 
> "If a town gets any ideas."

Hostages immediately complicate the mission.

### Objective

1. Free hostages.
2. Evacuate them safely.
3. Destroy the vessel.

---

# Strong Session Ending

I'd end with the party approaching the ship.

Not the destruction itself.

Final scene:

Night.

Fog.

Capone warship ahead.

Dim lanterns.

Armed guards.

Hostages somewhere aboard.

Then:

> Vodka lowers his spyglass.
> 
> "That's the one."
> 
> "Once we start..."
> 
> "There's no going back."

Cut session.

That gives you a strong cliffhanger and lets the next session begin immediately with infiltration, stealth, rescue, and eventually the ship destruction mission.
