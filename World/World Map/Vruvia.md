# Vruvia

War-torn former nation; birthplace of [[Mira the Unbreakable]]. Sea/region not yet stated.

- One of the three rebel nations that rejected the World Government in the war the [[Marines]] fought against them — called the **Unity War** in [[[Backstory] Mira — Journey Part 1]] and the Tribune, the **Trinity War** in Linus's account (the other two: [[Virellis Kingdom]] and the Republic of Starris).
- By the war's end it had ceased to exist as a nation. Eulisis ([[Blackhand Cane]]) describes it as once having "lush forests, green fields and quiet bogs"; after the war it is desert and rotting dead.
- [[Blackhand Cane]] and [[Horus]] served there as Marines; Blackhand took the five-year-old Mira from its ruins.
