# Lex Imperia

The judiciary island across the bridge from [[Noosehold]]: the high court, whose basement holds about 1,900 cells, public petition lines and Marine warships. Lawyers such as [[Cassian Valehart]] work here; [[Judge Valehart]] presides over [[Zim]]'s case here.

## Episode 21

- Roma enters in a carriage with the judge's son's lunch, poses as a guard and frees every prisoner, starting a riot ([[Session 021 — House of Justice|Episode 21]]).

## Episode 22

- The warships docked here hold the high-court prisoners; Zim was kept in a seastone cell on Voss's ship ([[Session 022 — The Missing Piece|Episode 22]]).
