# Bramble

Shipwright at [[Spirit Cliff]] ([[Bramble & Brothers]] shipyard). [[Mira the Unbreakable|Mira]] tells [[🦾 Saplea D. Isla — “Dreadnaught”|Saplea]] he may grant a Grand Line–capable vessel after errands — not easily.

## Episode 3

- Offers the crew a Grand Line–capable ship in exchange for stealing a small chest from the [[World Government Bank]] director's cabin. Provides blueprints and has his workers supply disguises (cloaks, masks, hats) ([[Session 003 — Cliffside Gambit|Episode 3]]).

## Episode 20

- Quotes the price of the ship's repairs at [[Freefield]]. [[Rashid al-Saffar]] offers to cover it in return for guarding his plantation; the loot from Rashid's house later pays for the repairs ([[Session 020 — Price of Freedom Part 1|Episode 20]]).
