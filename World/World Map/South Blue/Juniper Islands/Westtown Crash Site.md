# Westtown Crash Site

Village at the edge of [[Westtown]] where an airship crash-landed in the middle of the square. The villagers fled. The crash looks deliberate: the creature cylinders aboard were smashed before impact, there are burned notes and no humans on board, and the last creature broke out of its tube because of the noise. The creatures regenerate fully from normal damage and die only to Armament Haki.

## Episode 27

- [[B.O.B]], [[Roma]] and [[Tray]] fight owl-like stalkers, a pig-bat brute, a burrower and a giant amalgamation. Bob uses Armament Haki on purpose for the first time and the others follow ([[Session 027 — Testing Tempers|Episode 27]]).
