# Juniper Islands

Island group made up of [[Brooktown]], [[Yorktown]] and [[Westtown]]. The Giuseppe family and Mira's Braveheart Pirates are based here, and the Giuseppe–Capone feud plays out across the islands ([[Capone Family]], [[Guiseppi Family]]). The crew arrived by sailing through a waterfall into a cave ([[Session 026 — Daliance of Hearts|Episode 26]]).

## Episode 26

- The crew lands, meets Mira, and trains on the outskirts of Yorktown ([[Session 026 — Daliance of Hearts|Episode 26]]).

## Episode 27

- The party splits to Brooktown (a cave) and Westtown (a crashed airship), with Yorktown as the base ([[Session 027 — Testing Tempers|Episode 27]]).
