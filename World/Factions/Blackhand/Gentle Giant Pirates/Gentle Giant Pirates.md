# Gentle Giant Pirates

The **Gentle Giant Pirates** are a unit of the [[Blackhand]] crew. A small but capable squad, they bring scholarly insight and master craftsmanship to the Blackhand fleet.

## Members

- [[Kalla of Shandia]] — scholar, linguist, and puzzle-solver; Skypiean from the White Sea
- [[Shako]] — mantis shrimp fishman, shipwright from CraggHammer Island
- [[Dravos]] — navigator ("The Mole"); [[Toneru Toneru no Mi]]

- [[⚔️ Fenris Wolfblood — “The Blood of Sun and Shadow”|Fenris]] — captain; leads the unit and hunts Cline D. Davis
- [[Facade]] and [[Malak Samum]] — Facade joined the unit; Malak is on loan from [[Mira the Unbreakable|Mira]]'s crew

## Episode 17

- Mira sends the unit as backup to Decibella. They patch up the Lunarfolds after the tower and brief Baptiste on Droven and Cline D. Davis. They stay with their own mission (Cline) while the Lunarfolds head north ([[Session 017 — Agony of Choas|Episode 17]]).
