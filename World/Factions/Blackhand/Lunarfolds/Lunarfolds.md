# Lunarfolds

The **Lunarfolds** are the player party's crew and a field unit of the [[Blackhand]] pirates. Operating under the broader Blackhand banner, they carry out missions across the seas with a tight-knit roster of specialists.

## Members

- [[Roma]] — cook, wolf mink from Zou; ate an ice devil fruit
- [[Baptiste]] — Lunarian fighter, rescued from a Germa facility by [[Blackhand Cane]]
- [[Cade Tigor Cooper]] — crew member
- [[Malphas]] — Lunarian warrior, Thunderbird Zoan user
- [[Zim]] — spy and informant
- [[B.O.B]] — scholar ("Hex Machina")
- [[Merlin]] · [[Luu]] — crew (Foundry-linked)
- [[Jazmine]] — Roma's air elemental
- [[Pegasus]] — B.O.B's mount
- [[Bessy Jr]] — sea-beast hermit crab (Bessie / Bessy Jr.; crew companion for now)

## History

- **Reverse Mountain**: Baptiste, Roma, [[Veyl Corven]] and [[Serica Corven]] cross into the Grand Line. The collapsed sails fill by themselves after Serica smacks the mast ([[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Interlude — Red Departs and Reverse Mountain]]).
- **Crew named** in [[Episode 06 - Hallow's End]] (~02:59). Baptiste settles on "Lunarfolds", a unit of the [[Blackhand]] pirates. Veyl and Serica ([[Sixfold]]) are part of the crew "for the time being".

## Ships

- **First ship (unnamed)**: it has a **Klabautermann**, which is why the sails filled at Reverse Mountain. On [[Callisto]] it raised its anchor and sailed off with no one aboard, searching for its **old crew** (DM, 2026-09-26). The bank journal was probably aboard. The crew then rode the [[Spider Nest Pirates]]' ship north.
