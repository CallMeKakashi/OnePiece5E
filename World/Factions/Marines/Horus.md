# Horus

## Description

Vice Admiral **Horus "The Maw"** of the [[Marines]] — described as massive and terrifying. Earlier accounts call him **Commodore Horus**.

## Role

Vice Admiral; campaigned against the [[Blackhand]] Pirates ("try to set foot in the Grand Line and I'll devour their ships whole"). Withdrew from that campaign after the death of his mentor, former Admiral [[Graff Bolt]]. With [[Commander Leon]] (whom he trained) he raised the objections that led Marine HQ to order [[Mugen Industries]] to cease operations (Tribune, 22nd Moon 1478, Evening).

## Backstory

- **Unity War** — In [[[Backstory] Mira — Journey Part 1]], a Marine named Horus stands with his comrade Eulisis ([[Blackhand Cane]]) in [[Vruvia]] and warns him that the child Mira "will hate you all her life"; he calls Eulisis a fool for taking her. That this is the same man as Vice Admiral Horus is implied (same name, same era as Blackhand's Marine service) but not stated outright.
- **Driftroot Isle** — As a visiting Commodore he dove into the Old City fire and saved survivors, including [[Commander Leon|Leon]] ([[🗡️ Goru Yamashita (formerly Goru Valencruz)]]).
- **Krake's Maw** — Raided Grinjaw's pirate contest and crushed the future [[Motley Crew]] until [[Veyl Corven|Veyl]] and [[Serica Corven|Serica]] got them out ([[1476-01moon [Backstory] The Birth of the Motley Crew]]; [[[Backstory] Linus — The Motley Crew]]).
- **Campaign 1** — Ambushed and easily defeated the crew at Craw Island ([[Session 3 - The Carnival and the Collapse]]).
