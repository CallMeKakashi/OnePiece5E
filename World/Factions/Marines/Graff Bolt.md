## Episode 13

- Arrives at [[W.G.A.R.]] with [[Melina Celeste]] while tracking slave-trader rumours, and asks the crew to hand over the clown ([[Bubbles D. Clown]]) and the crab ([[Bessy Jr|Bessie Jr]]). When the party refuses and it escalates, he knocks the crew out with Conqueror's Haki. When they wake in the worker settlement he puts them to work repairing the island, which [[Kyle Bloodfin]] and [[Esper Grimrose]] had wrecked, and tells [[Baptiste]] the Marine-side story of the Unity War (Blackhand, [[Horus]] and Stella Celeste). He then splits from the party with Melina to follow the slave-trader trail to the South Blue ([[Session 013 — The Reaper|Episode 13]]).
