# Capone Family

One of the five mafia families of the West Blue (with the [[Marlon Family]], [[Moulan Family]], [[Donatelli Family]] and [[Russo Family]]), all of which joined the assault party on the Juniper Islands.

An attempt on boss [[Lorenzo Capone]]'s life — blamed on the [[Guiseppi Family]] — broke down negotiations and gave the Capones the justification for war. Per the lore, the negotiations were designed to force the [[Royal Flush Gang]] into a position where war became unavoidable. Capone forces took West Town within minutes of landing, then seized Guiseppi ports until only York Town remained, demanding the heads of every remaining Guiseppi in exchange for sparing the island. The current Capone leader is said to be kind to slaves. See [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Capone Invasion — Time Skip]].
