# Giggles D. Cloud

## Description

Captain of the [[Circle of Clowns]]; wears a half porcelain mask. (Not the Foundry stub [[Giggles]] under [[Soundless 5]].)

## Role

Slave trader fronting as a traveling circus captain, "stranded for about a year" at W.G.A.R. **Dead** — killed by the crew in [[Session 012 — Clown Tricks|Session 12]].

## Personal Quests

None.

## Backstory

- **Session 11:** introduces himself at the tent in the W.G.A.R. dome, claiming a ship on the south side and asking for help at night. When the fight starts he flees by speedboat toward the Calm Belt; [[Veyl Corven]] rams it ([[Session 011 — Welcome to the Jungle|Episode 11]]).
- **Session 12:** brought back to the crew, he keeps yelling at [[Bubbles D. Clown|Bubbles]] while questioned; the crew kills him afterwards ([[Session 012 — Clown Tricks|Episode 12]]).
