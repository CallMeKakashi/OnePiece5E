# Bubbles D. Clown

## Description

Heavyset ("the fat guy") handler of the Sea King [[Bessy Jr.|Bessie Jr]] in the [[Circle of Clowns]]. The crew nicknames him **Bessie Senior**.

## Role

Controls Bessie Jr, who carries the clowns' ship. After [[B.O.B]] wins him over he sides with the crew and steers Bessie Jr back toward W.G.A.R.

## Personal Quests

Freedom, and good treatment for himself and Bessie Jr, as promised by Bob.

## Backstory

- [[Session 012 — Clown Tricks|Session 12]]: kept under by the captain [[Giggles D. Cloud]]. Woken and bargained with by Bob (treats, food, a massage, "you won't have to answer to nobody"), he turns on the clowns. The crew rests on Bessie's back and bonds with him; [[Baptiste]] later impersonates him to the slave-trade contact on the clowns' den den mushi.
- [[Session 013 — The Reaper (No DM Audio)|Session 13]]: [[Graff Bolt]] and [[Melina Celeste]] want him and [[Bessy Jr.|Bessie Jr]] handed over; the crew refuses and is knocked out by Graff. He and Bessie wait at the beach near [[W.G.A.R.]] until the Marines are ready to leave.
