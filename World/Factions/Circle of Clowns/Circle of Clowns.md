# Circle of Clowns

Traveling circus-slave trade ring (also referenced as Ring of Clowns). A circus front; in play ([[Session 011 — Welcome to the Jungle|Session 11]]) they are slave traders, "stranded for about a year" at W.G.A.R. They ran a ship carried by the Sea King [[Bessy Jr.|Bessie Jr]]. The crew killed the clowns in [[Session 012 — Clown Tricks|Session 12]]; a slave-trade contact in the wider network still believes the circus is bringing slaves to South Blue.
