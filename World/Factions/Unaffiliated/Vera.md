# Vera

## Description

One of the [[Omen Sisters]]. Resembles Kira, a girl [[Baptiste]] and Kalla met on a shared side mission.

## Role

Appeared briefly to [[Baptiste]] on [[Decibella Kingdom]], seemed to know him, and vanished in front of him. The crew freed her when guards were dragging her through the dirt; she left. Her purpose there is unknown ("short and strange encounter", DM, 2026-10-02).

## Episode 14

- Sent the crew to [[Riff Sin]] and the Decibella rebellion. Riff curses her ("that woman, I shouldn't have trusted her").
- Had gone with [[Cadence]] to the Upper City and was captured; Riff says she "acquired ships" to evacuate the commoners, which was a lie to keep him compliant.
