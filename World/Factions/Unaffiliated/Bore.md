# Bore

## Description

A ticket-taker at [[W.G.A.R.]], dressed in a bear costume. Like the rest of the staff, says they are "gifted by our God to entertain".

## Role

Gatekeeper of the W.G.A.R. dome. Takes the crew's entry fee of **600 berries** for six people after [[Roma]] haggles down from 6,000.

## Personal Quests

None.

## Backstory

- **Session 11:** tells the crew that their Lord is inside everyone and that "the Lord perished all the devils", and that the staff took over the animals' roles after World Government visitors sent only one animal. Roma threatens to cut Bore open to find the Lord, and [[B.O.B|Bob]] rips off Bore's tail costume ([[Session 011 — Welcome to the Jungle|Episode 11]]).
