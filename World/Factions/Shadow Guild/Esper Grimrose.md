# Esper Grimrose

## Description

Member of the [[Shadow Guild]], **Scorpion Unit**, rank **19**.

## Role

Bounty hunter, the surviving half of the Scorpion Unit with [[Kyle Bloodfin]]. Her brother belonged to the **Reaper** unit, a past crew that [[Baptiste]] and [[Malphas]] killed.

## Personal Quests

Revenge on [[Baptiste]] and [[Malphas]], for her brother in the Reaper unit and now for Kyle.
