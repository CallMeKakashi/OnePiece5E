# Kyle Bloodfin

## Description

Bull shark fishman. Member of the [[Shadow Guild]], **Scorpion Unit**, rank **9**. Status: **dead**.

## Role

Bounty hunter. Half of the two-person Scorpion Unit with [[Esper Grimrose]]. The Guild declared open season on all [[Blackhand]] crew after [[Blackhand Cane]]'s disappearance, and Kyle was sent after the whole Lunarfold crew.

## Personal Quests

Hunt the Lunarfolds. Killed before he finished the job.
