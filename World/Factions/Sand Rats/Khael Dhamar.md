# Khael Dhamar

Not the same person as [[Kael Dhamar]] (the fishman Foundry actor).

## Description

Human (Alabastan), male, late 30s, neutral good (reformed). Tall and lean with sun-scarred skin and dark-red eyes, in tattered desert robes mixed with scavenged naval gear. Twin talwars at his hips, one etched "For Sin", the other "For Redemption". Fighter 3 / Rogue 2, former smuggler. Slow, gravelly voice. Rubs a pouch of desert sand when anxious.

## Role

Leader of the [[Sand Rats]], who free slaves smuggled out of Alabasta. Mentor to [[Tariq Solen]].

## Backstory

Khael guarded Alabasta's royal caravans, then smuggled slaves to foreign merchants for coin. A fellow guard betrayed him, selling his wife and daughter into bondage and leaving him for dead on the dunes. An old hermit found him and brought him to the Sand Rats, who distrusted him until his knowledge of slavers and routes proved useful. Years later he leads them, freeing the same kind of people he once sold, hoping to find his family or die trying.
