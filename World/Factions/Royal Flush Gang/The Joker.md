## Description

"Mad Demon of the [[Royal Flush Gang]]." Wears a crimson coat and a white mask; fights with two massive blades joined by a chain. Laughs at the wrong moments, asks his victims whether they have ever killed someone, and loses patience fast. Status: alive.

## Role

Royal Flush Gang enforcer. Introduced himself in the South Blue as "Number One" of the [[Shadow Guild]]. After three months of silence he sent word to [[The Ace]] about the hit on [[Lorenzo Capone]]: "It ain't them, dummy." [[Matthew -The Jack- Burgess|Jack]] has faced his "wave of blades" before.

## Backstory

**South Blue arms market** ([[Timeline/Undated/[Backstory] Joker — Man in Red]]) — Arriving as Number One of the Shadow Guild to buy passage home, he learned the [[Sixfold]] had hit one of the guild's main bases: nobody killed, but most of its hunters crippled. He laughed, shot the smuggler boss ("Months of preparation... and now there's no satisfaction left to be had"), killed the generator and slaughtered the warehouse in the dark. He then freed the captive tribesmen who had been searching for a swamp god, refused to be called their savior, and offered them the ship if they sailed him to the Juniper Islands.
