## Description

**Four-Armed Crusher Jahera** — first mate of the [[Spider Nest Pirates]].

## Role

First Mate.

## D&D 5e stat block

**Large humanoid (longarm), neutral evil**  
**Armor Class:** 17 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 190 (20d10+80)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**STR 20 (+5), DEX 12 (+1), CON 18 (+4), INT 10 (+0), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 14 (+2)**

**Skills:** Athletics +10, Intimidation +7  
**Challenge:** 13 (10,000 XP)

**Multi-Armed Combatant.** Jahera can make one additional weapon attack per turn with her extra arms.  
**Unyielding.** When reduced to 0 HP, Jahera can make a DC 15 Con save. On success, she drops to 1 HP instead (once per long rest).

**Actions**  
**Multiattack.** Jahera makes four melee attacks.  
**Crusher’s Grip.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 18 (2d10+5) bludgeoning damage, and target must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be grappled.  
**Ground Slam (Recharge 5–6).** Jahera slams all four arms into the ground. Each creature within 15 ft. must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 27 (6d8) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.
