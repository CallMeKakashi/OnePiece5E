## D&D 5e stat block

**Medium humanoid (longleg), chaotic evil**  
**Armor Class:** 16 (flexible armor)  
**Hit Points:** 70 (12d8+12)  
**Speed:** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

**STR 14 (+2), DEX 20 (+5), CON 12 (+1), INT 10 (+0), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 14 (+2)**

**Skills:** Acrobatics +10, Performance +7  
**Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP)

**Spider Agility.** Chloe can climb difficult surfaces, including ceilings, without a check.  
**Chaotic Acrobat.** When Chloe takes the Dodge action, she may also move up to 10 ft.

**Actions**  
**Multiattack.** Chloe makes three Spider Kick attacks.  
**Spider Kick.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 9 (1d8+5) bludgeoning damage.  
**Web Dance (Recharge 5–6).** Chloe dashes up walls or across surfaces, striking at up to three different targets. Each must make a DC 16 Dex save or take 14 (3d8) bludgeoning damage.
