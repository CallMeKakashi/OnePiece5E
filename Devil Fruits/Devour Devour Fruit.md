## Description

Any space between the user's fists is devoured and erased. Seen in the Emperor's Unity War footage, where chunks of the battlefield are missing ([[Session 015 — Animal Within|Session 15]], DM, 2026-10-02). [[Coda]] imitates the technique, firing a void from a ring of his fingers that deletes a circle of wall, because he has seen it personally.
