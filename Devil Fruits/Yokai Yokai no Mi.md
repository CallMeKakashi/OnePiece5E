## Description

Campaign-registered **Paramecia** devil fruit ("Yokai Yokai No Mi — Owned by Simon", devil-fruit-dex 2026-07-27). Current eater: [[Simon The One Armed Tyrant|Simon Tideborn]].

## Powers

Per Simon's live Foundry sheet:

- **Conjure yokai** — invisible yokai entities inspired by Japanese mythology; unseen by creatures without Observation Haki.
- **Command** — up to three yokai at once; they can attack, restrain, frighten, or manipulate the battlefield. Yokai attacks count as magical and haki-infused.
- **Named yokai** — see [[Yokai]] (Kurohime, Aburasen, Senju Gaki, Kagemegan, Shirō Hone-Oni, Kyōkan, Byakkōmaru).
- **Yokai Dominion** — full release: yokai flood the battlefield for a minute.
- **Enhancement** — a Yokai-enhanced [[Zara Tideborn]] form has been shown.

## Drawbacks

- **Ocean's Scorn** — the eater cannot swim; running water and seastone apply as in [[What Are Devil Fruits]].
