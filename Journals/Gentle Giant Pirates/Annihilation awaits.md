An unnamed island south of Drum Island

"Stop! Leave them. Take me instead," as the man vomits blood with every breath.

A spray of blood splatters onto the man as a look of agony and despair dawns on his face.

He screams in rage and fury as his blood starts to boil, and he attacks the man wearing a Vice Admiral's coat standing in front of him.

But before he can even make contact... a flame erupts from the side, burning him to a crisp.

All that remains is the locket he wore around his neck of the Sun God Nika.

"Took you long enough, Rookie." The Vice Admiral speaks to the man with embers still erupting from his hand, wearing a Captain's coat.

"I apologize, sir. The last battle left some aching wounds, but I have dispatched the Logia user."

"What of the machine?" The Vice Admiral questions as he searches through the body of the headless Fishman lying at his feet.

"I am sorry, but he seems to have escaped... swam right into the ocean. I could not follow him." The Marine Captain says as he looks at the remains of a Skypiean near his feet.

"Did we need to go this far, sir? They surrendered as soon as you destroyed the fishboat they called their ship," the Captain queried as his eyes wandered into the forest. He notices small creature footsteps coming up to the charred remains of the man he just killed, Fenris Wolfblood, one of the remaining Blackhand Pirates and the captain of his own unit.

The Vice Admiral turns around to look at the monkey as he raises his fist and forms a claw, with the monkey in his peripheral vision. The other Marine reaches out and restrains the monkey.

"What do you think you are doing, Rookie? Put him down so I can erase this poor creature?" the Vice Admiral barks at the lower-ranking Marine.

"Horus, sir. I think this monkey can be of use to lure the rest of the Blackhand Pirates. Let me take him in as a prisoner," Leon pleads.

Horus lowers his arm. "Fine."

A Marine recruit runs through the bloodied snow as he salutes the two high-ranking Marines.

"Sir, we have a message from Sir Heathcliff. He encountered another unit, as our intel suggested. He is currently on hold and asked me to escort you to the communication array. He has something to show you."

"Very well. Come, Rookie. We've got work to do," Horus exclaims as he hands the recruit a bag.

"Take this into custody. Send them to Cipher Pol. I need the location of each remaining Pirate that still aligns themselves with Blackhand. Verify the identities of the dead Pirates behind us and rescind their bounties. I need them to know that I will be coming for them as well. Increase the bounty of fleeing Pirate."

"Aye, aye, sir." The Marine salutes and runs to the Marine warship.

Leon knocks out the monkey as he puts him on his shoulder, following Horus.
