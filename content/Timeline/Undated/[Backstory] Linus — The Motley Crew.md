# Linus — The Motley Crew

[[Linus Marrow]] tells the party (Mira, Thompson, Tray, Jack, Baptiste, Zim present) his story, prompted by Jack deciphering his old journal: slavery and Nikolai's MADS facility with his sister Elizabeth ([[Liz]]); the attack (by the crew Mira was with) that separated them; the Dead Spot mist and the impossible abandoned *Queen Anne's Revenge*; Pasha, [[Ronan]] and Astor; Horus at Grinjaw's contest; the [[Sixfold]] and reuniting with Liz; Vorro ([[Calder Voss]]) and Rias; the birth of [[Vireth]]; Blackhand's Marine past and the Trinity War (Virellis, [[Vruvia]], Starris); and the road to [[Virellis Kingdom]]. Written as a table monologue script.

---

_"I was waiting for everyone to get better before I could finally say my piece about whatever has been happening."_

_Jack here approached me a few days earlier. Apparently, he found my journal back on your ship, Baptiste, and deciphered what was written in it._

_"Baptiste, do you remember what we mentioned back at Spirit Cliffs? We had all encountered the same type of ring before. That was two years ago at Virellis Kingdom."_

_"Now, to give full context, I'll begin from the start."_

_"I have a sister, Elizabeth Marrow. You've probably heard of her. She's part of the Sixfolds. Anyway, my family comes from a long line of Marines. One day, a pirate came to our house, killed my parents for revenge, and took us as slaves to be sold to a warlord in the North Blue."_

_"We lived in those cages for so long that we started making plans to escape, but none of them ever came to fruition. One day, a man in a white coat bought us. He looked at our cage and... I guess he saw something in us."_

_"He took us to a place of science and knowledge. I got caught up in it. I learned everything from him. His name was Nikolai. He worked for a secret sect of a larger organization called MADS. They researched all types of species—Minks, Fishmen, Merfolk, Sky Islanders, and more—in search of the perfect creature. That was his vision."_

_"I got lost in that vision, but my older sister knew what we were doing wasn't right. One night she convinced me to leave with her. She found a way off the island. We split up for only a few moments..."_

_"And that was the day the facility was attacked by a pirate crew because they had someone from their crew there."_

_Later I found out that person was Mira."_

Everyone turns toward Mira.

_"Wait, what happened?"_

Linus continues.

_"Anyway... I found my sister buried beneath the rubble. During the chaos, part of the facility collapsed. I tried to pull her out from under the burning rocks."_

_He removes his gloves, revealing the burn scars on both palms._

_"But I didn't have these powers back then."_

_His hands phase through one another._

_"I heard people coming. I had to leave. I ran for so long, but eventually someone who knew Nikolai found me. He was kind enough to let me go—as long as I paid him for his troubles."_

_"So over the years, I stole. I stole and stole and stole from wherever I could. I even stole this fruit. And then I stole from you."_

_He looks at Jack._

_"Anyway, around six or seven years later, I saw a man in an orange jumpsuit stumble into a bar I used to visit. He was bleeding. From the jumpsuit I could tell he'd escaped from a facility."_

_"The guilt finally outweighed my fear. For once, I used what I'd learned for something good."_

_"When I patched him up, I learned his name was Pasha. He was being chased by bounty hunters. Sure enough, those bounty hunters arrived. We fought them off alongside two nosy brats named Astor and Ronan who couldn't mind their own business like everyone else."_

_"We stole the bounty hunters' ship and ran because I knew Nikolai would send more."_

_"Pasha navigated us out of rough waters, and while Astor and Ronan mostly butted heads, we eventually arrived at the Dead Spot."_

_"At the time, there was a famous dead zone in the East Blue called the Dead Spot. Ships vanished there. Nobody knew why. The reports were always the same: a bright flash of light and a sea of mist."_

_"You wouldn't know about it, Mira. It only appeared for a few years in the East Blue while all of you were in the Grand Line."_

_"We wandered through the mist for what felt like forever, and eventually we stumbled upon a ship."_

_"A dead husk rose from the depths of the sea."_

_"At the time I didn't know it, but it was definitely Blackhand's Queen Anne's Revenge."_

Mira interrupts.

_"That's not possible. I was with Blackhand during that time, and we were on the Queen Anne's Revenge."_

_"Yes. I have a theory. Stick with me."_

_"During my time working for Nikolai, I reviewed papers from other scientists. One project involved Nikolai and a man named Caesar. They were trying to harness the power of an ancient artifact to generate this mist."_

_"The papers said they couldn't control it."_

_"Soon after, we found an abandoned MADS facility. We destroyed it as a last resort and escaped."_

_"I think Nikolai eventually learned to control that power."_

_"There was a note attached to the MistFrame Bioweapon project. It said the flow of time experienced by subjects varied from none to decades. Some subjects even claimed to encounter one another despite entering years apart."_

_"Now, let me continue."_

_"When we were leaving that island, Astor found something curious."_

_Linus produces a drawing._

_"A vivre compass. Instead of a needle, it used a piece of vivre card bearing Blackhand's sigil. Astor should still have it to this day."_

Thompson stares at Mira.

_"Captain... that's impossible. Renzo had one made. The old man never got one made. Said it was a bad omen. 'The Marines are spying on us.' That was always his reasoning."_

_"I agree. It's all very jarring. But I'm only putting these pieces together now. I hadn't looked at my journal in years. Thanks to Jack, I started reading it again."_

_"Anyway, Astor insisted we follow the compass. He knew Blackhand."_

Mira nods.

_"Ronan resisted, but he was outvoted."_

_"So we headed toward the Grand Line."_

_"Along the way, we stopped at an island where we met an idiot pirate named Grinjaw. Ronan insisted we enter one of his pirate ballads to earn some money."_

_"Like moths to a flame, the Marines attacked."_

_"And not just anyone. Vice Admiral Horus the Maw."_

_"We had no chance. He crushed us like insects."_

Mira scoffs.

_"There is no way in hell you escaped Horus."_

_"Well, as luck would have it, only one person in the world could have rescued us."_

_"Actually, make that two."_

_"Veyl and Serica rescued us. They introduced themselves as members of the Sixfolds and told us their boss wanted to meet us at Spirit Cliffs."_

_"Since they saved us, we agreed."_

_"I should probably tell you a bit about Ronan."_

_"Ronan came from a small village in the East Blue. He grew up at the Church of Sister Elise, where Sister Elise raised orphans with the help of the townsfolk."_

_"That's also where Blackhand's old helmsman Morley retired."_

_"Morley taught Ronan the art of Stingray Judo."_

_"But Morley was eventually killed in a conflict with the Marines by a rookie named Briggs."_

_"Yes. The same Briggs who's a Commodore now."_

_"Afterward, Briggs extorted the island. If they didn't pay, he'd send pirates he hired to wreak havoc."_

_"Ever since then, Ronan has spent every day trying to earn money and send it home."_

_"During our travels, he carried a very special item he hoped to sell, but buyers for something like that don't come around often."_

_"That's important later."_

_"When we reached Spirit Cliffs, Ronan accepted a job from Bramble. Construction supplies for a new project were being stolen from a route protected by Kirro King, a crawfish Fishman."_

_"Ronan knew him. Kirro had once served under Morley."_

_"So Ronan left to earn reward money."_

_"I went to meet the boss of the Sixfolds."_

_"Astor went looking for food and ended up meeting Aralia after beating up some Marines who were harassing her."_

_"And that's when I met the boss."_

_"Liz."_

_"My sister was alive."_

_"She'd stolen a Devil Fruit. During the collapse, she ate it while trapped beneath the rubble. It saved her life."_

_"She'd spent years looking for me while I spent years hiding from everyone."_

_"Then she showed me the roster of her mercenary group."_

_"One name stood out immediately."_

_"Rias Decibella."_

_"The bastard I'd been paying ransom to all these years."_

_"He knew Liz was alive."_

_"I told her everything. She was furious."_

_"But there was another problem. One of the Sixfolds had been captured and was being held at the new Marine base under construction at Spirit Cliffs."_

_"Luckily, they hadn't received seastone reinforcements yet, so breaking him out was easy enough."_

_"After that, we all had to leave. We'd caused too much trouble to stay."_

_"Liz left behind the home she'd built over the years—the Armada-A."_

_"We took the Sixfolds ship, the Armada-B, instead."_

_"We didn't want to abandon our old ship, though. Pasha suggested taking the wheel from our old vessel and replacing the Armada-B's wheel with it."_

_"Astor handled that easily enough."_

_"Then we sailed for the Grand Line."_

_"Liz wanted to pick up another member of the Sixfolds. Vorro. Nowadays he's known as Captain Calder Voss."_

_"At the time, we didn't know his connection to everything."_

_"That was also the day we finally called ourselves the Motley Crew."_

_"We never really had a captain, but everyone knew it was Ronan and Astor."_

_"Not long afterward, we learned Vorro's true identity."_

_"Liz's powers worked somewhat like a vivre card. She could track anyone carrying paper she'd touched."_

_"That's how we discovered Rias was on one of the islands we visited."_

_"On another island, while gathering supplies, we learned Vorro and Rias were working together against us."_

_"Aralia was taken hostage, and there was nothing Ronan could do."_

_"That's when I revealed something I'd been keeping secret."_

_"Aralia was pregnant."_

_"The child was Astor's."_

_"And that was when Astor decided to call in a favor."_

Linus looks toward Zim.

_"I helped them track down those two losers."_

_"The fight was brutal. Even with all of us together, they were powerful fighters with dangerous Devil Fruits."_

_"That's when Ronan finally ate the fruit he'd been carrying all this time."_

_"That was the day Vireth was born."_

_"Ronan ate the Fusion-Fusion Fruit."_

_"Normally it lets him fuse objects, but in extremely rare cases, if two souls possess perfect symmetry, it can fuse people."_

_"To break free of Rias's Command Fruit, which was forcing Astor and Ronan to fight one another, Ronan placed his hands on Astor."_

_"And they became Vireth."_

_"One Devil Fruit can topple nations."_

_"Two Devil Fruits wielded as one?"_

_"The fight ended instantly."_

_"The fusion didn't last long, though. They separated moments later."_

_"Nobody could continue fighting after that."_

_"Vorro grabbed the half-dead body of Rias and escaped."_

_"Ronan wanted to chase them down and finish it, but Aralia convinced him otherwise."_

_"After that, we finally followed Astor's vivre card."_

_"And it led us to Blackhand."_

_"I held a grudge against Blackhand. I blamed him for Liz and I being separated."_

_"So I did something incredibly stupid and attacked him."_

_"Only for Mira to launch me straight into the ocean."_

Mira grins.

_"Damn right I did. He thinks he can lay a hand on the old man aboard his own ship?"_

_"Anyway, Blackhand calmed everyone down. He was happy Astor had found a life for himself."_

_"I should probably explain how Astor knew him."_

_"Some of you may not know this, but Blackhand was once a Marine."_

_"One of the heroes credited with ending the Trinity War."_

_"Later, those heroes became known as the Tragic Three. One was punished for failing to stop another from murdering the third."_

_"At least, that's the Marine version of the story."_

_"I think some of you already know the truth."_

_"The Trinity War was fought against three rebel nations that rejected the World Government."_

_"Virellis Kingdom."_

_"Vruvia."_

At that, Tray glances toward Mira.

_"Wait... that's where you're from, isn't it, Captain?"_

Mira merely folds her arms.

_"Keep talking, Linus."_

_"And the Republic of Starris."_

_"By the war's end, two of those nations had ceased to exist. Virellis remained independent, but eventually bowed to the World Government to spare its people from endless war."_

_"Astor was the son of a Marine."_

_"The same Marine whose wife, Stella Celeste, was allegedly killed by Blackhand."_

_"Astor still doesn't know that."_

_"When Blackhand met him in Loguetown and learned his name, he withdrew from the New World and left it to the next generation."_

_"The Roger Pirates and Rocks ruled those seas."_

_"Blackhand instead remained in the East Blue for a time."_

_"More recently, however, a crew member he considered as much a son as Astor got mixed up with the wrong people."_

_He looks toward Baptiste._

_"Blackhand had originally intended to travel to the Grand Line to find a kid named Dravos who could take him to the South Blue and make sure the Guiseppi Family wouldn't retaliate against Baptiste."_

_"Instead, as a final gift to Astor, he used Dravos's abilities to send him to Virellis."_

_"And that's how we arrived at Virellis Kingdom."_

_"The home of Aralia Cindross."_

_"Princess of Virellis."_

_"An island shaped like a perfect circle, carved in the image of the moon."_

## Shorter version

A condensed take on the same monologue (formerly `Sessions/Linus' Monologue.md`). It ends with a conclusion the long version lacks: the rings, the drug, the mist, the experiments and the Spider Nest "all lead back to two men" — Nikolai and Briggs.

---

Linus sighs heavily and rubs his forehead.

"Jack showed me something few days ago. My old journal. I thought I buried all of this long time ago."

"My sister Elizabeth and I were sold into slavery as children. We were bought by a scientist named Nikolai. He worked for MADS."

"He researched every race he could find. Fishmen. Minks. Sky Islanders. Everyone. He wanted to create what he called the perfect creature."

"My sister realized what we were doing was wrong. We tried to escape."

"The day we ran, the facility was attacked."

He glances toward Mira.

"You were there."

Mira blinks.

"What?"

"You attacked the facility years ago. You just never knew I was inside."

"I lost my sister that day. Thought she died."

He pauses.

"She didn't."

"Elizabeth is Liz. Leader of the Sixfolds."

A long silence follows.

"I later learned something else. The Dead Spot in East Blue was not natural."

"It was created."

"Nikolai and other MADS scientists were experimenting with a strange mist generated through ancient technology."

"I read the reports myself."

"The mist did strange things."

"Some people lost years."

"Some gained years."

"Some claimed to meet people who entered the mist years before or years after them."

He takes a deep breath.

"I think Nikolai learned how to control it."

"Years later I sailed with Astor and Ronan."

"Inside that mist we found something impossible."

"An abandoned Queen Anne's Revenge."

Mira immediately stands.

"That's impossible."

"I know."

"Because according to every account, Blackhand was still sailing on that ship at the exact same time."

He reaches into his coat.

"And Astor found this."

A sketch of a Vivre Compass.

"A vivre card tied to Blackhand."

Thompson nearly chokes.

"The old man never allowed vivre cards."

"Exactly."

Linus nods.

"Something impossible was happening."

He looks around the room.

"The reason I tell you all this now is because after reading my journal again, I keep reaching the same conclusion."

"The rings."

"The drug."

"The mist."

"The experiments."

"The Spider Nest."

"They all lead back to two men."

"Nikolai and Briggs"
