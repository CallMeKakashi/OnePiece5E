# Gentle Giant Pirates

A second week of downtime on Decibella, then a **Davy Back Fight** against the Gentle Giant Pirates for **Dravos**, whom Baptiste wants as guide to Zim (Episode 18).

## Beats

1. **Downtime** — Bob trains in his Necrotic Savant body; Roma looks after the animals Riff gave the crew (for Tigor's W.G.A.R. reserve); Baptiste tests his bell fragment with Kalla's against the rings.
2. **The ask** — Baptiste asks Fenris for Dravos; Fenris refuses to give him up free and proposes a Davy Back Fight (best of three, no devil fruits).
3. **Game 1** — Bob beats Facade. The Battle of Beasts follows in [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] The Walking Dead]].
