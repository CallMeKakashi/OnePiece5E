# The Friendly Baron

The crew boards **Goru Yamashita**'s noble ship and he agrees to hide them at **Cassian Valehart**'s mansion; Roma goes Sulong under the full moon and kills 10 to 20 Marines; Malphas and Bob pull Roma out of the sea (Episode 24).

## Beats

1. **Dream** — [[Baptiste]] sees Aegir's defeat.
2. **Goru** — boarding the noble ship, Black Hand contact; Jack and Linus's grudge; Goru learns Zim and Voss are aboard.
3. **Valehart** — [[Cassian Valehart]] hosts the crew at the mansion for a week.
4. **Roma** — kills on his walk, then Sulong in Noosehold; Malphas lures him into the sea; Bob helps haul him out.
5. **Mira's call** — kill or release [[Calder Voss]].
6. **Supplement** — Ace, Thompson and ship scenes in [[Session 024.5 — Interstitial RP]] (not a separate episode).
