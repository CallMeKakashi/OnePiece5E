# Callisto — Hallow's Trap

Unrecorded session on [[Callisto]], known only from the DM's account; see [[Session 005.5 — Callisto]]. It follows [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Interlude — Red Departs and Reverse Mountain]] and leads straight into [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Hallow's End]].

## Beats

1. **Reunion**: both parties plus [[Veyl Corven]] and [[Serica Corven]] meet on Callisto and see workers, including children, labouring day and night.
2. **Lure**: [[Hallow]] (a lineman) points them to a large building, promising a clue about Vireth.
3. **Ambush**: the [[Spider Nest Pirates]], who hold a grudge against Veyl and Serica, attack. Veyl and Serica are split off to fight [[Ben]].
4. **Garbage chute**: the party falls down a chute and fights [[Daniel]] and [[Chloe]] while Hallow tries to crush them with trash compactors. Daniel and Chloe are tied up.
5. **Facility run**: they chase Hallow past empty Mugen armours and mechanical factory arms.
6. **Core**: Hallow unleashes chimeras. Veyl and Serica crash down from above with a defeated Ben.
