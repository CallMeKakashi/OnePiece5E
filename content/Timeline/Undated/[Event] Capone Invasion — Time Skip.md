# Capone Invasion — Time Skip

Three weeks on the Juniper Islands between the ravine collapse and the crew waking up (world-lore, 2026-06-19).

## Beats

1. **Ravine collapse** — After the battle with the titan ape, the ravine buries the survivors. [[Matthew -The Jack- Burgess|Jack]] survives a fall into the ocean, reaches shore, and fishermen alert the [[Guiseppi Family]]. Jack reports everything, including the [[Spider Nest Pirates]]' involvement.
2. **Clinic** — The rescued crew reach [[Linus Marrow|Linus]]'s clinic barely alive; many stay unconscious for nearly three weeks.
3. **Capone landing** — Right after the ravine incident, [[Capone Family]] forces land and take West Town within minutes. The attack on [[Lorenzo Capone]] gave them the justification allies required; the negotiations had been designed to force the [[Royal Flush Gang]] into war.
4. **Royal Flush returns** — [[King]] and [[Queen]] turn back before reaching the West Blue. [[The Ace]] arrives a couple of weeks later.
5. **Guiseppi cornered** — Guiseppi ports fall one by one until only York Town remains. The Capones demand the heads of every remaining Guiseppi in exchange for leaving the islanders untouched, and promise not to harm held territory unless retaliated against.
6. **Sato** — After several failed attempts, [[Zim]], disguised as "Sato", a woman supposedly escaping Guiseppi enslavement, infiltrates Capone territory and gathers intel. She drops the act on hearing of the crew's near death, waits at the clinic, and resolves to infiltrate the Capone stronghold in West Town (the current Capone leader is said to be kind to slaves).
7. **Uneasy alliance** — [[Rum Guiseppi|Rum]] is angry and distrustful of her, but the Guiseppis cannot lose another ally: [[Mira the Unbreakable|Mira]] alone keeps the Capones out of York Town, and Zim has Mira's protection.
8. **Waking** — Three weeks after the collapse, the crew wake to an island on the brink of war.
