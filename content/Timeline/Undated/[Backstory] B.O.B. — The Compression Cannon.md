# B.O.B. — The Compression Cannon

Young Bob and [[Ronan]] at the Scholars' Guild: an experimental compression cannon misfires straight up, and Bob improvises a steel-plate ramp and piston "suspension system" to save the machine, with Ronan bracing the plate beside him. Short story by player Xenos.

---

The scholars guild usually faced many dangers. Some came from the sea. Some from the things the scholars insisted on studying despite everyone else in the community agreeing that they should probably leave them alone. And some came from Bob.

It was another afternoon when Bob found Ronan in the guilds outer workshop, helping some scholars by carrying huge thick steel plates, probably ship parts. Bob had come to return some tools and instruments he had borrowed the previous week. Ronan saw Bob walk to them and greeted him - "Hey, messing with the guilds stuff again?"
Bob - "Not at all. I just came to return these", pointed at the instruments he's carrying- some hammers, gauntlet, pistons, wires.
Ronan - "I've seen you be here for 2 hours"
Bob - "I got distracted"
Ronan - "Heh, you always get distracted"
Bob ignored him. His eyes were already locked to the enormous cannon sitting at the far end. It was unlike anything Bob had seen before. Far bigger in diameter than the cannons mounted on most ships, but similar in length. It was a heavy mortar dressed up like a cannon with a strange rotating mechanism beneath it. Several scholars were moving around, some lifting a massive iron sphere beside it with pulleys. 
Bob went to a scholar nearby and asked, "What is it?"
The old man replied, "It's an experimental compression cannon"
Bob’s eyes lit up.
Ronan made a weak attempt, "Bob, you've already spent your entire afternoon here. Elara's gonna get mad if you turn this into another two hours."
Bob couldn't hear him or his sigh as he was lost in the conversation with the old man about it's pressure, projectile velocity, modulus of toughness and several other things Ronan was too tired to pay attention to.

Soon, the experiment began. Scholars loaded the cannon with the iron sphere, tilted it to aim at the sea. Everyone moved behind the safety line. Ronan pulled a reluctant Bob away too.
A warning got shouted, the fuse was lit. Then -
BOOM!
The cannon fired.
Except it didn’t fire correctly.
With the force of explosion from the combustion inside the cannon, it's rotating mechanism failed to hold it rigidly as it tilted upwards, shooting the extra large cannonball straight up to the sky.
Bobs eyes widened, "Uh oh..."
Ronan stared at him, "What?"
Bob - "It's about to come down..."
The scholars began shouting by then.
The cannonball was descending directly towards the cannon. If it landed where it headed, that might crush the barrel in a direct collision and break the entire mechanism.
"Move!", someone screamed, in a voice that is full of panic but at the same time one that is used to experiments failing all the time.
Ronan was about to do exactly that when he noticed Bob by the cannon, somehow he managed to push a trolley with the thick steel plates almost to the cannon. 
Ronan rushed to him,  "What do you think you're doing?!"
Bob - "Making a ramp! Or the cannon will break!"
Ronan - "You've got to be joking"
He looked up, the iron sphere was too close for them to pull Bob to a safe place by now.
Cursing, he grabbed the plate Bob was trying to get up and pulled with him
"Higher!" Bob shouted
Ronan - "Higher?!"
Bob - "Angle it!"
Ronan shoved the plate upward with both hands. Bob got behind him, climed on the cannon housing and put on his gauntlet
"What are you doing!?" Ronan shouted
Reply came, "Suspension system!"
Bob put a bunch of large pistons together on the ground and angled them, fastenned a belt around them with quick hands, connected their end to the gauntlet elbow and slammed the large palm of the gauntlet against the plate, "Brace for impact!"
"We're so dead", Ronan muttered against the steel plate.
The cannonball hit.
The plate buckled violently, the spring of the pistons made a loud cranking noise.
Ronan and Bob screamed as the weight drove them downward.
For a tiny fraction of a second during the collision, it seemed like they'd be crushed. Then the cannonball rolled. 
It scrapped across the plate, bounced once,  and rolled straight toward the beach.

Ronan stumbled backwards and nearly fell over.
Bob was lying on top of the cannon breathing heavily, his hand out of the gauntlet, arms and head hanging limply.
"You absolute lunatic!", Ronan yelled as he pulled him by his collars
"Uhh", Bob blinked.
Ronan - "We were about to die!"
Bob - "But we saved it"
Ronan - "Saved what?!"
Bob - "The cannon!"
Ronan - "..."
Bob - "It's... a pretty cool mechanism, you know. Would be a hassle to even get closer to this again due to all the precise measurements."
Ronan - "..."
Bob - "..."
Ronan finally spoke, "Thought you were a coward"
Bob - "Uhh, but I am tho?"
Ronan - "So you're just an idiot"
Bob - "Hey how-... you know what, fair I guess. Just... dont tell mom would you?"
Ronan - "You know she'll know about this soon, right?"
Bob - "Weeell, that might take a day or two. At least I'll be safe till then"
Ronan - "Sigh, you really are an idiot"

As they were cleaning up with the schollars, Bob suddenly said, "You were also standing underneath it"
Ronan - "Yeah"
Bob - "Why?"
Ronan - "To make sure you didnt die shithead"
Bob - "Oh... huh. Thanks I guess."
Ronan - "No wor-"
Bob - "Well, technically, I had already attached the pistons. To think one could manage to hold the plate from that kind of impact any more than what the pistons were doing is kinda stupid too, don't you think?"
Ronan - "... You... sigh. Just dont die before I get to the North Blue, can you?"
Bob - "Of course, why on earth would I ever wanna die" Bob laughed.
Ronan left him with his tinkering, thinking how soon he could return and get him and Elara out of here. He obviously needed someone to keep him in check and Elara can barely manage as is…
“Sister Elise would like them I'm sure.”
Meanwhile Bob was kneeling beside the cannon. He noticed it's firing mechanism was somewhat out of balance.
Bob grinned.
He was gonna get in trouble with Elara anyway tomorrow, what more could spending the night here do.
He finally had something new to fix…
