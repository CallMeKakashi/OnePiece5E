# Hallow's End

Destruction of [[Mugen Industries]]' facility on [[Callisto]] (Episode 6). Continues from [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Callisto — Hallow's Trap]].

## Beats

1. **Cogwork Core**: [[Roma]] keeps [[Ben]] down. [[Daniel]] agrees to help destroy the facility and says the old Tyrant, Silas, was killed by "our boss".
2. **Ring flare**: [[Veyl Corven]]'s ring box flares, the blood evaporates and a cog opens. The party escapes on foot, with Veyl carrying some of them using his Speed Fruit. Nobody is teleported.
3. **Hallow's workshop**: they find Hallow's journal ("the Doctor", [[Dr Nikolai Tesla]], built the core), a photo of Rye and Nera, blueprints labelling the core the "Divine Furnace", and loot. Then a long rest.
4. **Graveyard**: [[Hallow]] releases [[RyeNera]], which kills him. Ben kills the amalgam. Veyl and [[Serica Corven]] evacuate the workers.
5. **Departure**: the party's first ship has sailed off by itself (a Klabautermann). The Spiders give them passage north, and the crew is named the [[Lunarfolds]].
6. **Destruction**: at sea, Roma presses Hallow's red button and Callisto sinks.
