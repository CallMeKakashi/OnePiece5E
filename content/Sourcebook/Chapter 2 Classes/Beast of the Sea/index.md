## Beast of the Sea

Medium beast, neutral
Armor Class
13 + PB
Hit Points
6 + six times your marksman level (the
beast has a number of Hit Dice (d10) equal to
your marksman level)
Speed
5ft., swim 60 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
14 (+2) 14 (+2) 15 (+2) 8 (-1) 14 (+2) 11 (+0)
Senses
Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12 +
your proficiency bonus
Languages
Understands the languages you speak
PB
Equals your proficiency bonus
Amphibious.
The beast can breathe air and water.
Primal Bond.
You can add your proficiency bonus to
any ability check or saving throw that the beast
makes.
