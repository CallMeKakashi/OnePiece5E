### Actions

Binding Strike.
Melee Weapon Attack: +your
creation attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one
target. Hit: (1d10 + 2 + PB) piercing or
bludgeoning damage. The beast uses your creation
attack modifier to hit. In addition to damage, the
target is grappled (escape DC equals your creation
save DC). Until this grapple ends, the beast can’t
use this attack on another target.
