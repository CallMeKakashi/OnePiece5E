### Fighting Style

You adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty.
Choose one of the options in Chapter 5, in the Fighter Styles
section. You can’t take a Fighting Style option more than
once, even if you later get to choose again.
Whenever you reach a level in this class that grants the
Ability Score Improvement feature, you can replace a fighting
style you know with another fighting style available to
fighters.
