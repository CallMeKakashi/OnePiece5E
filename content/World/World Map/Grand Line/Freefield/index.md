# Freefield

An island the crew reaches after leaving [[Decibella Kingdom]]. They stop for ship repairs and supplies. Mayor [[Rashid al-Saffar]] ran it from a chili plantation that hid smuggled Alabastan slaves behind a spice trade.

## Episode 20

- The crew puts in for repairs, supplies and cage materials for its pets. [[Bramble]] quotes the repair price ([[Session 020 — Price of Freedom Part 1|Part 1]]).
- Rashid offers to pay the bill if the crew guards his plantation. The crew meets [[Khael Dhamar]]'s [[Sand Rats]] raiding it and learns Rashid is a slave trader.
- The [[Sharkfin Pirates]] kill Rashid and loot his house. The crew and Khael kill the captain, and the Sand Rats take the captives ([[Session 020 — Price of Freedom Part 2|Part 2]]).
