# Noosehold

Execution island twinned with [[Lex Imperia]], joined by a bridge (Marine warships line the bridge and the right-hand dock). Cases decided on Lex Imperia end here ("people get hanged here"). Civilian and fishing boats use the left-hand docks. A cathedral stands at the centre of a fountain square, ringed by the mansions of the judges. The Marines are building a gallows platform inside the cathedral for [[Droven Calligos|Droven]]'s execution.

## Episode 21

- The crew's tunnel mishap drops them here; they hide their ship in a cave dock ([[Session 021 — House of Justice|Episode 21]]).
- Baptiste burns two Marines in the cathedral, Roma sets off a prison riot, and armored Marines lock the island down.

## Episode 22

- Roma and Linus free Zim from a seastone cell on Voss's warship at the dock. The crew drives Voss's car back through the Valehart mansion to the cave dock and sails out under fire ([[Session 022 — The Missing Piece|Episode 22]]).
