# Callisto

Grand Line industrial island run by **[[Mugen Industries]]**. [[Hallow]] was station master, and it housed [[Dr Nikolai Tesla]]'s ("the Doctor") fusion and chimera program. Its workers, many of them children, laboured day and night in poor conditions. Transcript speech-to-text: "Calisto" / "Colisto".

**Status: destroyed.** It sank after [[Roma]] triggered Hallow's red-button failsafe in [[Episode 06 - Hallow's End]].

## Places

- **Harpoon Cay**: harbour where [[Malphas]] and [[B.O.B]] were to rendezvous with [[Baptiste]] ([[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Interlude — Red Departs and Reverse Mountain]])
- **The Core** ("Divine Furnace" on the blueprints): the cogwork core that fed bodies to the machine
- **Hallow's workshop**: old, abandoned foundational building with a trapdoor up to the graveyard
- **Graveyard**: underground burial ground where [[RyeNera]] killed Hallow
- **Hallow's office**, factories, and several docks. The buildings lock down with ID-card doors when the alarm sounds.
