# World Government Bank

World Government facility and bank branch on [[Spirit Cliff]]. The **branch director’s cabin** holds a small chest [[Bramble]] wants stolen (Episode 3 deal). Heavy marine patrols inside and around the city; no secret entrance per Bramble's blueprints.

## Overview

- **Type**: World Government facility (bank branch)
- **Primary function**: treasury operations, deposits/seizures, and financial control for [[Spirit Cliff]].
- **Security posture**: uniformed guards, controlled access, and rapid-response coordination with local Marine assets.

## Staff & leadership

- **Branch director**: [[Delaroth Halward|Director Halward Lynne]]

## Facilities

- **Director’s cabin**: target location for the chest theft during the Spirit Cliff arc.
- **Records/ledger access**: see [[The Black Ledger of Director Halward Lynne]].

## Episode 3–4

- Heist planned via casino (director gambles), disguises, and marine-armor infiltration.

## Episode 5

- Night raid: [[Red]] retrieves chest from director's cabin; marine/Sixfold battle at bank and casino — see [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Shackles Broken]].
