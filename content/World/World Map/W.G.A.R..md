# W.G.A.R.

**World Government Animal Reserve.** A semicircle island with a white dome over its centre; the log pose is fixed on it. The island was not originally World Government property; they took it over at some point. Sea not yet stated.

## Places

- **The dome**: empty except for a sign. The crew pays **600 berries** (six people, after [[Roma]] haggles down from 6,000) to [[Bore]] at the gate. Shops inside, visited by [[Daniel]] and [[Ben]].
- **The clowns' tent**: pitched in a hole cut in the dome by the [[Circle of Clowns]].

## Inhabitants

- Staff in animal costumes who say they are "gifted by our God to entertain", that their Lord is inside everyone and that "the Lord perished all the devils". There is no Lord: the staff and townsfolk were playing up their characters to keep visitors entertained ([[Session 013 — The Reaper (No DM Audio)|Session 13]]). Their story: visitors from the World Government once came to make a habitat but only sent one animal, so the staff took over the animals' roles; the real animals ran out long ago. Gatekeeper: [[Bore]]. The island is run by [[Bugor]], a bear.
- The [[Circle of Clowns]], a traveling circus (a front for slave traders) "stranded for about a year", led by [[Giggles D. Cloud]].

## Events

- [[Session 011 — Welcome to the Jungle|Session 11]]: the crew arrives, meets the clowns and a fight breaks out. Open question: what the "Lord" is.
- [[Session 012 — Clown Tricks|Session 12]]: the crew rides [[Bessy Jr.|Bessie Jr]] back toward the island.
- [[Session 013 — The Reaper (No DM Audio)|Session 13]]: [[Kyle Bloodfin]] and [[Esper Grimrose]] had wrecked the island hunting the Lunarfolds, until the [[Spider Nest Pirates|Spider's Nest]] drove them off. [[Graff Bolt]] and [[Melina Celeste]] arrive; Graff knocks the crew out with Conqueror's Haki, then has them repair the damage. The "Lord" was a bit the staff played up for visitors. [[Bugor]] (a bear, Tigor's older brother) runs the island and sends [[Cade Tigor Cooper|Tigor]] with the crew to collect animals for a restart. Someone on the island finds the crew's old ship.
