# Risky Choice

Tavern in [[Yorktown]] owned by [[Whisky Guiseppi]], with the Giuseppe family's meeting room upstairs. The crew stays here during the Juniper Islands arc.

## Episode 26

- Mira brings Baptiste and Jack here to meet [[Rum Guiseppi]] and his siblings ([[Session 026 — Daliance of Hearts|Episode 26]]).

## Episode 27

- A poker game ends in chaos when Mira drags Baptiste out; Whisky tallies the chips at 600,000 berries for Roma, pours Jack a whiskey and lets Roma cook in her kitchen on credit. [[Zim]] turns up upstairs disguised as "Sato", Rum's secretary ([[Session 027 — Testing Tempers|Episode 27]]).
