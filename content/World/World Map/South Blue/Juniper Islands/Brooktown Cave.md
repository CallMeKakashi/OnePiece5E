# Brooktown Cave

Cave at the edge of [[Brooktown]], reached by rowboat from [[Yorktown]]. Narrow mouth, torches at the entrance, mining tools left behind, and no one inside. Blind cave crawlers that home in on sound live inside, along with a smarter creature covered in grafted palms with a hollow in its chest, and a stretch of green slime hanging from the ceiling.

## Episode 27

- [[Matthew -The Jack- Burgess|Jack]] kills about nine of the crawlers; [[Thompson Caneheart]] is dragged off by the palm-covered creature, hung in slime and freed. Thompson collapses part of the cave to get out ([[Session 027 — Testing Tempers|Episode 27]]).
