# Yorktown

Island in the [[Juniper Islands]] and the crew's base. The [[Risky Choice]] tavern, where the [[Guiseppi Family]] meets, stands here, and so do the docks and a training ground on the outskirts.

## Episode 26

- Mira takes Baptiste and Jack to the Risky Choice to meet the Giuseppes, and trains the party on the outskirts ([[Session 026 — Daliance of Hearts|Episode 26]]).

## Episode 27

- Thompson hands out jobs at the Risky Choice; Jack and Thompson row out from the docks, and the party comes back to the tavern afterwards ([[Session 027 — Testing Tempers|Episode 27]]).
