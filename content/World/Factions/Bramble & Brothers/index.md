# Bramble & Brothers

Shipwright operation and dockyard on [[Spirit Cliff]]. The crew first makes contact here at the start of [[Episode 03 - Cliffside Gambit]], where [[Bramble]] offers a Grand Line–capable ship in exchange for a job against the [[World Government Bank]].

## Role on Spirit Cliff

- **Dockyard / shipwright hub** for repairs, refits, and ship procurement.
- **Bramble’s cover** for recruiting “contract work” that intersects with Marine/WG scrutiny in-town.
