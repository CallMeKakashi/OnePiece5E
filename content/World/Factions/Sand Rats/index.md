# Sand Rats

Liberation army. A covert network that frees slaves smuggled out of Alabasta, led by [[Khael Dhamar]].

## Episode 20

- Khael's raiders hit the chili plantations on [[Freefield]], which hide slaves behind a spice trade. They clash with the crew at Rashid's plantation, then fight the Sharkfins alongside it, and take the freed captives away ([[Session 020 — Price of Freedom Part 2|Part 2]]).
