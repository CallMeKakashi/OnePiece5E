# Judge Valehart

Judge of [[Lex Imperia]] who lives on [[Noosehold]]. Wears a judge's headpiece, a white moustache and a blue and silver garb. First name unknown. Father of [[Cassian Valehart]]. He is scheduled to hear [[Zim]]'s case alone, tomorrow.

## Episode 21

- Hires [[Roma]] (posing as a chef under an alias) after tasting his food, and refuses a feast until the executions are done. Holds the Marines' execution of [[Droven Calligos|Droven]] without due process in contempt, but looks forward to Zim's case ([[Session 021 — House of Justice|Episode 21]]).
