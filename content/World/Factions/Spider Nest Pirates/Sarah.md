## Description

**Two-Toed Swift Sarah** — scout/hunter of the [[Spider Nest Pirates]].

## Role

Scout / Hunter.

## D&D 5e stat block

**Medium humanoid (longleg), chaotic neutral**  
**Armor Class:** 17 (leather armor + agility)  
**Hit Points:** 110 (16d8+32)  
**Speed:** 50 ft.

**STR 14 (+2), DEX 20 (+5), CON 14 (+2), INT 10 (+0), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 12 (+1)**

**Skills:** Acrobatics +10, Perception +7, Stealth +10  
**Challenge:** 9 (5,000 XP)

**Unmatched Speed.** Sarah can Dash as a bonus action every turn.  
**Deadly Kicks.** Her unarmed strikes deal 1d10+5 bludgeoning damage.

**Actions**  
**Multiattack.** Sarah makes three kick attacks.  
**Two-Toed Kick.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 10 (1d10+5) bludgeoning damage.  
**Lightning Dash (Recharge 5–6).** Sarah moves up to her speed without provoking opportunity attacks and makes one kick attack against each creature she passes within 5 ft.
