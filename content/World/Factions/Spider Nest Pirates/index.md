## Overview

The Spider Nest Pirates suffered heavy losses — most of the original officers are dead ("six feet under" per Daniel). Silas, the old Tyrant, was killed by "our boss" [[Simon The One Armed Tyrant|Simon]], who took the title. The remaining crew regrouped under him. On [[Callisto]], Daniel, Ben and Chloe ambushed Sixfold and the party ([[Session 005.5 — Callisto]]), then allied with them to destroy [[Hallow]]'s facility and gave them passage north on their ship ([[Episode 06 - Hallow's End]]).

After the ravine collapse on the Juniper Islands, [[Matthew -The Jack- Burgess|Jack]] told the [[Guiseppi Family|Guiseppis]] about the Spider Nest Pirates' involvement in the ravine ([[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Capone Invasion — Time Skip|Capone Invasion — Time Skip]]).
