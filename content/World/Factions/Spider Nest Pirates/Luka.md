## Description

**Long-Arm Reaper Luka** — strategist of the [[Spider Nest Pirates]].

## Role

Strategist.

## D&D 5e stat block

**Medium humanoid (longarm), lawful neutral**  
**Armor Class:** 16 (breastplate)  
**Hit Points:** 150 (20d8+60)  
**Speed:** 35 ft.

**STR 18 (+4), DEX 16 (+3), CON 16 (+3), INT 14 (+2), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 12 (+1)**

**Skills:** Acrobatics +8, Stealth +8, Insight +7  
**Challenge:** 11 (7,200 XP)

**Extended Reach.** Luka’s melee reach is 15 ft.  
**Calculated Strike.** Once per turn, Luka deals an additional 14 (4d6) damage if he has advantage on an attack.

**Actions**  
**Multiattack.** Luka makes three Scything Arm attacks.  
**Scything Arm.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8+4) slashing damage.  
**Reaper’s Sweep (Recharge 5–6).** Luka makes a sweeping strike with both arms. All creatures within 15 ft. must succeed on a DC 16 Dex save or take 22 (5d8) slashing damage.
