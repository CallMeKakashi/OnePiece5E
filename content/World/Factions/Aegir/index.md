# Aegir

Antagonist force — **Control** and **Dominian** forms.

## Members

- [[Aegir (Control)]]
- [[Aegir (Dominian)]]

## Episode 16

- Aegir offers to save Baptiste from [[Coda]] in exchange for being taken to his vessel; Baptiste refuses and makes his flames himself. A third ring of Aegir fuses onto Baptiste in the Emperor's control room ([[Session 016 — Broken Promises|Episode 16]]).

## Episode 17

- A burst of power in the tower lets Aegir take Baptiste's body while Baptiste fights him in an inner dominion. Aegir copies Baptiste's flames, fights with a shadow blade and a void body that reforms, and says Baptiste "gave him the gift of freedom". Baptiste beats him; Malphas's electric punches end the physical fight. Aegir is contained in the three rings "for now" and promises to return ([[Session 017 — Agony of Choas|Episode 17]]).

## Episode 24

- Baptiste dreams of Aegir's defeat: five figures stand in the smoke over crumbled cities. A man in golden-sapphire armour holds a sword set with a stone of swirling golden dust, with a woman with a closed third eye beside him, a white-haired, red-eyed man with black wings, a lion-maned mink and a fishwoman. The man says "this is the end for you, Aegir" ([[Session 024 — The Friendly Baron|Episode 24]]).
