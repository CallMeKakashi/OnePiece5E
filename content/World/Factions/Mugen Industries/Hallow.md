## Description

Human lineman/station master with a hammer and no devil fruit. Daniel first calls him cruel, but his journal shows a man who tried and failed to stop the Doctor's core. Transcript speech-to-text: "Hello".

## Role

- **[[Callisto]]**: **station master** (quartermaster) of the Mugen Industries facility on the island, part of **[[Dr Nikolai Tesla]]**'s ("the Doctor") amalgam program. Controls the chimeras and holds the **remote** (red-button failsafe) for the Core, the "Divine Furnace".
- **Status: dead.** Killed by his own amalgam [[RyeNera]] in [[Episode 06 - Hallow's End]].
- Field operator for Nikolai's chimera and fusion work; distinct from the named amalgams **[[RyeNera]]** and **[[Monster Manual/Aberrant Abomination|Aberrant Abomination]]**.

## Personal Quests

## Backstory

- Came to Callisto with "the Doctor" ([[Dr Nikolai Tesla]]) hoping to change things for the better. Once he understood what the core did he tried to stop it, but stayed "a cog in the system" (his journal, 1477; see [[Timeline/Dates]]).
- His best friends were a married couple, **Rye and Nera**. Nikolai fused them into [[RyeNera]]. Hallow told the Spiders they had died.
- To avoid feeding living people to the machine, he paid gravediggers for corpses and remains (office receipts).

## Session 005.5

- Lures the party and Sixfold into a trap with a promise of a clue about Vireth. Tries to crush them with trash compactors, then sets chimeras on them in the Core ([[Session 005.5 — Callisto]]).

## Episode 6

- Waits in the graveyard and says he always meant to destroy the place. Releases RyeNera ("Near… you remember me?"), which crushes his head after Baptiste's black hole knocks him prone. His last words tell the party to destroy the machine.
- Leaves behind his journal, a burnt photo of a young Hallow with Rye and Nera, his office key, ฿200,000 and the remote. Roma uses the remote to sink Callisto.
