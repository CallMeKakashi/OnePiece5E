## Description

**"Skyblade" Vireth** — **Page 1** of the [[Sixfold]].

## Role

Sixfold leader tier (Page 1). Assault on Marine Base G-55 with [[Liz]] (Guillotine Liz) per  — bounties restored and raised.

## The fusion

Vireth is not one person. [[Ronan]] found a fusion fruit in the North Blue about four years ago and used it to fuse with [[Astor]]. After an explosion they could no longer separate, and after [[Aralia]] died their memories broke: Vireth has mixed memories and doesn't know he is two people. The [[Sixfold]] play along; [[Linus Marrow|Linus]] has worked on it for years. [[Zim]] seeks a Marine device that permanently removes a devil fruit's power, which could undo it ([[Session 009 — The Lunarfold Tournament Part 2|Episode 9]]).
