# Julie

## Description

A girl about 4'3" who calls herself the mascot of the [[Braveheart Pirates]]. Rides and commands Crunch, the armadillo-porcupine sea beast.

## Role

Treats everyone as a pet. A pat on the head forces a Wisdom save and compels obedience; she does it to animals and tried it on [[Roma]], who failed. She kicks [[Tray]] across a room for pulling her hand away. [[Thompson Caneheart]] warns newcomers to be careful of her. The source of the power (fruit or otherwise) is not named.

## Episode 26

- Jumps out of Crunch's tongue in the waterfall cave and asks [[Baptiste]] for a present, then food for Crunch. Gives Roma a toy kaiju after patting his head. Stays behind with Thompson when the crew goes to train ([[Session 026 — Daliance of Hearts|Episode 26]]).
