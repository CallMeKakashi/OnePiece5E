# Sharkfin Pirates

A small crew that raided [[Freefield]] and killed Mayor [[Rashid al-Saffar]]. Its captain died in [[Session 020 — Price of Freedom Part 2|Episode 20]].

## Episode 20

- Joe Mitch's crew, including a teleporting fighter and a pistol user, kills Rashid and loots his house, then fights the crew and [[Khael Dhamar]]. Mitch and the teleporter die, the pistol user is knocked out, and [[Eric]] is sent away ([[Session 020 — Price of Freedom Part 2|Part 2]]).
