- Boarding Combat: Crossing Ships: gangplank 10 ft (metal 30 ft), set as an action; chain-shot bridge = difficult terrain; Swinging needs 50-ft rope anchored overhead, spend half movement, Athletics/Acrobatics DC 10, fail = no distance, success = 20 ft + 5 ft per 5 over DC; Pulling Ships: helm uses action, ship rolls d20 on its turn, 10 = 10 ft closer, advantage if puller larger, smaller pulls itself, +10 ft per extra chain, Str pull if no mechanism. - Creations and Objects: unworn/uncarried objects and structures are valid targets for all damaging creations/tricks (still immune to poison/psychic).
