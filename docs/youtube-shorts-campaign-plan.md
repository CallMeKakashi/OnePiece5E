## Campaign strategy (overview)

**Positioning:** One Piece D&D actual-play — devil fruits, Marine bounties, Den Den Mushi, naval combat, Lunarfolds crew chaos.

**Edit formula:**
1. **0:00–0:03** — Hook overlay + loudest/funniest line
2. **0:03–0:45** — One beat; cut rules talk unless it's the joke
3. **End** — Full episode CTA + subscribe
