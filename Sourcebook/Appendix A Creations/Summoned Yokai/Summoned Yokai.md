## Summoned Yokai

Medium monstrosity
Armor Class
12 + the level of the creation (natural
armor)
Hit Points
40 + 15 for each level of the creation
above 3rd
Speed
40ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
14 (+2) 16 (+3) 16 (+3) 4 (-3) 10 (+0) 16 (+3)
Damage Immunities
necrotic, force
Condition Immunities
frightened
Senses
darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 10
Languages
understands the languages you speak
Terror Frenzy (Fury only).
If the Summoned Yokai
hits a creature that is frightened by it with an
attack, that attack is a critical hit.
Weight of Sorrow (Despair only).
Any creature, other
than you, that starts its turn within 5 feet of the
spirit has its speed reduced by 20 until the start of
that creature’s next turn.
Shadow Stealth (Fear only).
While in dim light or
darkness, the Summoned Yokai becomes invisibile
until the end of its next turn, or until it makes an
attack.
