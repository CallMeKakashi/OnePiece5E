# Grand Line Tribune — 22nd Moon, 1478 (Evening)

_"Truth Rides the Waves" — Price: 3,000 Beli_

## COMMODORE BRIGGS’ DAUGHTER KIDNAPPED — 1 BILLION BERRIES OFFERED FOR HER SAFE RETURN

> Notorious “Plague” of the Sixfold Mercenary Company Named as Suspect — Commodore Calls Upon the World Government to Spare No Resources

A shocking kidnapping has sent ripples throughout the Four Seas.

The daughter of **Commodore Briggs** has been confirmed missing, with authorities identifying the notorious mercenary known as **Cline “The Plague”**, a member of the feared **Sixfolds**, as the primary perpetrator.

In response, Commodore Briggs has issued an urgent plea to the **World Government**, calling upon its forces to spare no resources in locating his daughter and bringing her home unharmed.

The Commodore has additionally announced a staggering reward.

**1 BILLION BERRIES.**

The sum will be awarded to whoever successfully returns his daughter to him **alive and unharmed**.

Marine and World Government forces are expected to increase their efforts across the seas as the search continues.

Citizens are urged to report any information regarding Cline “The Plague” or the missing girl to the nearest Marine authority.

The Tribune will continue following this developing story as further information becomes available.

## THE BRONZE DRAGON AWAKENS

> Former Admiral Candidate Cole Heathcliff Returns From Years-Long Coma

In what many are calling one of the most remarkable recoveries in recent Marine history, **Cole Heathcliff**, former Admiral Candidate and bearer of the codename **Seiryu — “The Bronze Dragon,”** has regained consciousness.

Heathcliff had remained in a long-term coma since the conclusion of the **Unity War**, leaving many to believe his days of active service had come to an end.

Those assumptions have now been proven incorrect.

Sources within the Marines report that Heathcliff has already returned to **active duty**, apparently wasting little time before involving himself in the increasingly volatile affairs surrounding the recent Mugen Industries controversy.

The former Admiral Candidate has reportedly been handling questions surrounding the growing backlash against **Mugen Industries**, following the company's official dismissal by Marine authorities and subsequent order to cease operations.

More information regarding the Mugen Industries situation can be found on **Page 12** of this issue.

---

## FROSTHAVEN DECLARES INDEPENDENCE

> Island Freed From Marine Governance Following Case Presented by Chief Eira Snowheart and Lord Cassian Valeheart

The island of **Frosthaven** has officially declared itself an independent territory, ending its governance under Marine authority.

The decision follows a highly publicized case presented by current Chief **Eira Snowheart**, alongside her attorney, **Lord Cassian Valeheart** of the Valehart Family.

According to sources familiar with the proceedings, the pair presented extensive arguments concerning alleged abuses committed by what were described as misguided Marine officials operating within the territory.

Following the conclusion of the proceedings, Marine governance over Frosthaven has been withdrawn.

Chief Snowheart will remain the island's acting authority as Frosthaven begins its transition toward independent governance.

The decision marks another significant change in Marine influence across the Four Seas.

---

## CAPTAIN CALDER VOSS STILL MISSING

> Marine Captain Disappears Weeks After Experimental Treatment

The whereabouts of **Captain Calder Voss** remain unknown.

Voss was among the Marines rescued following the disastrous confrontation with the **Lunarfold Pirates**, an operation that came at a heavy cost to Marine personnel.

Following his rescue, the Captain underwent an experimental treatment conducted by **Mugen Industries engineers**, intended to restore both his mental and physical capabilities after the severe injuries he sustained.

The treatment was reportedly successful.

However, several weeks later, Captain Voss was reported missing.

No confirmed sightings of the Marine officer have been reported since.

Marine authorities have yet to provide an explanation regarding his disappearance.

The circumstances surrounding Captain Voss' whereabouts remain under investigation.

---

## JUNIPER ISLANDS DESCENDS INTO CHAOS

> Spider Nest Sightings Reported as Tusk Treaty Collapses — West Town Destroyed

The recent collapse of the treaty between the **Tusk Pirates** and the **Spider Nest Pirates** has been followed by renewed sightings of Spider Nest members throughout the region.

The situation escalated dramatically during the incident on **Juniper Islands**, where civilians and Marines alike reported encounters with members of the Spider Nest Pirates.

The island was subsequently engulfed in violence as the conflict between the **Five Families of the West** and the **Guissepi Family of the South** erupted into open warfare.

Marine forces were dispatched to the island with orders to rescue civilians while apprehending as many members of the criminal organizations involved as possible.

However, considerable uncertainty remains surrounding the destruction of **West Town**.

Sources familiar with the incident remain unable to establish exactly what occurred in the city before its destruction.

The **Braveheart Pirates** and **Lunarfold Pirates** have both been identified as the primary suspected culprits behind the incident.

The Tribune will continue to investigate the events surrounding Juniper Islands as more information becomes available.

## THE BRONZE DRAGON SPEAKS

> Heathcliff Issues Warning Following Juniper Island Incident

Following his return to active duty, the Tribune was granted a brief interview with former Admiral Candidate **Cole Heathcliff**.

The reporter in question has insisted that the following statement be reproduced **exactly as spoken**.

> “Who are you... what is that—oh, a Transponder Snail? They have really grown small over the years, huh!
> 
> Interview? Since when can you runts walk up to us and ask questions... Very well...
> 
> The storm that raged through the city was the doing of that good-for-nothing child Mira... Who gives a shit how old she is...
> 
> Well, that's not all. I sensed a familiar feeling when fighting this Obsidian fellow. I saw his posters... Do you know who he is? No? Oh well, maybe Master Horus knows...
> 
> Wait, I'm not done yet.
> 
> You wanna interview me, then you better keep all of this in... otherwise your newspaper would be nothing but dust...
> 
> Although the destruction of the city can be blamed on the Mafia and Mira and her underlings... the sphere I saw hovering above was the reason Obsidian was here.
> 
> Here is my message to them...
> 
> You've given me the taste for a glorious battle after so long... I am twitching to fight him again.
> 
> The second I even hear you breathe in the Four Seas, I will come and hunt you down.
> 
> Whoever you are—the man who commands Obsidian.
> 
> That's all. You can go now.”

These were reportedly the former Admiral Candidate's final words to the Tribune before he proceeded to take a paddle boat from a Marine vessel and departed **alone into the sea**.

His current whereabouts remain unknown.

## TUSK PIRATES’ BOUNTIES RESTORED

> Spider Nest Remains Unlisted Under New Leadership — Updated Bounties Included With This Issue

Marine Headquarters has officially restored the bounties placed upon members of the **Tusk Pirates** following recent developments surrounding the criminal organization.

Despite the recent incidents involving the **Spider Nest Pirates**, Marine authorities have stated that the crew, now operating under new leadership, is **not currently considered a threat**.

As a result, their previous bounties have not been restored.

Updated bounty posters for the **Tusk Pirates** are included with this issue of the _Grand Line Tribune_.

Due to the Tribune's recent financial difficulties following the growing popularity of **Video Transponder News**, however, readers will now be required to pay an additional **10,000 Berries** to receive the bounty collection.

The Tribune thanks its readers for their continued support during these difficult times.

## SAND RATS AND DECIBELLA REBELS FORM ALLIANCE

> Coalition Publicly Denounces World Government and Marines

A new alliance has emerged between the **Sand Rats** and the **Decibella Rebels**, with both groups issuing a joint announcement openly denouncing the **World Government** and the **Marines**.

The statement reportedly contained numerous threats directed toward the established powers of the Four Seas, including threats against the Noble Families.

Due to the graphic nature of the declaration and its repeated calls for violence, the _Grand Line Tribune_ has elected not to reproduce the full message.

World Government officials have yet to issue a detailed response regarding the newly formed alliance.

The emergence of another organized opposition movement has nevertheless raised concerns among authorities already struggling to contain the growing number of conflicts across the Four Seas.

## PAGE 12 — MUGEN INDUSTRIES ORDERED TO CEASE ALL OPERATIONS

> Vice Admiral Horus and Commander Leon Raise Objections Following Growing Controversy

Marine Headquarters has officially ordered **Mugen Industries** to cease all operations following a growing series of rumors and objections surrounding the organization.

The decision reportedly follows concerns raised by **Vice Admiral Horus** and **Commander Leon**, who has since recovered from the injuries sustained during the recent Marine convoy incident.

Mugen Industries has been instructed to suspend its operations pending further developments.

The company has previously worked alongside Marine forces on a number of experimental and technological projects, making the sudden order a significant development.

The Tribune will continue reporting on the situation as Marine Headquarters determines what action will be taken next.
