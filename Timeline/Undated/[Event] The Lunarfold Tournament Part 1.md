# The Lunarfold Tournament (Part 1)

Shipboard **crew-ranking tournament** after [[Callisto]] (Episode 8). [[Sixfold]] operatives [[Veyl Corven]] and [[Serica Corven]] treated as full crew; captain rank fixed — bracket determines pecking order below captain. Rules: **no killing**; otherwise unrestricted (including ally interference).

## Beats

1. **Downtime (~3 days)** — recovery, devil-fruit talk, [[Baptiste]] speed training with Veyl.
2. **Tournament called** — deck fights; non-lethal only.
3. **Results** — [[Veyl Corven]] beats [[B.O.B]]; [[Serica Corven]] beats [[Roma]] on captain [[Baptiste]]'s ruling; [[Malphas]] beats Serica. Serica guaranteed **3rd**; Malphas vs Veyl (1st/2nd) and Bob vs Roma (last) in Part 2 ([[Episode 09 - The Lunarfold Tournament Part 2]]).
