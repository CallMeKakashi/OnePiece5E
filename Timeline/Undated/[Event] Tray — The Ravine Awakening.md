# Tray — The Ravine Awakening

Downtime vignette (downtime-actions, 2026-06-19 14:39 UTC). After a ravine collapse traps his crew, [[Tray]] is hunted by Sandvipers; knocked out, he awakens — white eyes, molten-red fists — kills both vipers, then a troop of Titan Apes. Origin of his epithet **"Tray the Wiper,"** which he hates — his bounty poster now reads "Tray the Viper."

## Text

The rumbling of the ravine quiets down as the Sandvipers finally hunt the prey left behind by the Titan Apes...

They spot one of the men still moving, trying to pull the rest out of the rubble. A weak-willed man, they can tell, as they have always been able to smell the fear of their prey.

One of the Sandvipers approaches. The movement of the snake makes the man flinch and look around with a frightened expression on his face, desperately trying to find a way to get his brethren out. The vipers feel safe approaching.


---

The man notices them and tries to run away, but he can't. The rubble has closed off any exit out of the ravine.

One of the vipers moves quickly and swats the man away with its tail. The man goes crashing through a softer rock on the other side. He gets up and sees the light.

The other viper hisses at the first, angry that they might have let one of the prey escape. No matter. They had made more, they thought.

They watch the man drag himself away, crying out in pain.

The vipers approach one of the men. It has a rotting scent and seems unconscious. Badly injured. They assume it is dead, but nourishment is a survival instinct.

The first viper wraps around the body as it prepares to pick it up, but soon a halberd comes flying into its torso. For now, the strength behind the throw isn't enough to pierce the thick hide.

To their awe, the man did not leave as they expected. Instead, he came back to protect his tribe.

Making large gestures and screaming:

"HEY! LEAVE THEM ALONE! I'M STILL STANDING! I WOULDN'T BE ABLE TO SHOW CAPTAIN MY FACE IF I LET MORE OF OUR CREW DIE!"

The other snake slithers from behind the first and rushes the man.

The man barely dodges the viper's attempt to swallow him whole, but as he tries to run away, he gets struck by a whip of the tail.

He goes flying back and hits his head on a collapsed protruding rock by the side of the ravine.

His eyes go white as the man passes out.

The first snake proceeds to continue devouring its prey.

Then, a sudden chill runs up its spine.

It notices a trail of blood, gushing like a fountain from the other snake.

On its back stands the same man.

But he doesn't look like weak prey anymore.

A predator.

---

The man's hair tie has come loose, his hair flowing in the wind rushing from outside. His eyes are white. Blood pumps through the veins at his temples. His fists glow with a molten reddish hue.

Before the viper can react, the man decapitates the other snake and punches a hole through the collapsed ravine.

Now, bright light shines onto the snake.

It tries to make the first move and devour the beast standing before it.

The speed of the creature's movement surprises even the viper.

Almost as if slithering through the sky, he takes its face and blows another hole through the viper's skull.

Its body collapses to the ground.

Titan Apes are mostly found alone.

But in truth, they are more similar to apes than bears. They prefer to stay with a troop of others of their kind.

Unfortunately, this would be the end of the troop.

As more and more Titan Apes drop down from the already weakened ceiling, they notice the man in the middle, breathing heavily.

He lets out a roar that shakes the earth...

30 Minutes Later...

The sea behind the ravine is stained red.

More blood than water.

In the vastness of the ocean it wouldn't make a difference, but today it looks like pristine wine being mixed into water.

Blood drips through the walls of this edge of the cavern, which now has more holes than rock.

And the only awake creature present is Tray the Wiper.

Note: Tray has been bummed about this name because it makes him sound like he wipes other people's butts. He has repeatedly bothered Marine Command to change it to "Viper" instead, even posing as different people from different branches of the Marines to submit requests on his behalf.
