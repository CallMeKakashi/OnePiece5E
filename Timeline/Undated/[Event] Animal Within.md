# Animal Within

The Broadcast Tower **1v1 gauntlet** begins: the PCs fight the [[Soundless 5]] in separate rooms (Episode 15).

## Beats

1. **Synth** — [[Cade Tigor Cooper]] and [[Riff Sin]] knock out his cousin [[Synth]], who injects a "6/6" serum and turns into a feral llama-beast. [[Dravos]] heals Cade through a PVC pipe, and Synth dies to a second rubber shot.
2. **Bass** — [[Malphas]] fights [[Bass Tremor]], who asks about his brother [[Baron Woofer]]. Malphas is hammered down.
3. **Coda** — [[Baptiste]] fights [[Coda]], the leader, who imitates techniques he has seen (Horus's void, Blackhand's black blade). Baptiste deals about 84 damage and is stabbed through the heart.
4. **Cliffhanger** — the Malphas and Baptiste rescues are deferred; [[Roma]] and [[B.O.B]]'s fights are teased.
