# The Reaper (No DM Audio)

**No DM audio** — Scorpion Unit attack, Sixfold leave, Marine standoff at [[W.G.A.R.]] (Episode 13). Beats below are from the DM's account.

## Beats

1. **Scorpion Unit** — [[Kyle Bloodfin]] and [[Esper Grimrose]] of the [[Shadow Guild]] attack the crew's ship on the way to W.G.A.R.; Kyle is killed, Esper captured.
2. **Sixfold leave** — [[Veyl Corven]] and [[Serica Corven]] take Esper and the Scorpion Unit's ship through the Calm Belt after the [[Vireth]] news.
3. **Marines** — [[Graff Bolt]] and [[Melina Celeste]] want the clown and crab; the party refuses slavery and Graff knocks the crew out with Conqueror's Haki.
4. **W.G.A.R.** — crew wakes in the settlement, repairs the island, hears the Unity War story, recruits [[Cade Tigor Cooper|Tigor]] as helmsman.
