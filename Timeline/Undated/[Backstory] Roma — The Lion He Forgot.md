# Roma — The Lion He Forgot

About a year before the present, a wandering Roma meets the Lion Mink [[Pasha]] — the isolated child he barely remembers from Zou — in Wano. They share a fire, fight a giant armored lizard in abandoned ruins and become real friends; on their last night Pasha uses his glowing markings to erase himself from Roma's memory before heading back to Zou, leaving only a pouch of Zou spices. Story by player THE OLD MAN.

---

Roma and the Lion He Forgot

About a year before the present day, during Roma's travels before becoming established with the **Blackhand Pirates**, the 34-year-old Wolf Mink found himself wandering through **Wano**.

Roma had been traveling alone for nearly two weeks. His food was running low, his clothes were worn, and his oversized iron skillet had collected several new dents along the journey.

One evening, while searching for somewhere to camp, Roma noticed smoke rising beyond the trees.

He followed it.

Sitting beside a small fire was a massive **Lion Mink**.

He looked young—much younger than Roma—but his size suggested otherwise. His golden-brown fur was covered in scars, and strange **white symbols** stretched across his arms, chest, shoulders, and other parts of his body.

Roma stopped.

Something about those markings bothered him.

Not because they frightened him.

Because he'd seen them before.

The Lion looked up.

For a few seconds, neither said anything.

Roma finally pointed at him.

“Wait a damn minute...”

The Lion raised an eyebrow.

Roma stared at the white markings.

Then an old memory surfaced.

**Zou.**

Monks.

And a very young Lion Mink who was almost always kept away from everyone else.

“You're that kid.”

The Lion's expression changed.

“You remember me?”

“Barely.”

Roma scratched behind his ear.

“You were tiny.”

The Lion looked down at his massive frame.

“Things change.”

“No shit.”

For the first time, the Lion smiled.

“Pasha.”

Roma nodded.

“Roma.”

“I remember.”

---

## Two Minks Far From Home

Roma joined Pasha beside the fire.

The conversation was awkward at first.

They weren't old friends.

They were practically strangers who happened to share the same homeland.

Roma remembered seeing Pasha on Zou, but because the monks had kept the young Lion isolated, they'd only encountered each other briefly.

Eventually Roma asked the obvious question.

“What happened to you after Zou?”

Pasha stared into the fire.

“I left.”

“To go where?”

“Anywhere the monks weren't.”

Pasha explained that after leaving Zou, things hadn't exactly gone according to plan.

For a time, **humans captured him**.

He didn't give Roma every detail.

Roma could tell there were parts Pasha wasn't ready to talk about.

But Pasha eventually escaped.

After that, he traveled.

He fought.

He survived.

And somewhere along the way, he found something he hadn't expected.

**Friends.**

“Good people?” Roma asked.

Pasha smiled slightly.

“Some.”

“That's not convincing.”

“It wasn't supposed to be.”

Pasha told him pieces of what had happened with the **Sixfolds**, eventually mentioning the incident at **Virrellis** and how he'd separated from them afterward.

Roma expected Pasha to say he was searching for another adventure.

Instead, Pasha looked toward the night sky.

“I think I'm going home.”

Roma stopped eating.

“Zou?”

Pasha nodded.

“A lot happened. I made some good friends. Saw things I never thought I'd see.”

He looked down at the white symbols covering his arms.

“But I think it's time.”

Roma understood that feeling better than he wanted to admit.

---

## Their First Adventure

The next morning, Pasha mentioned hearing about abandoned ruins nearby.

Supposedly, beneath them was an old storehouse containing preserved food and supplies.

Roma immediately stood.

“Why the hell didn't you start with that?”

Pasha blinked.

“With what?”

“The food.”

For the first time, Pasha laughed.

Roma pointed his skillet at him.

“Don't judge me.”

The two headed toward the ruins.

What should have been a simple trip quickly became anything but.

They crossed a half-collapsed bridge, fought off enormous wild boars, got chased through a bamboo forest, and spent nearly twenty minutes arguing because Roma insisted he knew where they were going.

He didn't.

Somewhere along the way, though, something changed.

The awkwardness disappeared.

Roma started cracking jokes.

Pasha started firing them back.

By afternoon, they were insulting each other like they'd been traveling together for years.

Then they reached the ruins.

The ground exploded.

A gigantic armored lizard emerged from beneath the earth.

Roma immediately charged.

Pasha stared after him.

“You always do this?”

“DO WHAT?!”

“Attack things without thinking!”

Roma swung his skillet.

**CLANG!**

The skillet bounced off the creature's armor.

Roma went flying.

Pasha caught him.

Roma groaned.

“Okay.”

Pasha put him down.

“Okay what?”

“Maybe I should've thought.”

The creature charged again.

Pasha stepped forward.

The **white symbols covering his body began to glow**.

Not a different color.

They became an intensely bright, almost supernatural **white**, like moonlight burning through his fur.

Roma's eyes widened.

“Well, you definitely couldn't do THAT when you were younger.”

Pasha grinned.

“Long story.”

He launched himself forward.

The fight became their first real test together.

They shouldn't have worked well as a team.

They barely knew each other.

Yet somehow, they did.

Roma distracted the creature while Pasha attacked its sides. Pasha created openings for Roma. Roma began recognizing Pasha's movements without needing instructions.

Finally, Pasha flipped the creature onto its back.

Roma climbed onto a broken rooftop.

He raised his skillet.

“HEY!”

The creature looked up.

Roma jumped.

**CLANG!**

The skillet slammed into the creature's exposed underside.

The beast collapsed.

Roma landed beside it.

He looked down at his skillet.

It was bent almost completely in half.

Silence.

“My pan...”

Pasha started laughing.

Roma slowly looked at him.

“You think this is funny?”

Pasha laughed harder.

Roma tried staying angry.

He couldn't.

Soon both Minks were sitting in the middle of the ruined village laughing like idiots.

That was the moment their friendship truly began.

---

## The Last Night in Wano

They found the storehouse.

That evening Roma used what remained inside to cook them a proper meal.

They stayed beside the fire long after they'd finished eating.

This time, their conversation wasn't awkward.

They talked about traveling.

About Zou.

About mistakes they'd made.

About people they'd lost and people they'd met.

Eventually Roma looked at Pasha.

“You sure about going back?”

“To Zou?”

“Yeah.”

Pasha nodded.

“I've spent enough time running from where I came from.”

Roma looked into the fire.

“Funny.”

“What?”

“I spent years trying to get away from there.”

Pasha smiled.

“And I'm trying to get back.”

Roma chuckled.

“Guess we're both idiots.”

“Probably.”

Eventually Roma lay back beside the fire.

“Maybe we'll run into each other again.”

Pasha looked at him.

“Maybe.”

Roma closed his eyes.

“Next time I'm cooking something better.”

“Please do.”

“Asshole.”

Within minutes, Roma was asleep.

Pasha remained awake.

For a long time, he simply watched the fire.

He'd finally gotten to know Roma.

Not as the older Wolf Mink he'd briefly encountered when he was a child.

As a friend.

And that made what came next harder.

Pasha quietly moved beside him.

The **white symbols covering his body began glowing brighter**.

One after another, the markings illuminated until brilliant white light covered him.

Pasha placed two fingers against Roma's forehead.

“Sorry, Roma.”

The light intensified.

Pasha didn't erase Wano.

He didn't erase their adventure.

He didn't erase the armored beast, their arguments, Roma's destroyed skillet, or the night they'd spent talking beside the fire.

He erased one thing.

**Pasha.**

His name disappeared.

Their childhood encounter disappeared.

The connection to Zou disappeared.

Their conversation about his past became fragmented memories without a clear face attached to them.

Roma would remember traveling through Wano with a Lion Mink.

But he would no longer remember **who that Lion was**.

Pasha stood.

He looked at Roma one final time.

“Maybe someday you'll remember.”

Then Pasha turned away.

His road would take him back toward **Zou**.

Roma's would eventually lead him toward the **Blackhand Pirates**.

---

## The Next Morning

Roma woke beside a dead fire.

“Ugh...”

His head hurt.

He sat up and immediately noticed his destroyed skillet.

Then memories of the previous day returned.

The ruins.

The giant lizard.

A Lion Mink.

Roma stopped.

“...Who the hell was that guy?”

But the name wouldn't come.

Neither would the face.

Stranger still, Roma felt like he'd known him.

Not just from yesterday.

From somewhere much further back.

Roma tried forcing the memory.

Nothing.

Then he noticed something beside his belongings.

A small pouch.

Roma opened it.

Inside were **spices from Zou**.

Roma stared at them for a long time.

“Okay...that's weird.”

He looked toward the road.

The Lion was already gone.

Roma eventually packed his belongings, strapped his damaged skillet to his back, and continued his journey.

He didn't know that the mysterious Lion was named **Pasha**.

He didn't remember encountering Pasha as a child.

And he didn't remember that, for one short adventure in Wano, the two Minks had finally become genuine friends.

But Pasha remembered.

And as Pasha began his journey back toward Zou, he carried something Roma no longer could:

**The memory of their first real friendship.**
