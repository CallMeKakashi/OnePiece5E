# Babylon

> **Secret.** The party does not know this story. It surfaces in [[Session 014 — The Decibel Decree|Session 14]] when [[Lady Soefra Anthem]]'s singing voice brings back Baptiste's memory of the mermaid. Source: DM backstory.

[[Baptiste]], undercover as **Haruki**, spends four years inside the Gambino Family (a slave-trading mafia) on [[Blackhand Cane]]'s orders. He befriends a caged mermaid used as a siren-singer in their gambling hall, and her song fails to charm him. She tells him about the cages, the drugs, and a child she freed. When he finally frees her, the Gambino siblings spring a trap. She sings a deadly song that collapses the estate and walks into the fire, giving Baptiste a small heirloom ("if you find one of my kind… tell them"). The papers name it **BABYLON**; Haruki becomes a "terrorist" and she "The Wailing Siren." Blackhand finds him days later, and he burns the Haruki alias. From the ashes he becomes **Masquerade**.

## Beats

1. **Invitation.** Baptiste hears the name Gambino whispered, watches a boy dragged from a home, and becomes Haruki. Blackhand: "you're walking into the belly of a Sea King."
2. **Four years.** A gambling hall; the mermaid's song; Haruki resists and signals "I'm awake." He asks only for "time with the singer."
3. **Babylon.** The cage opens too easily; the Gambino siblings reveal the trap; Lunarian fire; her song; the estate burns.
4. **Aftermath.** Blackhand: "You survived. That's different." Haruki is burned; **Masquerade** is born.
