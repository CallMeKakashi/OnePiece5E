# Interlude — Queen Anne's Revenge and Frosthaven

Downtime write-up the DM posted in #downtime-actions on 2025-10-15 ("Downtime actions from party 3"), between [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Hallow's End|Episode 6]] and [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] The Lunarfold Tournament Part 1|Episode 8]]. The text is verbatim.

## **The Captain's Quarters — Queen Anne’s Revenge (North Blue)**

The lantern swayed gently, casting shadows that crawled across the Captain’s quarters. The room smelled of salt, steel, and weathered maps strewn across a massive oak table. At its far end sat Blackhand Cane, the legend himself—a towering figure with a presence that filled every inch of the space. His scarred face was set in stone, his broad shoulders hunched forward slightly as he rubbed absently at the base of his hooked arm, where metal met flesh. Every touch to that old wound seemed like a ritual of pain.

Opposite him, Renzo sat with unnerving calm, fingers drumming lightly against the edge of his glass.

The door creaked. Bob entered, hesitant. This was the third time he had ever stood before Blackhand Cane, and it was enough to make the air feel heavy in his lungs.

Blackhand’s steel-gray eyes lifted, fixing Bob in place with the weight of command. His gravelly voice rumbled like distant thunder:“You barely leave your room.”

The words cut through the silence before the captain leaned back, gaze never softening.“Suppose the progress on those tests is going good… huh? Keep at it. We’ll need them.”

He gestured toward the map sprawled across the table.“We’re in the North Blue now. You probably noticed when we crossed Reverse Mountain last week.” His jaw tightened. “But that’s not why you’re here.”

He slammed a calloused hand on the map, finger stabbing at a marked point.“Goro’s missing. The Second Fleet commander doubt you've met him before.”
“That boy’s reckless as hell. His entire fleet vanished up north. Last known location—here.” His finger pressed into the parchment until it crinkled. “The scouts I sent haven’t returned either. First Linus calls out of nowhere, asking favors… then the Kirro mess, and now this.”

His hand gripped the edge of the table as he muttered, almost to himself,“Every time this damned arm aches, something bad happens.”

Renzo gave a dry chuckle.“That’s not like you, Captain. You never cared for my superstitious crap.”

For a heartbeat, the corners of Blackhand’s mouth curved in a humorless grin.“Maybe you’re rubbing off on me.”

Then his attention sharpened on Bob like the edge of a blade.“I want you on this. Go to the last island Goro was sighted near. Take the Onis with you.”

His eyes flicked to Renzo.“You don’t mind, do you?”

Renzo raised his glass slightly.“Not at all.”

“And take Malphas,” Blackhand added, his voice dropping like an anchor. “Keep him cloaked. Those wings stay hidden.”

Renzo inclined his head in acknowledgment.

Blackhand’s tone darkened, heavy with unspoken weight:“Hope Saplea’s holding her ground. Fenris is probably out of Driftroot by now.”

Before another word could be spoken, the world outside changed. A frantic cry tore through the ship, boots thundered overhead. Then:“Ghost ship! Dead ahead!”

The three men stormed onto the deck.

And there it was—a hulking corpse of a vessel, cloaked in mist and silence, its hull crawling with barnacles, sails tattered and black. It moved as if alive, yet no soul stood aboard. The air thickened; the sea turned violent.

From nowhere, a storm was born. The sky split with lightning. Waves rose like mountains, slamming against the Queen Anne’s Revenge. To port, Renzo’s ship—the Crimson Wraith—lurched violently, its mast ripped apart by the gale.

In the chaos, figures were flung into the abyss:

A woman—screamed as she was hurled into the freezing dark.

A tall figure—without hesitation—dove in after her, vanishing beneath the waves.

The cloaked figure with black wings flashing like a shadowed omen soared skyward, but a bolt of lightning struck, and he disappeared into the storm.

Renzo gripped the railing, roaring over the tempest:“What the hell is happening?!”

Blackhand’s gaze locked on the ghostly ship, his face twisting with fury.“Renzo! It’s them! Those bastards! How could they let this happen?! Makes sense Goro just vanished!”

Then he seized Bob by the collar with terrifying force, hauling him close as the storm screamed around them.“Find them! Find any survivors! Get to the island I marked!. After you learn what happened there.. you find Vireth. Tell him it's happening again. He'll know what that means.”

Renzo turned sharply, shouting over the thunder:“But you said Vireth’s in the Grand Line! Linus swore he was there!”

Blackhand’s eyes burned like a forge.“Then they go to the grand line and find him. He's the only person that can help them after we are gone.”

With that, Blackhand hurled Bob over the railing. The world became cold and endless as the sea swallowed him whole.

Above, lightning struck as the ghost ship collided with the Queen Anne’s Revenge, and the legendary flagship vanished in fire and mist.

Darkness.

When consciousness returned, there was only snow, jagged rocks, and the ragged edge of breath. Bob lay sprawled on a desolate, frozen island, the taste of salt burning his throat. Around him was the one Large figure trying to resuscitate a woman lying next to him, battered and unmoving. Another large man black-feathered shape—the cloaked one—was curled in the snow, broken wings like torn banners.

And then—faint, but growing—a ship’s horn bellowed through the fog. A silhouette loomed on the horizon.

## **Frosthaven (North Blue)**

_Snow whips across the base of the mountain at Frosthaven. Lightning cracks overhead, painting the blood-soaked ground in silver light. Droven crouches low over the splattered remains of Commander Svarn, his jaw tight._

**Droven (thinking):** _“Did they really throw him off? What the hell were they thinking…?”_

_The hairs on the back of his neck rise — someone’s behind him. Instinct takes over. He fires a glob of goop into the storm just as another flash of lightning tears open the sky._

_A tall figure is revealed in the light. Red eyes. Black horns. Calm. Familiar._

**Droven (lowering his guard, grinning):** “Buddy, sneakin’ up on people ain’t nice.”

**Kanto (eyes narrowing):** “And firing on friends isn’t either.”

**Droven (surprised, relieved):** “Oi… is that Kanto? Man, it’s been what, two years? Damn, time flies. Where’s Fafnir? Still bitey?”

**Kanto (smirking slightly):** “Sleeping. Wake him now, he’ll bite me, not you.”

**Droven (tilting his head):** “So… why you here? Chasing after a bounty?”

**Kanto (pulling out a few papers, handing them over):** “Yeah… The Shadows sent me. Got contracts for these two. The girl is a murderous maniac, and the guy is not any better. Caused a lot of problems for us. I took the job because it was my kind… well, at least half.”

_The bounty posters of Dorian Dusk and Saphira Dusk flap in the wind. Droven’s face hardens._

**Droven:** “…Shit. We gotta move. I know where they are.”

**Kanto (backing a step):** “Oh no. Not this again—”

**Droven (grinning, already shifting):** “Hold still.”

_In a blur, shadows engulf Kanto as Droven rockets up the mountainside, snow and ice tearing past them. At the peak, Kanto is spat unceremoniously into the snow, coughing._

**Kanto (wiping himself off, disgusted):** “Never fuckin’ do that again. It’s gross.”

**Droven (shushing him, suddenly serious):** “Quiet. I knew something was wrong about these two. I heard Renzo recruited some Onis — but this is not Renzo’s style. Let me talk first.”

_Kanto ignores him, stepping out of the shadows with his bone gun raised._

**Kanto:** “You two. Dorian Dusk. Saphira Dusk. You’re coming with me.”

**Droven (shaking his head):** “I lost to you one time. And this was before we joined Blackhand. I’m not the same guy I was before. I’ve met some real monsters in this crew.”

_He looks back down toward the bloodied snow at the base of the mountain._

**Droven (grim, voice low, speaking to Bob and Malphas):** “I’m guessing they did that to the marine commander. And the marine cadet… tongue ripped out. You really think Renzo would recruit people like that?”

_The wind howls. The snowstorm thickens._

**Droven (looking at the others, jaw tight):** “These two were never Blackhand’s. We were played.”

_The statue looms motionless once more, the stolen ring fused into its arm. Kanto spins and points his bone gun with a snap._

**Kanto:** “Whatever the truth is, I’m collecting this one. And we’re not done here.”

_The storm howls louder. Fade to black._
