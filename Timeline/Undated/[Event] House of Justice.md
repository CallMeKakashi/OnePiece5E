# House of Justice

[[Noosehold]] and [[Lex Imperia]], the execution and judiciary twin islands. Roma infiltrates [[Judge Valehart]]'s household, Baptiste burns Marines in the cathedral, Roma's jailbreak starts a riot, and [[Linus Marrow]] pulls Roma out as Baptiste and Malphas fight Marine armors and [[Calder Voss]] (Episode 21).

## Beats

1. **Transit**: Freefield → [[Dravos]]'s Thousand-Mile Tunnel; a mishap drops the ship at [[Noosehold]].
2. **Infiltration**: Roma as a chef for Judge Valehart; wanted posters; two radio calls with Voss.
3. **Cathedral and jailbreak**: Baptiste kills two Marines; Roma frees the cells (~600 real criminals among ~1,900); Marines fire into the crowd.
4. **Cliffhanger**: a white G-45 armor against Baptiste, Voss against Malphas, Linus pulls Roma through the floor.
