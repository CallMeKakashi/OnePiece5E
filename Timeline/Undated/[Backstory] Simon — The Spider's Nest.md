# Simon — The Spider's Nest

The origin of the [[Spider Nest Pirates]] told as a fable of nine little spiders: the oldest leaves to grow strong after giants crush the owl he looked up to; the second oldest holds the others together through years of forced labor; when the oldest finally returns, the caretaker dies and every sibling is maimed, and the family takes to the sea vowing no one will tear it apart again. Told by [[Simon The One Armed Tyrant|Simon]] — the second oldest, who is left with one arm (sibling order per world-lore, 2026-07-26: "Second: Simon"). [[Sessions/Ganimede]] prep has Simon telling Part 1 of his story during the elevator descent. The opening of the tale was also posted to world-lore and is filed, with the sibling-order table, as [[Timeline/Undated/[Backstory] Spider Nest — The Nine Little Spiders|The Nine Little Spiders]]; this note is the full telling.

---

There once was a spider's nest tucked beneath the roots of a great old tree.

It was not the biggest nest, nor the prettiest, but to the little spiders who lived there, it was home.

An old caretaker watched over them. Every morning she mended torn strands, every evening she tucked the smallest spiders into silk blankets, and every night she reminded them,

"A web is not strong because of one strand. It is strong because every strand holds another."

There were nine little spiders.

The oldest was fierce. He climbed the highest branches, fought the hardest battles, and always placed himself between danger and his brothers and sisters.

The second oldest was frightened of almost everything. Storms made him shake. Strange noises made him hide. Yet whenever one of the younger spiders was scared, he wrapped himself around them until they felt safe.

The third was a brave little sister whose courage was greater than her size.

Another spider was clever and calm, always solving problems before anyone else noticed them.

One shone brighter than sunshine itself, making everyone smile even on the darkest mornings.

One was larger than all the others, yet cried whenever another creature was hurt.

One loved colorful petals and shiny things, weaving beauty into every corner of the nest.

One rarely spoke, choosing instead to watch the world with quiet determination.

The youngest never stopped climbing. Every branch was an adventure, every breeze a race.

The nest was small.

Their meals were simple.

But every strand held another.

And that was enough.

---

Then one day...

The ground began to tremble.

Great boots marched into the forest.

The giants demanded more food from the woodland creatures than they could possibly give.

The oldest brother climbed down from the nest.

He stood before the giants.

He knew he could not win.

But someone had to stand.

An old owl the oldest brother looked upto rushed after him, begging him to stop.

The giants answered with laughter.

And before anyone could move...

One careless step crushed the old owl.

Silence spread through the forest.

The oldest brother stared at the broken web...

...and the owl who had always perched on that branch.

He blamed only himself.

"If I had been stronger..."

"I would have saved him."

That night, without waking anyone, he climbed to the highest branch.

He left behind only a promise.

"I'll become strong enough that no giant can ever threaten our home again."

Then he disappeared beyond the trees.

---

Morning came.

The eight remaining spiders searched every branch.

Every leaf.

Every corner of the forest.

But their oldest brother was gone.

The second oldest looked at the empty place in the web.

He was terrified.

He wanted to cry.

He wanted someone older to tell him what to do.

But there wasn't anyone left.

So he climbed into the place the oldest had always stood.

He became the strand holding the rest together.

Every night, when the younger spiders asked,

"When is he coming back?"

The second oldest always smiled.

"Soon."

Even when he wasn't sure.

---

The giants returned.

This time...

They did not destroy the web.

They took it.

The eight little spiders were torn away from their forest.

Loaded onto a great wooden ship.

Taken somewhere no sunlight reached.

There they were forced to spin silk from sunrise until long after sunset.

If they slowed down...

They were beaten.

If they cried...

They were mocked.

If they collapsed...

They were dragged back to their feet.

Even the youngest had to work.

Some nights they were so hungry they dreamed of eating leaves.

Some nights they were too tired to dream at all.

Through every terrible day, the second oldest held them together.

He divided the scraps of food.

He comforted nightmares.

He carried the youngest when they couldn't walk.

Whenever hope began to disappear, he would look toward the sea.

"He promised."

"Our brother will come."

And because he believed...

The others believed too.

---

Years passed.

Then one morning...

A torn piece of paper drifted into the factory.

It showed a familiar face.

Their oldest brother.

Smiling.

Laughing.

Wanted by the world.

Another poster came months later.

Then another.

Each one showed him stronger.

More famous.

More alive than ever.

The younger spiders smiled.

The second oldest smiled too.

But only until nobody was looking.

Then he quietly folded each poster away.

Because every new smile on those pages asked a question he never wanted to think about.

"If he's become so strong..."

"...why hasn't he come?"

Still...

He never stopped believing.

---

Then came the day the island itself began to die.

The ruler of that terrible place lost his mind.

Fire consumed everything.

Buildings collapsed.

The prison became a furnace.

At last...

Their oldest brother returned.

---



He fought with all the strength he had spent years searching for.

But strength had arrived too late.

The caretaker climbed forward to protect the others.

She smiled at them one last time.

Then the fire swallowed her.

The explosion that followed tore everything apart.

When the smoke finally settled...

No spider looked the same.

The second oldest had only one arm left.

The strong little sister had lost both arms...

...and both her eyes.

The quiet thinker could no longer hear the wind...

...and one of his legs was gone.

The cheerful one lost an arm and a leg.

The gentle giant lost one arm forever.

The beautiful spider would never walk again.

The silent watcher lost both legs.

The youngest's tiny body was broken in so many places that every breath hurt.

The oldest looked upon them.

Each scar...

Each missing limb...

Each broken body...

And understood something he could never forget.

He had become strong.

Just not soon enough.

The forest called them monsters now.

The giants called them criminals.

No one called them children anymore.

So they gathered what remained of their web.

They spun a new home upon the sea.

And the oldest spider made one final promise.

"No one will ever tear apart my family again."

If strength was what the world respected...

Then he would become stronger than the world itself.

And so the Spider's Nest sailed into the horizon.

But weaving a web so vast...

...that no one would ever again mistake kindness for weakness.
