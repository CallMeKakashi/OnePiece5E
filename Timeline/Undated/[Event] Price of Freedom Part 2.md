# Price of Freedom (Part 2)

The Sharkfin Pirates kill Rashid al-Saffar on [[Freefield]] and the crew and [[Khael Dhamar]] fight them (Episode 20 part 2).

## Beats

1. **The Sharkfins** — [[Joe -Mitchblade- Mitch|Joe Mitch]]'s crew kills [[Rashid al-Saffar]] and loots his house.
2. **Combat** — Baptiste's void arms, Malphas's Divine Smite, Bob's smoke; Mitch and the teleporter die.
3. **Aftermath** — [[Eric]] is sent away; the loot pays for the repairs; the Sand Rats take the captives; no bounty, no level-up.
