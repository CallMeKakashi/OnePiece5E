# Interlude — Red Departs and Reverse Mountain

Downtime write-up the DM posted in #downtime-actions on 2025-09-13, between [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Shackles Broken|Episode 5]] and the unrecorded [[Timeline/Undated/[Event] Callisto — Hallow's Trap|Callisto session]]. The text is verbatim.

## **Spirit Cliff (East Blue)**

The morning fog clung to the jagged edges of Spirit Cliff as the battered crew gathered outside the abandoned warehouse. Red staggered forward, blood trailing from a cut along his temple, though his eyes burned clear for the first time in weeks.

“Sorry to worry you guys,” he rasped, steadying himself against the wall. “I had an encounter with my past. I remember everything now… when Blackhand fished me from the water, I’d lost my memories. But Jack here—” he gestured to the pigeon-eyed man at his side, “helped me remember. Rough as always.”

Jack S. Parrow adjusted his feathered coat with a sharp grin. Unlike Red, he bore wings that were no gift of birth—his came from the **Pigeon-Pigeon Fruit**. Red, meanwhile, admitted the truth: he was no Mink, but a Zoan user of the **Parrot-Parrot Fruit**.

Red bowed his head to Baptiste. “I want to ask the captain’s leave. I’ve unfinished business with my old crew. If I stay, I’ll put us all at risk.”

Baptiste clenched his jaw, torn between loyalty and reason. But he saw it in Red’s eyes—this was something that couldn’t be fought alongside them. “Then go,” Baptiste finally said, voice low. “We’ll hold the line until you return.”

When they rejoined at the Armada, they learned Saplea’s condition was stabilizing, though frail. Linn, grave and quiet, revealed he once knew her father. “There are answers back on her home island. I have the contacts to get her there,” he said.

Turning to Veyl and Serica, he added, “Go with Baptiste and Roma. Find Vireth. The time has come to start going after the list.”

The path forward split, like the sea before them—two directions, one purpose. 

Veyl gripped the helm as the crew neared Reverse Mountain, the roar of water rising all around them. Without warning, the sails collapsed, ropes and canvas spilling uselessly as the ship slowed, dragged toward the rocks.

Serica cursed, storming forward. “Not now!” She smacked the mast in frustration.

To everyone’s shock, the sails snapped wide open, filling as if caught by an unseen wind. The ship surged forward, racing up the raging current.

They clung on as the vessel crested the summit and plunged down the far side—slamming into calm waters of the Grand Line, completely unharmed.

Breathless, Veyl looked to Serica. “What was that?”

Serica only smirked. “Whatever it was… it worked.”

The crew breathes a sigh of relief... as the log pose starts pointing to there next destination.

## **Seas near ShatterBark (North Blue)**

Elsewhere, the sea still boiled from the thrashing of a slain Sea King. The battered ship of bounty hunter **Kanto** limped through the waves, its hull cracked and sails torn. Droven, Malphas, and Bob had survived, but barely.

Mira’s voice crackled through a den den mushi: _“There’s a crew stranded in the East Blue. They need rendezvous on the Grand Line. Meet them there.”_

But Kanto’s ship would never make it.

That night, under starlight, Droven dissolved into his plasmid form, slipping into Kanto’s quarters. Acid burned through side of the ship until the chest with the **Ring** was free. He pressed it into Malphas’s palm. “Keep it safe. Get to Mira. I’ll draw the Navy’s eyes north. They won’t guard Reverse Mountain if they’re busy chasing me.”

Before dawn, Malphas and Bob hid themselves aboard a Germa merchant vessel bound for the Grand Line. Their destination: **Harpoon Cay**, where they were to rendezvous with a name Malphas recognized—**Baptiste**.

As the sails carried them forward, Droven’s silhouette melted into the blizzard of the North.
