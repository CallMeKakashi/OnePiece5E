
## **Drathmir**

The lands that make up Drathmir are vast and filled with wonder. The regions are filled with their own ways and races. From places high in the sky to far below the depths, lush groves filled with a variety of wildlife and green, to lands where the borders between the planes tore and influences from other worlds were able to enter the realm. All regions coexist with each other, trading resources.

The draconic deities that made the world the way it is are not forgotten. Their bodies might have passed, but their spirit lives on within an avatar that governs their own land and tasks. They live on within them and watch how their land evolves.

#   
Kingdoms of Durnovar

  
Made up of two kingdoms. Ouranos, the one that climbs high beyond the clouds, and Thalassor, the one that reaches into great depths. They live in harmony with each other. The people, for the most part, get along well. The kingdoms are the places that house the most variety of races. All who want can live on the inclining lands and sail the seas at will.  
  
Ouranos’ queen, Caelire Virelle, was chosen by the sky dragon Zephyra. And Thalassor’s King Pelagorath swims the seas with the spirit of the sea god Kelthuron.

# Verdelith

The lush trees of Verdilth spread far, but at the center of it all is the tree, Laufrast, that houses Melaenia Primrose, the Leafmother, and the avatar of Faeralith. All ecosystems are welcome under her branch, and she wants to make them all strive to their fullest. You may call the musk of the swamps home, or live within the rustling leaves, for her, it doesn’t matter

# Shatterfell

When Xalavirethra and Aelkrondrys were creating the other gods, their control over the planar borders weakened. Rifts opened, causing other worlds to seep through. Five major realms spread the fastest and changed the land the most. Since those that spread the most were too different from each other. The gods, still in their weaker state, decided that it would be better to section the planes off and see how they use their parts. Over the land forms a dark dome that makes it hard for things to just wander off the island. Within it, the districts are separated by a bright light, originating from the mountainous forge, the Ignarok, within. Nyxalune Onerienth, chosen of Selaneth, maintains the dome, while Coale Emberborn mans the forge in the name of Solynthar.

Distinct realms:

Clockhollow: Where flesh was deemed inefficient and improved by artificial means.

Umbryss: Where horrors lurk within the shadows.

Briarwisp: Where the mischievous have a never-ending party.

Colossara: Where titans walk to become the strongest.

Stellarae: Where you sail the stars, instead of the waves

# Welkinmire

If you see an island surrounded by multiple smaller ones floating along in the sky, you just saw the grand university of Welkinmire. Mages from around the regions enrol to learn to harness the weave or to learn to control the power they were given at birth. The surrounding islands specialize in their own school of magic with their own shops and classes. At the same time, the center island has the largest library of the realm. The headmaster Threxion Varn’Thal is rumoured to be either the first avatar of Aelkrondrys or even be the god himself.
